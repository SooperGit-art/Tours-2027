## Quick answer

**Yes** — Leon Bridges tours through most of 2027 on The Happiness Anytime Tour. The run starts in Australia (February 25 – March 7), then crosses North America from April 7 in Las Vegas to September 29 in Phoenix, with 37 confirmed headline dates including Madison Square Garden, Red Rocks and Seattle's Climate Pledge Arena.

**October 2026 check:** both legs are announced and on sale or in presale now. Festival slots at Lollapalooza South America and Festival Estéreo Picnic in March are part of the routing but are festival appearances, not headline dates listed below.

## The tour so far, for context

Happiness Anytime is Bridges' fifth album and his most groove-focused: made with Jungle's J Lloyd-Watson and Lydia Kitto, it blends the retro-soul warmth of his 2015 debut Coming Home with the psychedelic lean of his Khruangbin collaborations. The live show has been rebuilt around that — a DJ, horn section and three backing vocalists, drawing on communal dancefloor energy.

The routing reflects his arena-level draw now: a hometown-adjacent Dickies Arena show in Fort Worth, a Madison Square Garden headline date on May 1, and a first Australian run since January 2025 that sold well enough to add second Sydney, Fremantle and Melbourne shows. The North American announcement on October 8, 2026 is exactly what pushed "leon bridges tour 2027" breakout in Google Trends.

## Ticket and presale info

Australian tickets are on sale through Frontier Touring and the venue outlets. For North America, sign-ups for the artist presale (October 13, 10AM local) run through Bridges' official channels, followed by venue and local presales on October 14 and the general on-sale on Friday, October 16 at 10AM local time. $1 from every headline ticket goes to The Big Good, the Texas-focused charity Bridges co-founded with Gary Patterson.
