## The tour so far, for context

Donny Osmond is one of pop's great survivors: teen idol in the 1970s with the Osmonds ("One Bad Apple," "Love Me for a Reason"), solo heartthrob with "Puppy Love," Broadway and TV star, and since 2021 a Las Vegas headliner with his "Donny" residency at Harrah's. His last studio album, *Start Again*, arrived in 2021, and his Vegas show — a career-spanning production with the auto-rap-ography and a Vegas-style finale medley — has been a consistent sellout.

Britain has always been Donny's second home: his UK tours regularly fill arenas, and the VIVA UK routing reflects that — a mix of big rooms (OVO Hydro, Co-op Live, Utilita Arenas) and prestige theatres (Eventim Apollo, Connexin Live). The set will lean on the residency blueprint: hits across six decades, theatrical medleys, and the high-energy production that has kept his live reputation strong.
