## Quick answer

**Yes** — Fuerza Regida open their 2027 with a 10-night stand at Palacio de los Deportes in Mexico City, from January 28 to February 7, 2027. The shows are listed on Ticketmaster Mexico, with presales already running on some dates and several flagged as low availability.

**October 2026 check:** the Mexico City stand is the band's only confirmed 2027 routing so far. No US or other international 2027 dates have been announced, and no separate 2027 tour name has been given — the run follows straight on from the This Is Our Dream Tour.

## The tour so far, for context

Fuerza Regida, the San Bernardino-born música mexicana group led by Jesús Ortiz Paz, have had a landmark run: their album 111XPANTIA debuted at No. 2 on the Billboard 200 and No. 1 on Top Latin Albums, earning them their first Grammy nomination. The This Is Our Dream Tour — billed as the first-ever US stadium tour by a regional Mexican group — carries them through Charlotte, Atlanta, Red Rocks and more in late 2026 before the Mexico City residency opens 2027.

A 10-night stand at Palacio de los Deportes is a statement booking: few regional Mexican acts play arena residencies of that length in the capital. It also explains the current Google Trends breakout for the band's 2027 dates.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets are handled through Ticketmaster Mexico. The official listing shows presales already under way for some dates, with low-availability flags on January 28, 31, February 1, 3, 4, 5 and 7. Because availability is moving, check the Ticketmaster MX event pages directly rather than resale sites, and note the 8:00 PM start for most shows (7:00 PM on the two Sundays, January 31 and February 7).
