## Quick answer

**Yes** — 30 Seconds to Mars officially announced the A Beautiful Lie vs. This Is War Tour for spring 2027: 17 confirmed dates across Europe, the UK and Ireland, running April 6 through May 1, 2027, from Lisbon's MEO Arena to Dublin's 3Arena. The tour celebrates their 2005 and 2009 albums with the Leto brothers joined by former member Tomo Milicevic. **October 2026 check:** all 17 dates stand as announced; no North American or additional legs have been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

30 Seconds to Mars released their most recent album, It's the End of the World but It's a Beautiful Day, in 2023, and marked the 20th anniversary of A Beautiful Lie with an expanded reissue in March 2026 featuring four previously unreleased session tracks. Rather than a standard album cycle, the band is leaning into its catalog: both A Beautiful Lie and This Is War produced some of the biggest rock radio hits of the 2000s, from The Kill and From Yesterday to Kings and Queens and Closer to the Edge.

The format also reunites the band with former guitarist Tomo Milicevic, giving longtime fans a reason to see a set built around two complete classic albums. The run is a pure arena itinerary — 17 cities in under a month — which is typical of the band's efficient European tours, and ticket resale sites list all 17 shows as on sale.

No viral fake posters or fabricated date lists have circulated for this tour in the way they have for other 2027 tours — the official announcement was clear and widely reported, so the rule here is simple: if a date is not in the band's announced list, it is not real.
