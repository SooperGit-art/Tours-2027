## Quick answer

**Yes** — Stick Figure's Enjoy the Ride Tour is confirmed for summer 2027: 18 amphitheater dates from June 3 (Phoenix) to July 17 (the Gorge in George, Washington), produced by Live Nation. The reggae outfit announced the run a full year early so fans could plan. **October 2026 check:** all 18 dates are confirmed and tickets are on sale via Ticketmaster; some third-party marketplaces also list a June 13 Bethel Woods date that does not appear on the official routing, so treat that one as unconfirmed.

## From a solo project to a touring phenomenon

Stick Figure began as the solo recording project of singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist Scott Woodruff and grew into one of the most reliable draws in American touring — a full band built on immersive live shows, a dub-infused reggae sound and a deeply loyal fanbase (plus Cocoa the Tour Dog, the band's loyal canine companion on the road). Chart-topping albums World on Fire, Wisdom and Set in Stone cemented the catalog, and the 2019 single "Angels Above Me" found a second life when a stolen AI-derived version went viral on TikTok, pulling a new generation into the fold.

The Enjoy the Ride Tour is explicitly billed as their biggest tour yet — an all-amphitheater summer run that leans into what the band does best: long outdoor evenings. Announcing a full year out was a deliberate move, giving fans flexibility to plan travel around the routing, and the band has tied the run to new music arriving before the first downbeat.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets have been on sale since June 2026: presales started in early June ahead of the general on-sale on June 5, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time. Ticketmaster is the primary seller for the Live Nation-produced dates; the Red Rocks show is presented by AEG. Amphitheater lawn seats for this tour have historically been the value play, with pavilion seats carrying the premium — prices on resale markets currently range from the mid-$40s to nearly $200 for Red Rocks.
