## Quick answer

**Yes** — Conor Oberst is touring North America solo in 2027. An Alleged Evening with Conor Oberst is a 49-date run announced November 10, 2026, opening February 19 at Liberty Hall in Lawrence, Kansas, and closing with two nights at the Stanley Hotel in Estes Park, Colorado, on May 14 and 15. It is his first solo headlining tour since 2019. **November 2026 check:** general on-sale opened November 13, 2026, so tickets are on sale now via Ticketmaster and conoroberst.com.

## The tour so far, for context

Conor Oberst is best known as the frontman of Bright Eyes, the Omaha band he has led since the 1990s, and he has also released a long run of solo records alongside side projects including Desaparecidos and Monsters of Folk. His last solo headlining tour was in 2019, so the 2027 run is a return to the road under his own name after seven years — and it is deliberately not a standard arena or theatre circuit. The routing favours unusual rooms: an underground venue in The Caverns, a converted Benedictine monastery chapel in Tucson, the Henry Miller Memorial Library in Big Sur, and two closing nights at the Stanley Hotel, the Colorado hotel that inspired The Shining.

The announcement drove "conor oberst tour 2027" up 250% on Google Trends. Bright Eyes are also active around the run, playing three nights at the Brooklyn Paramount in New York in January 2027, performing the albums I'm Wide Awake, It's Morning and Digital Ash in a Digital Urn in full. Oberst has also stepped behind the camera recently as a short-film director, but the 2027 dates are firmly a music story: extended evenings built around his decade-spanning catalog.
