## The tour so far, for context

The ARIRANG World Tour marks BTS's first extended run of shows since 2022, following the group's return from mandatory military service. It kicked off with three nights in Goyang, South Korea in April 2026, then moved through North America (28 shows across 12 cities, including first-ever K-pop headline shows at stadiums like El Paso's Sun Bowl and Foxborough's Gillette Stadium) and a full European/UK run. Demand across that first wave was described by organizers as unprecedented — all 41 North America/Europe/UK dates sold out, totaling nearly 2.4 million tickets, with extra dates added in Tampa, Stanford, Las Vegas, and Latin America to meet demand.

The 2027 Asia-Pacific leg you see above is a direct continuation of that same tour, not a separate announcement.

## Stage design

This tour uses a 360-degree, in-the-round stage setup, placing the audience around the performance rather than facing a single front stage — a deliberate choice to expand stadium capacity while keeping the show immersive from every angle. That's consistent across every leg, including the 2027 shows.
