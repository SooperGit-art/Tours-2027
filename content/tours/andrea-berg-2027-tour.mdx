## Quick answer

**Yes** — Andrea Berg is touring in 2027. Her "Andrea Berg – Live 2027" arena tour is officially announced and on sale now: 24 arena dates from 2 October to 21 November 2027, covering 20 cities in Germany plus Zurich, Vienna, Graz, Linz and Salzburg. It follows her sold-out 2025 "Party, Hits, Emotionen" tour and is promoted as her biggest stage production to date.

**October 2026 check:** All 24 arena dates and venues are confirmed by promoter Semmel Concerts. Support acts and setlist details are still unannounced.

## Why a 2027 arena tour makes sense

Andrea Berg remains one of the most commercially successful German-language artists of all time: over 16 million records sold, 14 number-one albums in Germany since her 1992 debut "Du bist frei", and a Guinness World Records entry for her 2001 best-of spending 352 weeks on the German charts. Her latest self-titled studio album, released in October 2024, debuted at number one in Germany, Austria and Switzerland.

Her 2025 "Party, Hits, Emotionen" tour was a major success — more than 20 sold-out arena shows across Germany, Austria and Switzerland, with press and fans praising the emotional intensity, modern staging and multiple costume changes. She said the connection with her audience on that tour moved her deeply, and she immediately wanted to build on it. The 2027 run is framed explicitly as a bigger, brand-new production: new stage concept, impressive visuals, live band, dancers and costume changes, blending intimate moments with large-scale show images.

## Ticket and presale info

The general presale launched on 15 October 2025 and tickets are currently on sale through eventim.de (Germany), oeticket.com (Austria) and ticketcorner.ch (Switzerland), plus the usual box offices. German shows are priced from about €55.40; the Zurich Hallenstadion show lists seats from CHF 69.90 up to CHF 199.90. Given that her 2025 arenas sold out, fans of the big-city stops — Berlin, Cologne, Hamburg and Munich — should not wait long. Her remaining summer 2026 open-air shows (including Aspach) sell through the same outlets.
