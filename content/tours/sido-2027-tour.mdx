## Quick answer

**Yes** — Sido's FRIEDEN TOUR 2027 is officially announced: 15 arena shows in 14 cities across Germany, Austria and Switzerland, running September 19 through October 8, 2027. The tour supports his tenth studio album "FRIEDEN" (out November 27, 2026), and tickets went on sale September 18, 2026 via Eventim. **October 2026 check:** all 15 dates are locked in, including a second Berlin show added for October 8; no support act or setlist has been announced yet.

## Why the FRIEDEN TOUR makes sense

Sido is still one of the most durable names in German hip-hop, more than two decades after "Mein Block" put him on the map. His tenth album, "FRIEDEN," arrives November 27, 2026, preceded by the August 2026 single "ISSA" — and the tour framing tells the story the album tells: the mask is back, only this time "completely transparent," with past and present, Siggi and Paul, folded into one live show.

The timing is classic Sido: a full Christmas residency first. He plays 18 nights at Berlin's Columbiahalle from December 2 to December 22, 2026 for his traditional Weihnachtsshow, then returns to the big arenas in autumn 2027. It's the same arc as earlier album cycles — a new record, a sold-out Berlin residency, then the arena run.

Expect a set built on "more than 25 years of Sido": the FRIEDEN material woven through the classics that made him one of German rap's defining artists — "Bilder im Kopf," "Augen auf," "Astronaut," "Mein Block" and the rest of the catalog. Arena-scale productions have been his standard for years, and the 2027 routing through the biggest halls in the DACH region (LANXESS Arena, Olympiahalle, Hallenstadion, Wiener Stadthalle) confirms the tour is being staged at that level again.
