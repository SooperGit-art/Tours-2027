## The symphonic series, for context

RAYE — Rachel Agatha Keen, the British singer-songwriter behind the independent breakthrough My 21st Century Blues (2023) and the UK No. 1 "Escapism" — is in the middle of a career peak. Her album This Music May Contain Hope won the Mercury Music Prize on October 22, 2026, the night before a sold-out show at London's Royal Albert Hall on October 23. She spent October 2026 supporting Bruno Mars on The Romantic Tour in Vancouver, then heads to iHeartRadio Jingle Ball dates in the US in December and Spilt Milk festival in Australia.

The symphonic series is the natural next step: a full orchestral reimagining of the Mercury-winning album, staged at seven world wonders, with an orchestral mix of "Click Clack Symphony" featuring Hans Zimmer released to mark the announcement. The December 10, 2026 detail drop — dates, venues, tickets — is the one to watch: if those seven shows sell the way the Royal Albert Hall did, a full headline arena tour will likely follow.
