## Quick answer

**Yes — two dates confirmed.** Andreas Gabalier will play the Kitzbühel Music Festival on **20 and 21 August 2027** at the Kitzbühel Tennis Stadium in Austria, officially announced by Kitzbühel Tourism, which notes these are his only open-air shows there in 2027. No full 2027 tour itinerary has been announced beyond that weekend. **October 2026 check:** a wider 2027 tour is still unannounced, but the August Kitzbühel dates are locked in and on sale.

## Why a 2027 appearance makes sense

Andreas Gabalier has turned August in Kitzbühel into a fixture: he has headlined the town's summer music festival every year since 2012, and the tennis-stadium weekend routinely sells out thousands of fans, so a 2027 return was widely expected. The 2026 edition ran as the 12th Musikfestival, and the 2027 dates were announced months in advance — a sign of how central this hometown-style residency is to his touring calendar.

The announcement lands while he is in the middle of a busy period: the "10 Jahre Volks-Rock'n'Roller Unplugged" tour spans 38 dates across Germany, Austria, and Switzerland in autumn 2026 (including Vienna's Stadthalle and Lucerne's KKL), following the 2025 release of his album *Point of No Return*. After parting ways with his long-time band in September 2024, Gabalier has been reshaping his live show around fresh lineups and production, which makes the reliably packed Kitzbühel weekend a natural anchor for his 2027 calendar.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for both August 2027 evenings are already on sale — the official event page points buyers to Ö-Ticket and Ticketorganizer, with VIP tickets handled by Kitz Musik (info@kitzmusik.at). Demand is high: the organizers flagged "coveted" ticket interest when the dates were announced, consistent with the years-long sellout pattern. Because this is his only Kitzbühel open-air appearance in 2027, both nights are the prime pick for fans in Austria, southern Germany, and northern Italy.
