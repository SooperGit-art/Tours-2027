## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Maluma has been off the road since 2025, when he played three big shows at Madrid's Movistar Arena, grossing over $7.7 million. The Don Juan World Tour of 2023–24 was his last full-scale global trek, with 30 US dates including stops at Madison Square Garden and the Kia Forum. Choosing Fénix Entertainment as his partner is a serious signal: the company co-promoted Luis Miguel's 2023–24 run, the highest-grossing Latin tour on record per Billboard Boxscore, and has partnered with Shakira across multiple markets.

The timing lines up with *Loco X Volver*, his May 2026 album that returned him to his Colombian roots with vallenato, salsa, merengue, and reggaeton sounds. The album already produced two No. 1s on Billboard's Tropical Airplay chart. In recent interviews Maluma has said fatherhood changed how he approaches his career — "I'm making music because I love creating art" — framing the next tour as a new chapter rather than a victory lap.
