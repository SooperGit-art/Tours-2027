## Quick answer

**Not yet announced** — Bon Jovi have not confirmed any 2027 dates. What we do have is Jon Bon Jovi's own word: closing the Forever Tour at Wembley Stadium on September 9, 2026, he told the crowd the band is already booking a tour for next year, after a break of roughly five months.

**October 2026 check:** no official Bon Jovi Tour 2027 dates, venues or on-sales exist. Listings on some ticket aggregator sites showing 2027 dates at Madison Square Garden, Murrayfield, Croke Park and Wembley are recycling the 2026 Forever Tour routing and should not be trusted.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

The Forever Tour was Bon Jovi's comeback: their first extended touring since Jon Bon Jovi underwent major vocal cord surgery in 2022 to fix an atrophying vocal cord. He told People he is now fully recovered, and the 2026 run backed that up — a nine-night Madison Square Garden residency in July (with a guest appearance from Bruce Springsteen at the finale), then stadium dates in Edinburgh, Dublin and three nights at Wembley.

At the Wembley finale the band also revived songs not played since 2019, and Jon had already said in interviews that he hoped for a much bigger tour in 2027. A singer who was not sure he would ever tour again finishing a comeback run by announcing the next one on stage is about as strong a signal as fans get without a formal announcement.
