## Quick answer

**Yes** — Anyma has a confirmed 2027 date: he brings his ÆDEN audiovisual show to Zamna Tulum in Mexico on January 4, 2027, as the first named date of the venue's 10th anniversary season. Tickets went on general sale in September 2026 through Zamna's official channels.

**October 2026 check:** the Tulum date is confirmed by Zamna itself. No full Anyma headline tour for 2027 has been announced — his 2027 calendar so far is this single date, with more ÆDEN dates possible as the year approaches.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Anyma is the solo project of Matteo Milleri, the Italian-American producer best known as one half of Tale Of Us and a co-founder of the Afterlife label. Under the Anyma name he released the Genesys trilogy — Genesys in 2023, Genesys II in 2024, and The End of Genesys in 2025 — and built a following of more than 6.8 million monthly Spotify listeners on the strength of his melodic-techno sound and large-scale visual productions.

What pushed him beyond dance music's usual circuit was Las Vegas: Milleri became the first electronic artist booked into Sphere, opening his residency on December 27, 2024, and selling more than 130,000 tickets across eight nights. ÆDEN is the show that follows Genesys, and the Tulum date is its next confirmed staging — a natural step for a production built for distinctive venues rather than standard arena runs.

The January 4 show also anchors Zamna Tulum's 10th anniversary season, which runs from December 31, 2026, to January 10, 2027. Anyma was the first date announced for the season, ahead of Black Coffee on January 6, Adriatique on January 8, and Adam Port closing on January 10 — a sign of how central the ÆDEN booking is to the anniversary program.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for the January 4, 2027 show are sold through Zamna's official channels, with general admission starting at $110 and VIP at $350 before fees. The presale opened on September 16, 2026, and the general sale followed on September 17. The Anyma night is ticketed separately from the rest of the anniversary season, so a season Multipass does not cover it — buy the January 4 ticket specifically.
