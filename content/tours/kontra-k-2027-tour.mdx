## The Aus der Asche era, for context

Kontra K (Maximilian Diehn), Berlin's leading voice of motivational German-language rap, has toured arenas relentlessly for nearly a decade — from the Gute Nacht and Erde & Knochen tours to the Die Hoffnung klaut mir niemand run following his 2023 album, and his 2025 album Augen träumen, Herzen sehen. He closed out his recent activity with a November 2025 Lanxess Arena show in Cologne and played Warsaw's Klub Progresja in September 2026.

The 2027 tour is built around his next album, Aus der Asche, announced in late August 2026 and released March 12, 2027 — his follow-up on his own Letzte Wölfe imprint. In press for the album he framed it as art as "freedom" and "life," promising music that is "hard when it has to be hard, and full of hope when it matters."

An autumn 2027 arena run is textbook Kontra K: large-scale German arena tours with a heavy fall schedule, plus festival slots in the summer (Sputnik Spring Break in May 2027). No statements from the artist suggest anything beyond the announced 12 shows, and there are no fake posters or rumors to debunk — the official announcement came straight from kontra-k.de.
