## Quick answer

**Yes** — Gracie Abrams has confirmed The Look at My Life Tour for 2026–27, with **54 dates in 2027** across North America, Canada, Europe and the UK. The 2027 leg opens on 26 January at Seattle's Climate Pledge Arena and closes on 28 May at Barcelona's Palau Sant Jordi, built around multi-night arena stands in nearly every city. **October 2026 check:** all dates are announced and on sale; no further 2027 additions have been announced since the May 2026 announcement.

The tour announcement came in late May 2026, roughly six weeks before *Daughter From Hell* arrived — a classic album-tour bundle rollout. Ten December 2026 shows (Denver, Oakland, Glendale, Los Angeles) precede the 2027 leg, making 64 dates in total. Support acts rotate by market and include Rachel Chinouriri, Del Water Gap, Holly Humberstone, Charlotte Lawrence, Grace Ives, Bella Kay, Jensen McRae and The Japanese House.

## The tour so far, for context

Gracie Abrams arrives at this tour as one of pop's definitive new arena headliners. Her second album, *The Secret of Us* (2024), debuted at No. 2 on the Billboard 200 and topped the charts in the UK, Australia and the Netherlands, with "That's So True" becoming her first Billboard Hot 100 Top 10 hit and her first UK No. 1. She earned a Grammy nomination for Best New Artist, opened Olivia Rodrigo's Sour Tour in 2022 and Taylor Swift's Eras Tour in 2023–24, and won the 2025 American Music Award for New Artist of the Year.

*Daughter From Hell*, her third studio album, released 17 July 2026 with lead single "Hit the Wall" (May 2026), co-created with longtime collaborator Aaron Dessner. The Look at My Life Tour is her biggest headline run to date — moving from the theaters and mid-size arenas of the Secret of Us era into full multi-night arena residencies, a jump that mirrors the scale-up her peers made one album cycle earlier.
