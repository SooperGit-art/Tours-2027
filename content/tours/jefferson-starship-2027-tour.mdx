## The tour so far, for context

Jefferson Starship carries one of the longest continuous touring histories in classic rock, tracing back through Jefferson Airplane to the 1960s San Francisco scene, and the Runaway Again Tour has kept the band on the road through 2026. The November 2026 expansion shows the model still works: a mix of casino and performing-arts rooms, co-bills with fellow veterans like John Waite, and the themed music cruises that have become a reliable part of the classic-rock calendar.

The two Fort Lauderdale cruises are the most distinctive part of the 2027 schedule. The '70s Rock and Romance Cruise sails March 16-20 and the Flower Power Cruise follows March 20-27, both with the band aboard for the full sailing. Add the October 7-9 Rockin' in Paradise appearance with Styx in Miramar Beach, and the 2027 calendar stretches from January to October even before any summer dates are added.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for the 2027 land dates are listed through Ticketmaster and venue box offices, with the new November-added dates going on sale alongside the announcement. Cruise bookings go through the cruise operators rather than regular ticket sellers, and the Rockin' in Paradise festival has its own ticketing. Because the November expansion added dates after many listings were first published, the band's official website is the most reliable place to confirm the full itinerary before booking.
