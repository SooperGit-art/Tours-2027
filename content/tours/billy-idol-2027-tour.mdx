## Quick answer

**Yes** — Billy Idol is touring Australia in April 2027 on the It's A Nice Day To…Tour Again! run: six shows from April 1 to April 11, opening with two nights at Crown Live in Melbourne and closing with two Red Hot Summer Tour dates in New South Wales. It is his first Australian run since 2020, announced on November 23, 2026, and follows a world tour that sold out venues across the US, Europe and Latin America.

**November 2026 check:** the six Australian dates are confirmed and on sale this week. No US or headline European leg has been announced yet — only the Australian run and four European festival appearances.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Billy Idol has been back on the road in a big way. The It's A Nice Day To…Tour Again! run is the continuation of the world tour behind his 2025 return, which took him through sold-out rooms across the US, Europe and Latin America — and Australia, where he last played in 2020, was the obvious gap to close. The six-date April leg is deliberately compact: two Melbourne nights, then Brisbane and Sydney, then a festival-style finish on the Red Hot Summer Tour, a bill he fits naturally alongside fellow Australian rock veterans.

The timing also explains why "Billy Idol tour 2027" broke out on Google Trends the morning the Australian dates were announced. The Red Hot Summer Tour pairing gives the run a second audience: fans coming for The Living End, Noiseworks, The Angels and The Superjesus get Idol headlining the same bill.

## Ticket and presale info

Artist presale for all six Australian dates runs from 10am on Wednesday, November 25 to 10am on Friday, November 27, 2026. General sale opens Friday, November 27, 2026 at 11am. Tickets are sold through billyidol.net/tour and livenation.com.au. The two Red Hot Summer Tour dates (Hunter Valley and Berry) are sold as part of that festival's ticketing. European festival dates are sold through each festival — OverOslo, La Prima Estate and the Release Festival events in Sofia and Athens.
