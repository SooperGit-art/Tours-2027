## Quick answer

**Yes** — Stray Kids have confirmed 2027 dates as part of their RUN IT World Tour, which runs across 2026 and 2027. Confirmed so far: two nights in Bangkok, Thailand (January 16–17, 2027 at the Impact Arena) and two nights in Singapore (March 6–7, 2027 at the Singapore Indoor Stadium).

**October 2026 check:** only the Asian dates are officially on sale. No North American, European, Latin American or Oceania dates for 2027 have been announced yet, though more stops are widely expected.

## The tour so far, for context

Stray Kids closed out one of the biggest touring eras in K-pop history with their dominATE World Tour, which hit 35 cities across Asia, Australia, Latin America, North America and Europe through 2024–2025 — including US stadium dates at SoFi Stadium and Citi Field. The group have scored five consecutive No. 1 debuts on the Billboard 200, a run that puts them among the most commercially dominant acts on the planet right now.

The RUN IT World Tour is the group's new touring cycle, following their 2025 album "KARMA" and their headline slot at Governors Ball NYC in June 2026. JYP's approach with Stray Kids tours is to announce legs in waves — Asia first, then the rest of the world — so the current four confirmed 2027 dates are almost certainly just the beginning of the year's routing.
