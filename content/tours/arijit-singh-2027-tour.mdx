## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Arijit Singh rose to fame through Fame Gurukul in 2005 and has since dominated Hindi playback singing for the better part of two decades, becoming India's most-streamed artist with hits like "Tum Hi Ho," "Channa Mereya," "Phir Le Aya Dil" and more recently "Dhun" from *Saiyaara* and "Gehra Hua" from *Dhurandhar*.

The timing matters. In January 2026 he announced he would no longer take on new playback singing assignments — a move widely described as a step back from playback work, not a retirement from music. This tour is his first major live announcement since then, framing 2027 as a celebration of his 20-year journey and a direct reconnect with the audiences who gave his songs "a life of their own," in his own words from the announcement statement.

It also fits the current stadium-concert boom in India, where marquee acts are moving from arenas to full stadium presentations with large-scale production — exactly what this nine-city run promises.
