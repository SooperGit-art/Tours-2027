## Quick answer

**Yes** — Frei.Wild have confirmed 2027 dates, though they are book shows rather than a full rock tour. The South Tyrolean band's official site lists 19 "Von Heimat zu Heimat" dates: intimate hall shows from March 5 in Vahrn, Italy, to March 18 in Leipzig, then five summer open-air shows from July 10 in Gelsenkirchen to August 14 in Regensburg.

**November 2026 check:** all 19 dates are confirmed on the band's own site with tickets through their hard-ticket shop. No full arena concert tour has been announced for 2027.

## The tour so far, for context

Frei.Wild spent May 2026 on the Immer unter Feuer Tour, a 12-city arena run across Germany, Austria and Switzerland that closed at the Gasometer in Vienna on May 31. The band — from Brixen in South Tyrol, Italy — have built one of the most loyal followings in German-language rock, selling out large halls while remaining largely outside the mainstream press.

The 2027 dates are a different kind of event: "Von Heimat zu Heimat" ("From Home to Home") is the show built around the band's book, staged in smaller halls with afternoon and evening performances, including two shows a day in Nürnberg and Berlin. The summer open-airs then take the same show outdoors. It is a deliberate step down in scale from the arena tour — an intimate format for the band's core fans, not a stadium push.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for all 19 dates are sold through the band's own hard-ticket shop at frei-wild-hardtickets.com, linked from each date on frei-wild.net. The double-show days (Nürnberg March 10, Berlin March 15) are ticketed separately per show, so check the start time — afternoon or evening — when buying. The summer open-airs are also ticketed through the same shop.
