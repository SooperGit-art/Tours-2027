Her preference is for small performances for friends — or now, fans coming to her.

That "come to me" idea surfaced publicly in September 2026, when she told Zane Lowe that the two Hollywood Bowl concerts celebrating her tenth studio album, *Bass Persuades* (released September 18, 2026), were a trial run for a residency. But a residency is the opposite of a tour: it stays in one city, and none has been announced.

Contextually, she is in an album phase, not a touring phase. *Something Beautiful*, her ninth studio album, arrived in May 2025, followed by *Bass Persuades* in September 2026.
