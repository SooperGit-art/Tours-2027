## The anniversary tour, for context

My Chemical Romance — Gerard Way's New Jersey band that defined 2000s emo rock — reunited in 2019 after a six-year split, and its 2026 run has been its biggest ever: "Long Live The Black Parade" took the full-album stadium production across South America, the UK, Europe, North America and Southeast Asia. The US leg closed with five Hollywood Bowl shows (October 21–31, 2026), ending on Halloween.

The tour rides the 20th anniversary of The Black Parade (2006), a 4x-platinum landmark that debuted at number two on the Billboard 200. The deluxe anniversary edition landed October 23, 2026 with six bonus tracks, including the newly titled 2007 Hoboken recording "Someone Out There Loves You." With new archival material surfacing and stadium demand proven, a 2027 leg is far from impossible — it just hasn't been announced.
