## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

milet has quietly become one of Japan's most exportable voices. The Tokyo-born, Canada-raised singer debuted in 2019 with "inside you" (produced by Toru of ONE OK ROCK), performed at the closing ceremony of the 2020 Tokyo Olympics, and then cracked the global audience through anime: "Kizuna no Kiseki" and "Koi Kogare" with MAN WITH A MISSION for Demon Slayer: Swordsmith Village Arc, plus the Frieren: Beyond Journey's End ending themes "Anytime Anywhere" and "The Story of Us." Her low, husky voice is instantly recognizable, and her fourth album Made of Glass arrived in August 2026 with a Brit-rock-influenced sound she calls "milet 2.0."

Her first Asia tour in 2024 proved the regional demand. The 2027 run is bigger and better organized — Sony Music, Clockenflap, and NEON LIT are behind the Hong Kong stop; SOZO, the Singapore promoter behind Anime Festival Asia, runs the Singapore date. She also appears at the matsuri '27 Japanese Music Experience in Bangkok on February 13, 2027, alongside LiSA and AiNA THE END — a strong signal of how high her regional stock is.

## Ticket and presale info

Singapore general sale opened September 18, 2026, through SOZO Live and Ticketmaster SG, priced S$98–S$248 across seven categories (CAT 1 at S$248 includes a lanyard, VIP badge, and digital signed poster). As of mid-October, CAT 1 is sold out but CAT 2–5 remain available — this show will sell out, so buy early. Hong Kong tickets went on sale via Ticketflap (HKD $680–$1,280) after an HSBC Mastercard presale. No refunds or exchanges at the Singapore show; ages 3+ need a ticket.
