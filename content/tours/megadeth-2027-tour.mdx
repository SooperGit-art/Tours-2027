## Quick answer

**Yes** — Megadeth has officially confirmed a 2027 tour. It's called **"Breakout: Hibernation Of The Nations,"** the European/UK chapter of the band's multi-year global farewell run, announced August 3, 2026. It kicks off March 9 in Belfast and wraps April 13 in Lisbon, with 24 arena dates across the UK and mainland Europe.

Black Label Society and Testament are direct support on every date. No North American 2027 dates have been announced yet — this leg is Europe/UK only for now.

Confirmed VIP offerings, sold exclusively through megadeth.com:

- Individual band member meet-and-greet upgrades
- Full-band photo opportunity upgrades
- Early entry packages (available through the Cyber Army presale window)

Exact pricing and per-city availability haven't been published yet. VIP inventory for arena tours like this typically sells out fastest in the Cyber Army presale window, so if a meet-and-greet matters to you, that's the window to act in.

## Why this tour, and why "farewell"

This leg follows the release of Megadeth's self-titled seventeenth and final studio album, which debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 — the band's first-ever chart-topping record. Dave Mustaine has framed the run not as a somber goodbye but as a multi-year, multi-continent celebration: the European leg alone touches 12 of the 22 global markets where that album hit the Top 5.

> This isn't about saying goodbye. It's about celebrating everything we've built together over the last four decades.
> — Dave Mustaine

Expect a career-spanning set pulling from four decades of material, though no confirmed setlist exists yet.

## Support acts: Black Label Society and Testament

Both bands are on the full 24-date run, not just select dates. That's a notably strong package — Testament's own thrash pedigree and Zakk Wylde's Black Label Society give this tour a genuine "if you like one, you'll want to see all three" pull, which is worth factoring into travel plans if you're weighing which city to catch it in.
