## Quick answer

**Yes** — Unheilig have a fully confirmed 2027 tour. The arena leg of the Liebe Glaube Monster Tour 2026/27 runs January–February 2027 across Germany, Austria, and Switzerland, followed by a summer open-air run titled Meilenweit Tour – Das Liebe Glaube Monster Finale. **October 2026 check:** arena tickets are on sale now; the summer finale dates are announced and listed below.

## The tour so far, for context

Unheilig's 2027 dates are the payoff of one of German rock's biggest comebacks. The Graf retired the band in 2016 with an emotional farewell at Cologne's RheinEnergieStadion — nine years to the day before the comeback announcement. The new album Liebe Glaube Monster, released in March 2026, is a concept record the Graf described as gathering nine years of life experience into music, a direct continuation of the emotional anthems like "Geboren um zu leben" and "So wie du warst" that made Unheilig a stadium act in the first place.

The tour structure reflects the scale of the return: a winter arena leg hitting the biggest halls in Germany, Austria, and Switzerland (Uber Arena Berlin twice, SAP Arena Mannheim, Wiener Stadthalle, Hallenstadion Zurich), then a summer of open-air finale shows. The Meilenweit branding nods to the band's early catalog, and the summer billing as a "Finale" suggests the 2027 open-airs are the victory lap of this comeback cycle. Swiss promoters reported the Bern Festhalle date sold out nearly a year in advance — demand for the comeback is real.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for the arena leg are on sale through Ticketmaster Germany and the individual venue box offices; the premium "Ticketmaster Suite" packages in Berlin and Hamburg are also listed. Summer open-air tickets are likewise available via the standard promoters. Expect high-demand pricing on the final arena shows — buy from official sellers, not resale sites, and note that the Ticketmaster-listed suite and premium-seat variants are legitimate upsells, not scalper listings.
