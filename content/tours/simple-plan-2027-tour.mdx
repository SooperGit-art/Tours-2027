## Quick answer

**Yes** - Simple Plan officially announced their Bigger Than You Think! Canada Tour on October 6, 2026, with 15 arena dates across eight Canadian provinces from January 25 to February 16, 2027. Yellowcard and Bowling for Soup support the full run, with Marianas Trench appearing on the two British Columbia dates. **October 2026 check:** the Canada dates, venues, and support acts are fully announced and presales are underway; no US, UK, or standalone European headlining dates for 2027 have been confirmed.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Simple Plan have been building toward this for a while. The Montreal pop-punk quartet has sold more than 10 million albums worldwide on the back of early-2000s anthems like "I'm Just a Kid", "I'd Do Anything", "Addicted", "Perfect", "Welcome to My Life", and "Untitled (How Could This Happen to Me?)". Their last major headlining national tour across Canada was the Harder Than It Looks Tour in late 2023, and while they have played individual Canadian stops and festival dates since - including a massive outdoor Toronto show with Bowling for Soup, 3OH!3, and Marianas Trench this past August and the Vans Warped Tour in Montreal - a full cross-Canada run has been over a decade in the making.

The timing is no accident. The band's songs have found a second life online - their "I'm Just a Kid" social media challenge generated over a billion views - and a career-spanning documentary on Amazon Prime Video brought their story to a new generation. Stacking the bill with fellow pop-punk veterans Yellowcard (fresh off their first album in nearly a decade, produced by Travis Barker) and Bowling for Soup turns the tour into a full reunion with an era that never really left.

## Ticket and presale info

Presales ran October 6-8, 2026: an artist presale on October 6 (code via the band's broadcast channel), a Live Nation presale on October 7, and a Spotify "Reserved by Spotify" presale on October 8. General sale is Friday, October 9 at 10am local time via Ticketmaster, with VIP packages available. Prices were not published in the announcement. The band confirmed that $1 from every ticket sold goes to the Simple Plan Foundation, which has donated more than $3 million in Canada and worldwide since 2005.
