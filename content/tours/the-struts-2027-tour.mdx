## Quick answer

**Yes** — The Struts are officially confirmed for 2027. The Derby glam-rockers will join Def Leppard's 2027 North American tour as special guests alongside the Goo Goo Dolls, playing 45 arena dates across the US and Canada from March 2 to July 2, 2027. **October 2026 check:** the dates below are the full announced schedule; no separate Struts headline tour has been announced yet.

## The tour in context

The Struts spent 2025 and 2026 celebrating the 10th anniversary of their debut album *Everybody Wants* with a 36-date North American headline run, wrapping recent US shows in October 2026 including dates in Kansas City and Dubuque. The band — Luke Spiller, Adam Slack, Jed Elliott and Gethin Davies — has a long history as a support act for stadium giants, having opened for the Rolling Stones, Guns N' Roses and Foo Fighters earlier in their career.

That track record made them a natural fit for Def Leppard's 2027 bill, announced October 7, 2026 — just weeks before tickets go on sale. Def Leppard come off a third sold-out Las Vegas residency and sold-out 2026 shows across the UK, Europe, South America and Mexico, while the Goo Goo Dolls bring a viral year behind them with "Iris" topping one billion streams in 2025 alone. For The Struts, the arena run is their biggest North American exposure yet, including landmark stops at the Hollywood Bowl and Madison Square Garden.

No viral fake posters or bogus Struts 2027 schedules turned up during research — the announcement is fresh (October 7, 2026) and sourced directly from the headliner's official channels, so treat any fan-made date graphics circulating online as unofficial.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets go on general sale Friday, October 16 at 10AM local time via LiveNation.com. Def Leppard's Rock Brigade fan club gets first access starting Monday, October 12 at 10AM (join at DefLeppardRockBrigade.com), with additional presales running through the week. Prices have not been published yet. A warmup date is set for October 15 at the Hard Rock Live in Hollywood, Florida.
