## Quick answer

**Yes** — Greensky Bluegrass have announced a Winter Tour 2027 of 24 dates across the United States. The three-leg trek opens January 14, 2027 at The Bellwether in Los Angeles and closes March 19, 2027 at the Ryman Auditorium in Nashville. Support rotates by leg: Magoo in January, Clay Street Unit in February, and Miles Miller in March, with Eggy joining in Denver. **October 2026 check:** all 24 dates are confirmed and on sale this week; nothing about the Winter Tour itself remains unannounced.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

For over two decades, Greensky Bluegrass have built their reputation on exactly this kind of run: long, coast-to-coast touring with die-hard fans travelling between multi-night stands at rooms like Red Rocks and the Ryman. The Winter Tour 2027 follows that pattern — eight California and Mountain West shows in January, a Northeast and Mid-Atlantic swing in February, and a Midwest run into the Ryman finale in March.

The timing also follows the band's 25th-anniversary year, which brought the album XXV and a first-ever live vinyl release drawn from their anniversary celebration at Kalamazoo's Wings Event Center. The band's songwriting stays rooted in traditional bluegrass even as their live shows stretch it outward, which is why a 24-date winter run — rather than a short residency — is the natural shape of a Greensky year. The January 1–2, 2027 Kalamazoo shows that precede the tour are separate New Year dates, not part of the Winter Tour routing.

## Ticket and presale info

Fan-club presale begins Wednesday, October 28, 2026 at 10 a.m., with sign-up information at greenskybluegrass.com/tour. General on-sale follows Friday, October 30, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time. Tickets are sold through the usual outlets, including Ticketmaster, for each venue on the run.
