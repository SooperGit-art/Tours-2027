## Quick answer

**No, not a headline tour — but yes to two festival dates.** The Chicks have no standalone 2027 tour announced. What is confirmed for 2027 is a pair of festival appearances: State Fayre at Hylands Park in Chelmsford, England, on June 18, and LASSO at Parc Jean-Drapeau in Montreal on August 14. Both dates are listed on Ticketmaster.

**November 2026 check:** the two festival dates are confirmed and on sale through the festivals. No headline tour, US dates, or additional 2027 shows have been announced.

## The tour so far, for context

The Chicks — formerly the Dixie Chicks — formed in Dallas, Texas, in 1989, founded by sisters Emily Strayer and Martie Maguire, with lead vocalist Natalie Maines joining in 1995. They went on to become one of country music's most influential and boundary-pushing groups, with 13 Grammy Awards and a catalog that crossed from country to pop stardom. Their 2006 album Taking the Long Way, recorded after years away from the spotlight, became the centerpiece of their most recent road work: the Taking the Long Way 20th Anniversary Tour, which began September 30, 2026, at Detroit's Fox Theatre.

That 2026 anniversary run is the immediate backdrop to the 2027 dates. Rather than announcing a full follow-up tour, the trio has so far confirmed only the two summer festival slots — one in England, one in Canada — nearly two months apart. Festival-only summers are a familiar pattern for legacy acts between album cycles, and with no new album announced, the two dates read as a light 2027 schedule rather than the start of a bigger run. If a headline tour follows, the Chelmsford and Montreal dates suggest the UK and Canada would be part of it.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for the two festival appearances are sold through the festivals themselves: State Fayre day tickets for Chelmsford (including a VIP upgrade option) and LASSO two-day passes for Montreal, both also listed on Ticketmaster. No standalone Chicks headline tickets are on sale anywhere, since no headline tour has been announced. Be wary of third-party listings for other 2027 dates — the Rogers, Arkansas, listing for August 14 conflicts directly with the Montreal show on the same day.
