## Quick answer

**Yes** — Jhené Aiko is touring North America in 2027 on the Ark to Agartha – A Westside Whimsy Experience tour: 37 arena dates, announced November 17, 2026, and produced by Live Nation. The run opens March 10 at the Enterprise Center in St. Louis and closes May 21 at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles, with H.E.R. joining from Brooklyn onward and Swae Lee and Karri on every date.

**November 2026 check:** the full 37-date routing is confirmed, presales run November 18–19, 2026, and general sale opens November 20, 2026. No dates outside North America have been announced.

## The tour so far, for context

Jhené Aiko's return has been building for six years. Her last studio album before this one came in 2020, and her 2024 Magic Hour Tour — her first headlining tour in seven years — sold out arenas across North America. Then came *Westside Whimsy*, released September 11, 2026: her fourth studio album, her first to debut at No. 1 on the Billboard 200, and the first R&B album by a woman to top the chart in 2026. Announcing a 37-date arena tour two months later was the obvious next step, and it is why "Jhene Aiko tour 2027" broke out on Google Trends on November 17–18, 2026, the morning of the announcement.

The bill is stacked for an R&B arena run. Swae Lee and Karri are on every date, and H.E.R. — herself a multi-Grammy winner and arena headliner — joins from Brooklyn onward. The routing covers the full map: a March swing through the Midwest and Northeast, April across the South and Texas, and May up the West Coast to the Los Angeles finale.

## Ticket and presale info

Artist presale registration opened November 17, 2026, via jheneaiko.com/tour. The artist presale begins Wednesday, November 18, at 10 a.m. local time, and Verizon customers get a Shine presale November 18–19. General sale opens Friday, November 20, 2026, at 10 a.m. local time via Live Nation and Ticketmaster. VIP packages — including premium tickets, the Westside Whimsy Soundbath Experience, a pre-show VIP lounge and exclusive merchandise — are available through VIP Nation.
