## Quick answer

**Yes** — Drive-By Truckers are touring heavily in 2027, with two runs announced. Their own early-year dates open February 10 with the four-night HeAthens Homecoming at Athens' 40 Watt Club and run through March 6 in Texas, and then the band co-headlines the 39-date Summer of Joy Tour with Jason Isbell and the 400 Unit from May 6 to September 7. That is 54 announced 2027 dates in total.

**November 2026 check:** both runs are confirmed and on sale (Summer of Joy general sale opens November 20, 2026). The only date still awaiting a venue is September 3 in Portland, Oregon, listed as TBC.

## The tour so far, for context

Drive-By Truckers have been one of America's great road bands for three decades, and 2027 looks like their biggest year in a while. The early run is classic Truckers: a four-night homecoming residency in Athens — their adopted hometown, where the shows again benefit Nuçi's Space — followed by a first full-on tour in over a year through the Southeast and Texas with Brown Horse as direct support.

Then comes the headline story. The Summer of Joy Tour reunites the Truckers with Jason Isbell, who played in the band from 2001 to 2007 and wrote some of its best-loved songs before going solo. The co-headline bill, announced November 17, 2026, is why "Drive-By Truckers tour 2027" broke out on Google Trends the same morning — alongside Isbell's own name. The two acts share the stage each night, and the routing reads like a greatest-hits list of American rooms: Red Rocks, Forest Hills Stadium, the Greek Theatre, two nights at Chicago's Salt Shed and two at Durham's DPAC.
