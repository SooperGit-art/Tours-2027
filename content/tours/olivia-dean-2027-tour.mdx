## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Olivia Dean is coming off the biggest two years of her career. Her second album, *The Art of Loving* (2025), turned the BRIT School graduate into one of Britain's defining contemporary voices, powered by hits like "Man I Need" — she became the first female solo artist in history to hold four singles in the UK top 10 at once. She then won Best New Artist at the 2026 Grammy Awards, the ultimate industry validation.

She toured behind the album with *The Art of Loving Live* in 2026, her biggest headline run to date, playing UK arenas including Glasgow and Manchester and a run of shows at The O2 in London, plus dates across Europe, North America, Australia and New Zealand stretching into October 2026. Artists at this stage of their career — a fresh Grammy, a breakthrough album cycle, and a sold-out arena run — almost always return to the road the following year. A full Olivia Dean 2027 tour would be one of the most anticipated British soul tours of the year, but until her team announces it, Lollapalooza India remains the only fixed date.
