## Quick answer

**Yes** — The Wiggles are touring the UK at Easter 2027 with their Sparkle! BIG SHOW arena tour. Confirmed dates so far: Cardiff (March 27, two shows), Manchester (April 2), Edinburgh (April 6), and Glasgow (April 7), as part of a 10-city UK and Ireland run — their first Irish appearance in a decade. Presale opened October 20, 2026; general onsale starts October 23. **October 2026 check:** the tour was announced only today, so the remaining UK and Ireland dates have yet to surface.

## Why a 2027 Wiggles tour makes sense

The Wiggles are one of Australia's biggest cultural exports, formed in Sydney in 1991 and now more than 35 years into bringing music and movement to preschoolers. Founder Anthony Field — still performing in blue — leads an eight-Wiggle lineup that now includes his daughter Lucia, with classics like "Hot Potato," "Fruit Salad," and "Rock-A-Bye Your Bear" staples of family life across generations.

The group has been touring the UK consistently: their 2026 Tree of Wisdom Spectacular played three nights at Edinburgh's Usher Hall and three at Glasgow's SEC Armadillo. The 2027 Sparkle! BIG SHOW steps up to full arena scale — OVO Hydro in Glasgow, AO Arena in Manchester — timed for the Easter school holidays, when family shows sell fastest. Field has said it is their "biggest and brightest show yet."

## Ticket and presale info

Presale for the 2027 UK dates opened October 20, 2026 at 10AM local time, with general onsale starting October 23 at 10AM via Ticketmaster. Family arena shows at Easter sell quickly — the double Cardiff booking shows demand — so the onsale date is the one to watch for Manchester, Edinburgh, and Glasgow. Ticketmaster UK lists the Cardiff, Manchester, and Glasgow dates; the Edinburgh Usher Hall date was confirmed via Scottish press listings.
