## Quick answer

**Yes** — Florida Georgia Line is genuinely touring together again in 2027. Tyler Hubbard and Brian Kelley officially announced the **Anything Goes Again: The Festival Tour** on September 15, 2026: a 12-stop festival run across the summer. Named stops so far are Stagecoach (Indio, April 23-25), Carolina Country Music Fest (Myrtle Beach, June 10-13), Windy City Smokeout (Chicago, July 8-11), and XRoads41 (Oshkosh, Aug. 12-14) — plus a headline show at the Pro Football Hall of Fame's Concert for Legends in Canton, Ohio on **Aug. 8, 2027**, the only stop with an officially confirmed performance day so far.

**October 2026 check:** the remaining 8 of the 12 festival stops have not been revealed — the duo announced them blurred out on a teaser graphic and is unveiling them one by one.

## Why the 2027 reunion is real, not a rumor

Florida Georgia Line were the dominant country duo of the 2010s — 19 No. 1 singles, the Diamond-certified "Cruise" (the best-selling digital country single of all time at certification), and the record-shattering "Meant to Be" with Bebe Rexha. They announced an indefinite hiatus in 2022, and the split turned genuinely bitter before both members rebuilt as solo acts: Hubbard stacked four straight No. 1 solo singles, and Kelley released his own albums and toured under his own name.

The road back started quietly. In mid-2026, Hubbard began telling interviewers that he and Kelley were talking about "some things in the works" for 2027 and admitted they were "itching to get back on stage." Then came the CMA Fest surprise in June 2026 — ring announcer Michael Buffer introducing them from opposite sides of Nissan Stadium, a two-song set of "Round Here" and "Cruise," and Hubbard's on-mic declaration: "We back, baby." Within weeks they signed new management and booking representation, the dormant FGL Instagram lit up again, and a Nashville billboard with a textable phone number teased "much more to come." The September tour announcement was the payoff. The name — *Anything Goes Again* — is a deliberate callback to their 2014 peak-era album, signaling that this comeback is about the songbook first.
