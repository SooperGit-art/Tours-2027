## Why a 2027 world tour makes sense

Sienna Spiro's rise has been one of the fastest in recent pop. The 20-year-old London singer-songwriter broke through on TikTok, where she has posted singing videos since 2021, and released her debut single "Need Me" in May 2024. Her 2025 EP *Sink Now, Swim Later* set up a breakout 2026: singles "Die On This Hill", "The Visitor" and "You Stole The Show" all charted on the Billboard Hot 100, her music passed 1.2 billion global streams, and she covered the June/July issue of Rolling Stone UK.

The *My House Tour* name comes from her song "My House", and her live show has a signature look — plush curtains, carpeted floors, vintage audio equipment and lamps — that earned rave reviews on the 2026 North American leg, with Variety calling her "one of the greatest voices of her generation". With the debut album *Visitor* recorded at Electric Lady, Abbey Road and Valentine studios, the 2027 world tour is the victory lap of a genuine breakout.
