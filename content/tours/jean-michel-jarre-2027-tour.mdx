## The tour so far, for context

Jean-Michel Jarre is the godfather of arena-scale electronic music. *Oxygène* (1976), recorded in his Paris home studio, sold millions and made him a global phenomenon; he holds multiple Guinness records for concert attendance, including 3.5 million in Moscow in 1997, and he headlined the closing ceremony of the Paris 2024 Olympics. After a decade away from France, the Oxygène & Beyond Tour marks a triumphant return — his three-night Paris stand at La Seine Musicale sold itself on nostalgia and spectacle before a single ticket went on sale.

This is also a forward-looking tour. Jarre is finishing a new album for spring 2027 and will perform some of its music live for the first time on this run. He described the show as "both a celebration of where it all began and an invitation to imagine what comes next" — expect the immersive, laser-heavy production he is famous for, rebuilt around the anniversary.
