## Quick answer

**Yes** — Joe Bonamassa is touring in 2027, with five dates confirmed so far: one US show at the Riverside Theater in Milwaukee on February 12, and four UK shows announced on November 23, 2026 — an outdoor headline show at The Piece Hall in Halifax on July 4, with special guest Toby Lee, followed by three arena dates in October: Bournemouth (October 25), Birmingham (October 27) and Glasgow (October 28).

**November 2026 check:** the four UK dates are confirmed, with a Planet Rock presale from November 25 and general sale on November 27, 2026. No London date is on the routing, and no further legs have been announced yet.

## The tour so far, for context

Joe Bonamassa is one of the hardest-working live acts in blues rock, playing well over a hundred shows most years, and the 2027 UK dates are his first announced return to Britain since his 2025 studio album *Breakthrough*. The October run is a compact arena swing — Bournemouth, Birmingham, Glasgow — with the July Halifax date as the summer outdoor centrepiece at the Piece Hall, one of the UK's most striking open-air venues.

The announcement, on November 23, 2026, is what pushed "joe bonamassa uk tour dates 2027" onto Google Trends: a three-date October arena tour plus the Halifax headline, with Planet Rock running the presale. Bonamassa has built his UK following the slow way, through constant touring rather than hit singles, and the October dates are sized accordingly — arenas, not theatres.
