## Quick answer

**Yes** — Netflix and AEG Presents announced the KPop Demon Hunters Golden World Tour on November 13, 2026, and it is a real arena tour, not a rumour. The show opens June 5, 2027, at CFG Bank Arena in Baltimore, with 61 performances announced in 35 cities across North America and Europe, and more than 150 performances planned across 2027 and 2028. **November 2026 check:** Mastercard presale begins November 18, fan presale November 19, and general sale opens Friday, November 20 at 10am local time.

## The tour so far, for context

*KPop Demon Hunters* started as a Netflix animated musical film and became a genuine phenomenon: the film sustained 63 consecutive weeks in Netflix's Global Top 10, "Golden" passed 2 billion Spotify streams, and the soundtrack broke the record for the longest-running No. 1 on Billboard's Top Soundtracks chart. The fictional girl group HUNTR/X — Rumi, Mira and Zoey — and their songs became real-world hits, which is why a live arena show was inevitable.

The Golden World Tour is that show: a next-generation K-pop concert experience built around the film's music, with the animated characters interacting with the audience through performance technology, live-action stunts and performers, choreography and audience participation. The Saja Boys and Derpy Tiger are part of the production too. Creative direction comes from Amy Tinkham, whose credits include Aerosmith's Peace Out Tour, Cirque du Soleil's Songblazers and Disney's Descendants/Zombies: Worlds Collide Tour. Several markets get two shows on the same day — a matinee and an evening performance — which is why the 25-show North American routing adds up to more performances than dates. The announcement drove "kpop demon hunters tour 2027" up 4,900% on Google Trends, the biggest breakout in today's pull.

## Ticket and presale info

Mastercard presale begins Wednesday, November 18, 2026, at 10am local time, followed by a fan presale on Thursday, November 19 — fans must register at kpopdemonhunterstour.com to access it. General sale opens Friday, November 20 at 10am local time via Ticketmaster. A limited number of VIP experiences and packages will be available, which may include reserved seating, on-stage photo opportunities, limited-edition memorabilia and custom merchandise. Trip.com is the tour's official travel partner.
