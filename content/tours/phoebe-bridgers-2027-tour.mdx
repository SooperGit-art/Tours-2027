## The tour so far, for context

Phoebe Bridgers kicked off The Lost Tour in Indiana in September 2026 and toured North America through the fall behind her third studio album, *Lost Weekend*, released in August 2026. Her most recent stops before the announcement were sold-out runs in San Francisco and Inglewood, California. She then crosses to the UK and Europe for sold-out runs in late November and December — Dublin, Manchester, Glasgow and two nights at London's O2 — before returning to North America on February 17.

The 2027 leg was unveiled on November 12, 2026, just one day after Bridgers was revealed as part of the Coachella and Hinterland festival lineups. The routing is arena-scale throughout: Moda Center in Portland, Rogers Place in Edmonton, Fiserv Forum in Milwaukee, Centre Bell in Montreal and the Kaseya Center in Miami, closing with a May 13 finale at the Palacio de los Deportes in Mexico City. The announcement drove "phoebe bridgers tour 2027" up 2,200% on Google Trends, the sharpest rise in today's pull.

## Ticket and presale info

Presale registration for the US and Canada shows is open now on phoebebridgers.com and closes Sunday, November 15, 2026, at 11:59pm local time. Fans who register are randomly assigned a presale day of either November 18 or November 19, with presales ending November 19 at 10pm local time. General on-sale opens Friday, November 20 at 10am local time via Ticketmaster. Coachella passes are sold separately, on sale November 13 at noon PT.
