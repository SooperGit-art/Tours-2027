**Yes** — greenbeats are touring Germany in spring 2027. The drum show's Momentum Tour '27 lists 8 dates so far, opening March 11 at Europahalle in Trier and running through May 28 at the Metropol Theater in Bremen, with arena stops in Hamburg and Rust along the way.

**November 2026 check:** all 8 dates below are listed on venue and ticket sites, with tickets on sale. More dates may be added — the tour has grown every year.

## The tour so far, for context

greenbeats are a German percussion act, billed by venues as "the most spectacular drum show in Europe" — an epic concert experience built around drumming, staging and showmanship rather than a conventional band set. The Momentum show follows a completely sold-out tour in 2025 and a 2026 run that included Berlin's Admiralspalast in May 2026. The 2027 routing sticks to Germany and scales up: the tour opens in the west (Trier, Emsdetten, Aurich), plays two nights at the Europa-Park Arena in Rust, then hits the big northern rooms — Hamburg's Barclays Arena, Paderborn's Paderhalle and Bremen's Metropol Theater.

One note for searchers: some ticket aggregators list only 3 greenbeats dates for 2027, because their listings are incomplete. The venue listings themselves show the full run of 8 dates below.
