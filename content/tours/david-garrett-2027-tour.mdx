## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Garrett is built for exactly this kind of tour: a German violinist who has sold more than five million albums and collected 25 gold and 17 platinum awards, moving between the classical world and rock stages. He plays a 1716 Stradivari "Ex Busch" and a 1734 Guarneri del Gesù, played Paganini on screen in the 2013 film The Devil's Violinist, and most recently toured the crossover programme Millennium Symphony — the project that showed how comfortably he pairs orchestral playing with stadium rock hits.

Immortal is deliberately a step back toward pure classical: Garrett arranged the entire album himself, joined by co-arrangers Franck van der Heijden and John Haywood, with The Prezent orchestra and pianist Julien Quentin. A world tour launching in February 2027, with Poland already named as one of its stops by Polish ticket outlets, fits that album cycle — but an album-cycle announcement is not a date list, and the gap between the two is where unverified listings breed. Until the label publishes routing, fans should treat any shared 2027 date list as unconfirmed.
