## Quick answer

**Yes** — La Voix has confirmed La Voix Live, a 35-date UK theatre tour for spring 2027. The show opens March 5, 2027, at BEAM in Hertford and closes April 30, 2027, at The Anvil in Basingstoke, with a London Palladium date on March 22, 2027, in between.

**November 2026 check:** all 35 dates listed here are confirmed, including the three dates added after the initial announcement — London, Manchester, and Blackpool. No dates outside the UK have been announced.

## The tour so far, for context

La Voix has toured UK theatres for a decade, most recently with the sold-out tour The Show Isn't Over, and built a much wider television audience in 2025 on Strictly Come Dancing, followed by appearances on This Morning and as the UK's Eurovision vote reader in 2026. She also played Miss Hannigan in Annie The Musical in 2026 to strong reviews. La Voix Live is pitched as the step up from that run: the same cabaret mix of showstoppers, stories, and comedy, but on her largest theatre routing yet.

The tour's shape is classic UK theatre touring — a new town almost every night through March and April, from Hertford to Glasgow, Cardiff to Carlisle. The London Palladium date is the symbolic centrepiece: her debut headlining performance at the West End venue, added after demand for the first announcement, alongside the Manchester and Blackpool additions.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets are sold through the individual theatres on the tour, with listings also on major UK ticket sellers. Because this is a theatre tour rather than a single-promoter arena run, prices and on-sale details vary by venue — the theatre's own listing for your date is the most reliable source, and the Palladium, Manchester, and Blackpool dates were added after the initial announcement, so they are worth checking separately if they first showed as unavailable.
