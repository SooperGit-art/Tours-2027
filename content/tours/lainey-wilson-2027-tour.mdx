## The road to 2027, for context

Wilson wrapped the Whirlwind World Tour in 2025 — the extensive, Live Nation-produced run supporting her Whirlwind album that carried her across North America with rotating openers including ERNEST, Muscadine Bloodline, Drake Milligan and others. The tour cemented her status at the top of mainstream country: a Grammy winner, a back-to-back CMA Entertainer of the Year, and the owner of nine country No. 1 hits including "Heart Like A Truck," "Watermelon Moonshine" and "4x4xU."

Since then her profile has kept expanding past the recording studio. She moved into acting with a role in Yellowstone, a Netflix documentary (Lainey Wilson: Keepin' Country Cool), a film acting debut in the Colleen Hoover adaptation Reminders of Him, and a second season of the Netflix sitcom Leanne. She also took on stadium bills alongside Chris Stapleton, showing she can command crowds well beyond the country faithful.

Musically, October 2026 brought her latest single, "Kinda Love It," co-written with John Mayer and Kid Harpoon — a playful, adventurous move that suggests she is having fun with the freedom that comes with sustained success. Her team has framed her as "gearing up for her next album," which is the usual prelude to a tour announcement. The Harry Styles support slot, announced in September 2026, puts her in front of up to 100,000 pop fans across two weekends in Toronto — a crossover moment that mirrors Styles' previous run with Shania Twain. If a headline tour follows the new album, that stadium-scale exposure will not have hurt.
