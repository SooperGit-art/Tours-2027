## Quick answer

**Yes** — Noah Kahan has officially announced four 2027 dates on **The Last Of The Bugs World Tour**, all of them first-ever stadium shows in the UK and Ireland: Glasgow's Hampden Park (13 July), Dublin's Aviva Stadium (17 July), and two consecutive nights at London's Wembley Stadium (23–24 July). Tickets went on sale in August 2026 and are available now. **October 2026 check:** only the UK/Ireland leg is announced so far — Kahan himself says more dates are coming, so US and world dates are still unannounced.

## Why a 2027 stadium run makes sense

Noah Kahan's rise has been one of the fastest in modern folk-pop. His breakthrough 2022 album *Stick Season* topped the charts in the US, UK, Canada and Ireland, earned him a 2023 Grammy nomination for Best New Artist, and turned the Vermont songwriter into a generational live draw. His fourth album, *The Great Divide*, released in April 2026, became his second UK Number One and delivered the biggest first-week debut for an international male artist in the UK that year.

The scale of his live business now demands stadiums. The Great Divide World Tour has sold more than 1.5 million tickets globally, he became the first artist to headline four consecutive sold-out nights at Boston's Fenway Park, and he played his largest North American headline show to date at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena — with a surprise duet with Chappell Roan. He also performed the title track and "Doors" on *Saturday Night Live* earlier in 2026.

The 2027 shows carry the name *The Last Of The Bugs World Tour*, taken from the deluxe edition of the album — and Kahan has made clear this is the start, not the end. "This is only the first of the last of the bugs… stay tuned for more," he wrote when announcing the shows, which means fans everywhere else are still waiting for their dates.
