## Quick answer

**Yes** — Ritchie Blackmore returns to the stage in March 2027 with Blackmore's Night, the medieval and folk-based project he leads with his wife Candice Night. The six-show US East Coast run opens March 6, 2027, at the Newton Theatre in Newton, New Jersey, and closes March 27, 2027, at Bergen Performing Arts Center in Englewood, New Jersey, marking the project's official 30th anniversary. **November 2026 check:** all six dates are announced, general sale starts November 7, 2026 (November 9 for Port Washington), and no dates beyond this run have been announced.

## The tour so far, for context

Blackmore is best known as the Deep Purple guitar legend, but for nearly three decades his main focus has been Blackmore's Night, the project he formed with Candice Night in the late 1990s, blending Renaissance and folk music with rock musicianship. The March 2027 run is deliberately close to home — every date sits in New York, New Jersey or Connecticut, near where Blackmore lives — and deliberately small, with theatre-sized rooms rather than arenas.

The comeback framing matters. Blackmore's Night had to abandon the remaining dates of its US East Coast tour at the end of 2025 after Blackmore dealt with health issues over the previous couple of years, and the November 3, 2026, announcement, shared by Candice Night, was pitched as a return: dust off the garb, celebrate thirty years of the project, and pick up where the band left off, starting at the same kind of intimate East Coast theatre the project has always favoured. At nearly 82 when the run begins, Blackmore is not launching a world tour — he is marking an anniversary with six shows his audience can actually reach.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for five of the six shows go on general sale on Friday, November 7, 2026, with the Port Washington show following on Monday, November 9, 2026. Presale dates and information were still to be announced when the run was revealed on November 3, 2026, so fans should watch Blackmore's Night's official channels for presale news before the general sale. A Ticketmaster listing for a November 16, 2027, Wilmington show is marked cancelled and is not part of this run, so it should not be bought or counted.
