**Yes** — but as special guests, not headliners. ## Why this 2027 run makes sense

The Sheffield five-piece fronted by Oli Sykes has grown from deathcore beginnings into one of the biggest arena draws in modern heavy music, blending metalcore with electronic and alt-rock across albums like Post Human: Survival Horror (2020) and Post Human: Nex Gen (2024). They are genuine UK arena headliners in their own right — which is what makes this billing a heavyweight double bill rather than a typical opener slot.

The tour was announced on September 28, 2026, after weeks of cryptic stadium posters teasing flaming Slipknot logos in cities worldwide. It's Slipknot's first-ever stadium tour, and pairing them with BMTH puts two of modern metal's biggest live draws on one bill for 13 North American stadiums — a package built to sell.
