## The tour so far, for context

Grupo Frontera formed in Edinburg, Texas, in 2019 and exploded in 2022 with a viral cover of Morat's "No Se Va", riding the regional Mexican wave to a first No. 1 on Billboard's Regional Mexican Airplay chart with the Carín León collaboration "Que Vuelvas" and a Latin Grammy in 2024 for Best Norteño Album. Their third album, *Lo Que Me Falta Por Llorar*, arrived October 23, 2025, and the Triste Pero Bien Cabrón Tour built on that momentum with a 27-date US arena run that opened July 16, 2026, at Bert Ogden Arena in their hometown of Edinburg and closed September 12 at the Kia Center in Orlando, after earlier legs across Mexico, Central and South America and Europe.

The demand behind that run is real: the group counts around 37 million monthly Spotify listeners and 7 million social followers, and the US leg played arenas from Las Vegas and Inglewood to Tampa and Orlando. With the 2026 dates winding down in Mexico in December, a 2027 run would be a natural next step — but the band has said nothing about one yet, and the trending searches this week appear to be driven by fans looking ahead rather than by any announcement.

One caution for fans searching right now: grupofronteratour.us presents itself as an "official tour schedule" and describes a 2027 world tour beginning in Miami, visiting Los Angeles, New York, Mexico City, Buenos Aires, London and Tokyo. The same page concedes there are "no concrete details" and that its information only goes back to December 2023 — it is AI-generated filler, not a tour announcement, and none of its dates, cities or on-sale dates should be trusted.
