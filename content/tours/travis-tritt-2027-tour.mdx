**Yes** — Travis Tritt has a full 2027 tour confirmed: 33 dates from March 9 to October 16, 2027, announced November 10, 2026. ## The tour so far, for context

Travis Tritt has spent more than three decades proving that country music sounds its best with a dose of Southern rock and blues, and the two-time Grammy winner is still selling out rooms on that formula. The 2027 run follows his newest album, *Country Chapel*, and it is one of the longest single announcements of his career: 33 cities from March through October, mixing the festival stages and honky-tonks that built his audience with casino and performing-arts rooms.

The family angle is part of the draw this time. Daughter Tyler Reese Tritt joins him in Macon, Durham and New Haven, Kentucky, while son Tristan Tritt plays both Mount Vernon, Kentucky, shows — a passing-of-the-torch element that has featured in the announcement coverage. The tour's breakout on Google Trends the day after the announcement shows the demand is still there 35-plus years in.

## Ticket and presale info

Presales for the 2027 dates began Wednesday, November 11, 2026, ahead of the general sale on Friday, November 13 at 10am local time for most dates. The March 12 Ryman Auditorium show is already sold out, so Nashville fans should look at the September 30 Brown County Music Center date in Nashville, Indiana, or resale. Tickets are listed through Ticketmaster and the usual venue outlets; a few dates on the full list still show venues as to be determined, so check the listing before booking travel.
