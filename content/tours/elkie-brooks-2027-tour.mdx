## The tour so far, for context

Elkie Brooks started performing in 1960, and the Long Farewell Tour is a celebration of an extraordinary career: she sang with Vinegar Joe alongside Robert Palmer, scored a UK Top 10 hit with "Pearl's a Singer" in 1977, and headlined a sold-out Royal Albert Hall the same year. Her return to the Hall in May 2027 comes almost exactly 50 years after both milestones, and at 81 she frames it plainly — "it's not about the milestones, it's about the moments."

The tour itself has been running since September 2024 and has grown by demand: over 40 UK shows and more than 60,000 tickets sold, with the 2027 extension adding the Royal Albert Hall night plus dates in Folkestone, Ipswich, Stockton-on-Tees, Halifax, Cardiff, Eastbourne and Newcastle. The show is a career-spanning set with her live band, touching on blues, rock and jazz — "Pearl's a Singer," "Lilac Wine," "Fool (If You Think It's Over)," "No More The Fool" and "Don't Cry Out Loud" among them.
