## The tour so far, for context

King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard are the Australian six-piece who built one of the most prolific catalogues in modern rock — 28 albums across psych, garage, folk and electronic sounds — and the 2027 tour is structured to match that range. Rather than one continuous trek, the USA/Canada '27 tour splits into two legs: an August run that opens on the West Coast, swings through the Gorge Amphitheatre and six Canadian cities, and an October leg that picks back up in the fall across the southern and eastern United States before closing in Chicago.

The June dates set the tone first: a French headline show, a Bologna festival-scale date, two nights at London's Eventim Apollo, a Manchester arena night and a headline slot at Bristol Sounds 2027, where the band were announced as headliners of one of the UK's most dramatic festival backdrops. The tour arrives alongside *Entropy*, their 29th studio album and the companion record to 2026's electronic release *Alien Metal*, with the lead single already out. The announcement drove "king gizzard tour 2027" to breakout status on Google Trends.
