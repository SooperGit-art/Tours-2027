## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

U2's most recent live work was the record-breaking Sphere residency in Las Vegas (2023–2024), where 40 sold-out shows grossed over $244 million — the fastest-grossing residency the city has ever seen. Before that, the 2017 Joshua Tree tour was the highest-grossing tour on the planet that year, pulling $316 million from 2.7 million fans across 38 cities.

The pieces for a return are lining up. The band has completed its first album of new material since 2017, due in late 2026 to coincide with U2's 50th anniversary. Larry Mullen Jr., who missed the entire Sphere residency due to neck surgery, has recovered and is playing again. In interviews the members have made it clear they want to get back on stage.

The most credible speculation — from long-running fan tracker U2songs.com — sketches a first leg in Mexico and South America in early 2027 followed by a European stadium leg from late May to July 2027, with an announcement possibly around October 2026. Stadium holds have reportedly been seen in the UK, Italy, Germany, and Ireland, including Croke Park in Dublin. None of this is confirmed, and it may change or never materialize.
