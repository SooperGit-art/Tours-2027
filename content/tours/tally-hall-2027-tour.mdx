## Quick answer

**Yes** — Tally Hall are touring again. The Ann Arbor band have announced a six-date spring 2027 tour, their first in 15 years, opening April 14 in Philadelphia and closing May 2 in Los Angeles, with stops in New York, Ann Arbor, Chicago and San Diego in between. **October 2026 check:** all six dates are confirmed; presales begin October 28, 2026 and general on-sale is October 30. Venues for the Philadelphia and Los Angeles shows were not named in the announcement coverage — this page will be updated when they are confirmed.

## The tour so far, for context

Tally Hall formed in Ann Arbor in late 2002 while the members were University of Michigan students, building a cult following on theatrical performances and intricate, unconventional pop-rock. They released Marvin's Marvelous Mechanical Museum in 2005 and Good & Evil in 2011, then entered an indefinite hiatus after their last regular period of activity that year. During the break, Cantor built an online audience, Sedghi became a family physician, Horowitz worked as a songwriter and producer, and Federman entered biotechnology.

The band's music never stopped finding listeners online. "Hidden in the Sand" has passed 350 million Spotify plays, and the band counted about 2.6 million Spotify monthly listeners in October 2026. After cryptic teaser posts in September 2026 — flyers around Ann Arbor, a phone number, a photo of the Blind Pig — the band played a reunion show at that same Ann Arbor club on October 22, 2026, their first together in over 15 years, with Casey Shea and Bora Karaca joining the four members. Local coverage described a crowd in a frenzy resembling Beatlemania. Four days later, the six-date spring tour was announced.

## Ticket and presale info

Presales begin October 28, 2026, with general on-sale October 30 at 10 a.m. through Ticketmaster. The Chicago show at Metro lists doors at 6 p.m. and a 7 p.m. start, with advance general admission at $35 and a VIP package at $155 — expect similar pricing tiers elsewhere. With only six dates and 15 years of pent-up demand, the smaller rooms on this run are likely to sell fast.
