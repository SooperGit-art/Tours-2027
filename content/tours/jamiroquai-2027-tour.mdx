## Quick answer

**Yes** — Jamiroquai are touring in 2027. The acid-jazz funk band announced a UK and European arena tour on November 6, 2026: 7 dates in July, opening with two nights at Amsterdam's Ziggo Dome (July 5–6), then two nights at Paris's Accor Arena (July 9–10), closing with a three-night run at London's The O2 (July 15, 16 and 19).

**November 2026 check:** all 7 dates below are officially announced. The tour runs ahead of the band's ninth studio album, which still has no title or release date, and no further 2027 dates have been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Jamiroquai, led by frontman Jay Kay, have been quiet on the album front since Automaton in 2017, but 2026 marked a clear comeback: the band returned to Japan and Latin America for high-profile shows, including headline festival appearances at Summer Sonic in Tokyo and Osaka and Rock in Rio in Brazil, celebrating 30 years since their genre-defining breakthrough "Virtual Insanity". The 2027 arena run is the next step — short, high-demand residencies in three major cities rather than a long slog, and timed ahead of the ninth album, their first in a decade.

The London dates carry extra weight: they form part of The O2's 20th anniversary celebrations in 2027, and the band have billed them as their only UK arena shows of the year. With just 7 dates announced and a presale tied to album pre-orders, demand is likely to outstrip supply quickly.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets go on general sale at 9am local time on Friday, November 13, 2026 via jamiroquai.com/events. A fan presale runs from 9am on Tuesday, November 10, 2026 until the general sale, with access for fans who pre-order any format of the forthcoming ninth studio album from the official store. No support acts or stage times have been confirmed yet.
