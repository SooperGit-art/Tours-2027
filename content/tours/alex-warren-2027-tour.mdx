## The tour so far, for context

Alex Warren's rise from Hype House creator to global pop star has been one of the fastest of the decade. His breakthrough single "Ordinary" became the top-selling song of 2025, spending 10 weeks at No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100 and 13 weeks atop the UK Singles Chart, and his catalog has amassed more than 11 billion streams. His debut album You'll Be Alright, Kid reached the Billboard 200 Top 5, and in August 2026 he released his second album WILDCHILD — a deeply personal record about his childhood featuring Nile Rodgers and John Mayer — which shot straight to No. 1 on the UK Albums Chart.

The live ascent has matched the recordings: Warren headlined sold-out arena tours worldwide, including two huge shows at The O2 in London on his 2026 "Finding Family On The Road" arena tour, and appeared at the GRAMMYs, MTV VMAs, and American Music Awards. The 2027 outdoor run is his biggest headline production yet — a deliberate step from arenas to major open-air parks and castles, headlined by the two-night London stand at Crystal Palace Park, a venue with a lineage that includes The Rolling Stones, Bob Marley, and Pink Floyd.
