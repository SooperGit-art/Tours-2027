The Happy Together Tour is a long-running concert revue celebrating 1960s and early-1970s pop and rock, anchored by The Turtles — whose 1967 No. 1 hit "Happy Together" gives the tour its name. Each night packs several hitmakers onto one bill: recent editions have featured Jay and the Americans ("Come a Little Bit Closer", "Cara Mia"), Little Anthony ("Tears on My Pillow", "Goin' Out of My Head"), and rotating guests including The Cowsills, The Association, The Vogues and Badfinger featuring Joey Molland, with The Turtles' Mark Volman acting as host and emcee.
