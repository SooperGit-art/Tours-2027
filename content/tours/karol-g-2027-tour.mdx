## The Tropitour so far, for context

Karol G arrives at this tour as one of the biggest live draws in Latin music. Her previous *Mañana Será Bonito* stadium tour ran through 2023–2024, capping a run that saw her become the first woman to debut at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 with a Spanish-language album — while *TQG*, her collaboration with Shakira, went Top 10 on the Hot 100 and won the Latin Grammy for Best Urban Song. That era established her as a stadium-level headliner across the Americas and Europe, so a full-scale world tour behind the follow-up album was widely expected.

The follow-up album, *Tropicoqueta* (2025), is her fifth studio record and the entire reason for the Tropitour's staging: the show's setlist is built around new material like "Latina Foreva", "Si Antes Te Hubiera Conocido" and the title track, reworked into tropical-leaning arrangements (cumbia, salsa and mariachi versions of her hits) alongside staples like "Tusa", "Provenza", "Amargura" and "TQG". It is a noticeably different production concept from the *Mañana Será Bonito* era, leaning into Latin roots music traditions.

The announcement itself was a spectacle: she confirmed the tour at the end of her Coachella 2026 headlining performance, with "We're going on tour" projected on the festival screens. When the first presale opened on April 27, 2026 (North America and Europe) and the Latin American presale followed on April 29, demand was high enough that second dates were added almost immediately in most major markets — Santiago's three added shows equaled Coldplay's record of four consecutive nights at Chile's National Stadium.
