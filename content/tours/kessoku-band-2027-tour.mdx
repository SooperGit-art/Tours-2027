## Quick answer

**Yes** — Kessoku Band have confirmed a four-show Asia tour for 2027, ASIA TOUR 2027 “To Asia.”, announced November 1, 2026. The run opens with two Osaka shows on March 13 and 14, 2027, then visits Seoul on May 8 and closes in Hong Kong on May 22, 2027.

**November 2026 check:** all four dates and venues are confirmed. Osaka's official advance ticket lottery opened on the announcement day; no further cities have been added.

## The tour so far, for context

Kessoku Band are an unusual touring act: they began as the fictional band at the heart of the anime Bocchi the Rock!, but the voice cast — Yoshino Aoyama as Hitori Gotoh, Sayumi Suzushiro as Nijika Ijichi, Saku Mizuno as Ryo Yamada, and Ikumi Hasegawa as Ikuyo Kita — perform live as the band, backed by a professional band. Their debut album topped Japan's digital charts in 2023, and they have since played the Zepp Tour 2024 across five Japanese cities and their first solo arena show, We Will B, in February 2025.

The Asia tour is their first tour outside Japan as a touring unit, following a first overseas solo concert in Korea in December 2025 and a first overseas festival appearance at Taiwan's Megaport Festival in March 2026. It was announced alongside the news of a new Bocchi the Rock! film for 2027, which helps explain the timing — the tour and the film are the franchise's two big 2027 events.

## Ticket and presale info

Osaka's official advance ticket lottery opened on November 1, 2026, the day of the announcement, with applications via Lawson Ticket. International fans should watch the official Bocchi the Rock! social channels for Seoul and Hong Kong ticketing details, which are handled separately by city — do not trust unofficial resale listings that appeared before the announcement, as they predate the confirmed dates.
