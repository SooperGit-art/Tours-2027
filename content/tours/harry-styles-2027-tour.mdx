## Quick answer

**Yes** — Harry Styles is officially touring in 2027. His Together, Together tour extends into a 41-date stadium run running April 2 to August 28, 2027, covering Phoenix, Dallas, Los Angeles, Toronto, Atlanta, Chicago, Berlin, Paris, Madrid, Rome, Dublin and London. The tour closes with three final nights at Wembley Stadium on August 25, 27 and 28. **October 2026 check:** every listed date was announced by Styles himself on September 10, 2026 or added over the following weeks due to demand; no further 2027 dates have been announced beyond Wembley.

## The tour so far, for context

Harry Styles ended a long hiatus with his fourth studio album, Kiss All The Time. Disco, Occasionally., released in March 2026, and launched the Together, Together tour in Amsterdam on May 16, 2026. The 2026 leg moved through a 12-night Wembley Stadium run in June, São Paulo and Mexico City in July and August, and then a landmark 30-night residency at Madison Square Garden in New York from August to October 2026, including two "Harryween" shows on October 30 and 31.

While mid-way through the MSG residency, Styles told fans on September 10, 2026 that more dates were coming, posting "Together, Together. 2027." — and revealed the 2027 stadium extension the same day. He has said the extra shows came directly from fan demand, admitting the scale of it was initially "daunting." More dates were added in late September and on October 2, 2026, extending stays in Dallas, Los Angeles, Toronto, Chicago, Berlin, Paris, Madrid and Dublin. The tour continues its sustainability push through Live Nation's Green Nation initiative.

Styles has also said that once the three Wembley shows wrap on August 28, 2027, he plans to step back from touring's "brutal demands" — so these may be the final shows of the Together, Together era for a long while.

## Ticket and presale info

General sale for North American dates began September 16, 2026 at 9am local time, following an artist presale on September 15 (fans had to register by September 13). The London Wembley dates went on general sale September 17, 2026 at 11am UK time. Tickets have been sold through Ticketmaster and Live Nation, with hospitality packages available via Seat Unique for the Wembley shows. Given the multiple sellouts that triggered the added October dates, late-announced shows like the extra Madrid and Dublin dates sold fastest — check official resale channels if your city shows as sold out.
