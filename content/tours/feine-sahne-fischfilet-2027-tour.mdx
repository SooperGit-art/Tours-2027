## Quick answer

**Yes** — Feine Sahne Fischfilet are touring in 2027. The German political-punk band announced the **"Endlich auf Reise"** tour in August 2026: 14 concerts across Germany and Switzerland from February 18 to March 9, 2027, with fellow Rostock punks Dritte Wahl as special guests on every date. **October 2026 check:** all 14 dates are officially announced and on sale; no additional legs or festival dates for 2027 have been confirmed yet.

## The tour so far, for context

Feine Sahne Fischfilet released their seventh studio album, *WIR KOMMEN IN FRIEDEN*, on May 30, 2025, and it went straight to number 1 on the German album charts. In July 2026 the band celebrated their 20th anniversary with a massive hometown concert in front of 17,000 fans at Rostock's IGA Park — and rather than slowing down, they turned the momentum into this tour. "Endlich auf Reise" is explicitly framed as the final big leg of the *WIR KOMMEN IN FRIEDEN* cycle, hitting cities the band had not yet visited with the album.

The choice of support act is a statement in itself: Dritte Wahl, the veteran Rostock punk band active since 1986, are on the bill at all 14 dates, making this a double package of north-German punk history. Before the tour kicks off in February, the band also have an international detour in October 2026, supporting Die Toten Hosen on three concerts in Argentina plus their own headlining show in Buenos Aires.

## Ticket and presale info

Presale started August 28, 2026 at 6 PM through the band's own ticket shop at TixForGigs, with general sales across all other vendors (Eventim and others) following on September 3, 2026 at noon. Tickets are priced from around €60 (Zurich from about CHF 78). The band also set aside a limited allotment of "Sozialtickets" — reduced-price tickets available only through their own TixForGigs shop, one per person — so fans who can't afford regular prices can still get in. Eventim listings show several dates already unavailable or sold out, so check current availability on the band's official site.
