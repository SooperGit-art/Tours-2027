## Quick answer

**Yes — 42 dates.** Sabaton have two confirmed 2027 runs: Den Stora Sverigeturnén III, a 21-show Swedish arena tour from January 29 to February 27, 2027 with Hulkoff, and the second leg of The Legendary Tour across the UK and Europe from April 7 to May 8, 2027.

**October 2026 check:** all 42 dates below are announced. No North American 2027 shows exist — that leg was rescheduled to late 2026.

## What's confirmed vs. what's still coming

<Callout type="confirmed" title="✅ Confirmed for 2027">
- 21 Swedish arena dates: January 29 – February 27, 2027, billed as The Grand Swedish Arena Tour
- 21 UK/European dates: April 7 – May 8, 2027, as The Legendary Tour part 2
- Support: Hulkoff on the Swedish run; The Legendary Orchestra on the European leg
</Callout>

<Callout type="rumor" title="⚠️ Not yet announced">
- Any further 2027 legs beyond May — nothing announced
- Summer festival appearances for 2027 — not part of this announcement
- North American dates in 2027 — the rescheduled NA run is September–December 2026
</Callout>

## Why this tour matters

Sabaton's 2025 leg of The Legendary Tour drew over 260,000 people across 13 countries with a production the band describes as its biggest arena setup ever — a show built around the 2025 album Legends. The 2027 plan extends that show into two distinct legs: a home-country victory lap (Sabaton's third Grand Swedish Arena Tour) and a second European push reaching 15 countries including first-time Legendary Tour stops in Ireland, Portugal and the Baltic states.

The Swedish run's January/February timing is deliberate winter-touring territory for the band — intimate by their arena standards (rooms like Jönköping's Elmia and Varberg's Arena Varberg) before the bigger spring sheds.

## Ticket and presale info

Swedish dates went on general sale Monday, June 15, 2026 at 10 a.m. CEST via sabaton.net/tour. The European leg's on-sales ran through national partners (Ticketmaster in the UK, local promoters elsewhere) in mid-2026. Many shows — including the smaller Swedish arenas — have limited capacity, so check the band's tour page for availability before resale sites.
