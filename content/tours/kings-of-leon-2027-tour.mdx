## Quick answer

**Yes** — Kings of Leon have officially confirmed two 2027 appearances so far: headlining the Friday night of Electric Avenue festival in Christchurch, New Zealand on 26 February, and a show at London's The O2 on 2 July as part of the venue's 20th birthday concert series. The O2 date is the only UK arena announced on the "O My Beloved" Summer 2027 tour, and two northern England outdoor shows are due to be announced later this month. **October 2026 check:** the NZ festival date and O2 London show are confirmed; the two northern England shows and a rumored Auckland date are still unannounced.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Kings of Leon are entering 2027 with a new record and a clean touring slate. *O My Beloved*, their tenth full-length studio album, arrived on 6 November 2026 — the band's first fully self-produced album, described as their most intimate and unguarded collection yet, reflecting on the people and journey that brought the Followill brothers to where they are today. The album cycle is exactly what a summer 2027 run is built for.

The band's live drawing power is undiminished: their 2024 Hyde Park run ended with a record-breaking fifth night in London, and in New Zealand they hold five number-one albums — which explains the headline billing at Electric Avenue 2027 and the loud fan demand for more down-under dates. The O2 has confirmed that London will be the only UK arena on the O My Beloved Summer 2027 tour, with the two northern England outdoor shows expected to complete the UK picture.

## Ticket and presale info

For the O2 London show on 2 July 2027, presales start Wednesday 7 October 2026 at 9:30am BST — O2 and Virgin Media customers get Priority access, and fans who pre-ordered *O My Beloved* from the UK Kings of Leon shop receive special presale access. General sale follows on Friday 9 October at 9:30am BST. Electric Avenue 2027 tickets are available through the festival's official channels.
