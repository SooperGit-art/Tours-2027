## The tour so far, for context

Paloma Faith's last album, *The Glorification of Sadness*, arrived in February 2024 and she toured it through the UK that spring, including two nights at Manchester's Bridgewater Hall. Her seventh album, *In Full Colour!*, is set for release on March 5, 2027 via RCA, preceded by the single "Gone", released November 13, 2026 — the same day the tour was announced. The album announcement also teased an 11-part TV-inspired visual series to accompany the record.

The October routing is a classic UK theatre-and-arena sweep: opening at Derby's Vaillant Live, working through Bristol, Brighton, Plymouth and Oxford, doubling up at Birmingham Symphony Hall, then a prestige night at the Royal Albert Hall on October 13 before Cardiff, Sheffield, Newcastle, Edinburgh, Glasgow, Bradford, Liverpool and Nottingham, closing at Manchester's O2 Apollo on October 28. The announcement drove "paloma faith tour 2027" up 2,050% on Google Trends, the sharpest single-day rise in today's pull.
