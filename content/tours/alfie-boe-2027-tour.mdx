## The tour so far, for context

Alfie Boe, the British tenor who became a household name through *Les Misérables* and his arena tours with Michael Ball, is taking a deliberately different approach in 2027. *Bohemia* strips the show back to voice and piano, built around a programme of Italian, Spanish and French songs, and plays rooms chosen for acoustics rather than capacity — Snape Maltings' Britten Studio, the Queen's Hall in Edinburgh, Buxton Opera House and St Giles-in-the-Fields in London.

The announcement drove "alfie boe tour 2027" to a 2,300% surge on Google Trends, the sharpest rise in this week's breakout list. The summer dates extend the year outdoors, from Llangollen Pavilion in June to The Piece Hall in Halifax in late July, and the August retreat with Lea Salonga at a South African safari lodge shows how far beyond the concert hall his 2027 calendar reaches.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for the Bohemia dates go on sale at 10am on November 20, 2026, through Gigs and Tours and Ticketmaster. The four summer outdoor shows are already listed on Ticketmaster, with the Llangollen, Lincoln, Scarborough and Halifax dates bookable now. The Spotlight: South Africa retreat with Lea Salonga is a separate five-day ticketed experience running August 7-11, 2027, priced from $9,890 per person for double occupancy, and is booked through the retreat organiser rather than concert ticket sellers.
