## The Love & Fear Tour, for context

Zac Brown Band are touring behind Love & Fear, their 2025 album, and the current leg is a full arena run: fall 2026 dates with Brothers Osborne and Grace Potter run from Sioux Falls on October 24 through Rosemont, Illinois on November 21. The band — three-time Grammy winners with 30 million singles and 9 million albums sold, and a record for the most consecutive sold-out shows at Fenway Park — have headlined eight North American tours, so a 2027 leg would be business as usual rather than a surprise.

The October 2026 search spike for their 2027 plans lines up exactly with the Moon Crush announcement: a brand-new, 4,000-guest destination weekend co-headlined with Lady A, with Colbie Caillat, Drake White, The Castellows and Nashville Yacht Club Band also on the bill. Destination weekends like this are increasingly how veteran headliners fill the gap between arena cycles — and the Milwaukee Summerfest booking shows the band is already routing summer 2027.
