## Quick answer

**Yes** — Montez has officially announced the Arena Tour 2027, with tickets on sale now. The German hip-hop artist plays four confirmed arena dates: Berlin's Uber Arena on September 25, Zurich's Hallenstadion on September 30, Hannover's ZAG Arena on October 7 and Hamburg's Barclays Arena on October 9, plus a Heroes Festival appearance in Freiburg on September 10.

**October 2026 check:** all four arena dates are confirmed and on sale; no additional 2027 dates have been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 arena tour makes sense

Montez represents the new generation of German hip-hop headliners making the jump to full arena scale. Where the genre's arena circuit was once dominated by a handful of established names, artists like Montez are now filling rooms like the Uber Arena and Barclays Arena on their own — a sign of how deep German rap's live audience has become.

The routing is classic German arena strategy: Berlin and Hamburg anchor the north, Hannover catches the mid-sized market, and Zurich captures the Swiss audience that routinely travels for German hip-hop shows. A late-September to early-October window also dodges the summer festival season, when the same audience is outdoors.
