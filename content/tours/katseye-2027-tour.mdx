## Quick answer

**Yes** — KATSEYE are touring in 2027. The group announced an 11-date Asia, Australia and New Zealand leg of the WILDWORLD Tour on October 29, 2026, promoted by Live Nation: it opens January 27 at Yokohama Arena in Japan and closes March 5 at Spark Arena in Auckland, with arena stops in Manila, Hong Kong, Seoul, Cebu, Singapore, Bangkok, Taipei, Melbourne and Sydney in between.

**November 2026 check:** all 11 dates below are officially announced. No further 2027 legs, and no North American or European 2027 dates, have been announced yet.

## What's confirmed vs. what's still coming

- 11 arena dates, January 27 to March 5, 2027, across 8 Asian cities plus Melbourne, Sydney and Auckland
- Seoul date on February 4 at the KSPO Dome — the group's first stand-alone concert in South Korea
- Presales underway from November 4, 2026, with general sales rolling out November 6–13 by market

No lineup changes or additional 2027 legs are confirmed. The group is also scheduled to headline Lollapalooza India in Mumbai on January 24, 2027, just before the Yokohama opener — a festival appearance, not part of the 11-date leg.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

KATSEYE's rise has been fast even by K-pop standards. Formed through HYBE and Geffen Records' Dream Academy project, the six-member group released their debut EP SIS (Soft Is Strong) in 2024 and followed it with Beautiful Chaos in 2025. Their latest EP, WILD, released in August 2026, debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200 — the biggest girl-group debut of the decade — with Billboard Hot 100 hits "PINKY UP" and "Animal".

The WILDWORLD Tour is their first world tour, and demand has outrun supply at every step: the initial dates announced in May sold out within 48 hours. The tour opened at Dublin's 3Arena on September 1, 2026, played Europe and the UK, and is currently working through North America, finishing with two nights at Palacio de los Deportes in Mexico City on November 27 and 28. Extending into Asia, Australia and New Zealand — including a first stand-alone Seoul show — is the natural next step for a group built as a global act from day one.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets are sold through katseye.world and local Ticketmaster sites, with VIP packages via the KATSEYE Weverse Shop. Sale dates vary by market: Australia and New Zealand presales began November 4, 2026, with general sale on November 6; Seoul's general sale is November 12; and most other Asian markets, including Singapore, go on general sale November 13, 2026. Check local listings for exact on-sale times, which vary by market.
