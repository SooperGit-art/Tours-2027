## Quick answer

**Yes** — CHVRCHES are touring in 2027 on a 27-date run across North America, the UK, and Ireland, from February 13 in Philadelphia to May 6 at London's O2 Shepherd's Bush Empire. The tour supports *All The King's Men*, their first album in nearly six years, released January 22, 2027.

**October 2026 check:** all 27 dates are confirmed and announced. The North American leg runs February–March 2027, followed by the UK and Ireland leg in late April and early May.

## The tour so far, for context

This is a comeback tour in the full sense. After 2021's *Screen Violence*, the Glasgow trio took the longest break of their career: Lauren Mayberry released her debut solo album *Vicious Creatures* in December 2024, Martin Doherty launched the rave-leaning project The Leaving with drummer Jonny Scott, and Iain Cook formed Protection with Scott Paterson. The band returned with the single "Roses" and then confirmed *All The King's Men*, recorded in weeks-long sessions back in Glasgow rather than remotely — a deliberate return to writing together in a room.

The routing tells the same story: a North American run through rooms the band has played for a decade (Brooklyn Steel, The Anthem, Hollywood Palladium), then a UK and Ireland leg that ends in London but pointedly starts at home in Glasgow. The band has described the record as its most fearless yet — "the most fearless record" they have made, in Doherty's words — and the tour is built as the reintroduction, not a nostalgia lap.
