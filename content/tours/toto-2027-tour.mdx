## The tour so far, for context

Toto marks nearly fifty years since forming in Los Angeles in 1977, when a group of elite session musicians — including Steve Lukather, David Paich, and the Porcaro brothers — stepped out from behind other artists' records to make their own. The catalog is deep: fourteen studio albums and over 50 million records sold, anchored by the 1982 blockbuster Toto IV, which swept six Grammy Awards including Album of the Year, with "Rosanna" and the perennial "Africa" topping charts and dominating streaming decades later. The band was inducted into the Musicians Hall of Fame and Museum in 2009.

The current era has been relentlessly active. The band toured the Dogz of Oz arena run through Europe in early 2025, played a North American summer tour with Christopher Cross and Men at Work, and wrapped a run of "An Evening With Toto" US shows in early 2026. Guitarist Steve Lukather and frontman Joseph Williams remain the creative core, and the 2027 run adds keyboardist Rai Thistlethwayte to the ensemble.

Japan has long been one of Toto's strongest markets — the band has returned repeatedly over the decades, making it a fitting launchpad for an anniversary tour that began ticket sales just this month.
