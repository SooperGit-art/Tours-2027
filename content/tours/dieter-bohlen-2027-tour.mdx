## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Dieter Bohlen is one of the most successful pop figures in German music history. As the songwriter and producer behind Modern Talking, he was part of five consecutive German number-one singles in 1985 and 1986, and his later project Blue System plus his work as a producer and TV personality have kept him in the public eye for four decades. His show material draws on that deep catalog — promoters describe the 2027 show as a run through his greatest hits and number-one records.

Bohlen has never really left the live stage for long. His most recent run, the "Jetzt oder Nie" tour presented as "The Spirit of Modern Talking," began in September 2025 and was slated to continue into 2027, playing his classic hits with a live band and acoustic elements. The Cheri Cheri Lady Tour 2027 is the next chapter of that momentum, now under a title that directly references his biggest era.

The May 2027 timing also fits his recent pattern of concentrated German arena runs rather than sprawling multi-month treks. With Berlin's venue listing tickets from around 78 euros and premium packages available, the production is clearly pitched at the established fans of his 80s catalog.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for all five 2027 dates are already on sale — there is no waiting for a general on-sale date. For Berlin, the Uber Eats Music Hall sells standard tickets from around 78 to 80 euros, with gallery seating, drinks packages, and front-row upgrades at higher price points. The Oberhausen show is listed through KoelnTicket, and other dates are available via Eventim-affiliated partners.

No separate presale windows have been advertised for this run. Because indoor venues like Sporthalle Hamburg and the Rudolf Weber-Arena have limited capacity, buying early is the safest move if you want a specific show.
