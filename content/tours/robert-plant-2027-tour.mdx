## Quick answer

**Yes** — Robert Plant is touring in 2027. He and Saving Grace announced the Root & Branch tour on November 2, 2026: eight dates in June and July, opening June 19 at London's Hampton Court Palace Festival and closing July 17 at Sigulda Castle in Latvia, with stops in Lincoln, Scarborough, Halifax, Dublin, Ghent, and Uppsala along the way.

**November 2026 check:** the eight dates below are officially announced. No North American dates, and no further legs, have been announced yet.

## The tour so far, for context

Plant formed Saving Grace in 2019 as a vehicle for the roots music he has pursued since his solo career began — a deliberate contrast to the arena scale of Led Zeppelin. The band, built around vocalist Suzi Dian, has toured relentlessly in recent years, and the Root & Branch billing fits the repertoire: songs drawn from folk, blues, and Americana traditions rather than a new studio cycle. Plant was recently honoured with the Lifetime Achievement Award at the Americana Music Association Awards, underlining where his current work sits.

The June routing is a classic English summer run: a palace festival opening, castles and open-air theatres, and a single Irish night in Dublin's Trinity College Summer Series. Kate Rusby and Seth Lakeman support on the Lincoln, Scarborough, and Halifax dates. Notably, the run overlaps Glastonbury weekend — Plant plays June 24, 26, and 27 — but he has no Glastonbury date listed.

## Ticket and presale info

Presales for the UK and Ireland dates run November 4 and 5, 2026, with general sale at 10 a.m. on Friday, November 6, 2026, via Ticketmaster and Planet Rock Tickets. Tickets for the Gent Jazz and Sigulda Castle dates went on sale October 30, 2026.
