**Yes** — New Order are touring the US in 2027. The band announced their first USA dates for 2027 on November 9, 2026: a seven-date run from March 5 to March 21, 2027, opening at Austin's Moody Center and closing with two nights at The Fillmore Miami Beach. Future Islands support the three Texas dates. **November 2026 check:** the artist presale opens today, November 10, at 10:00 a.m. local time (password Mojito27), with general sale on Friday, November 13.

## The tour so far, for context

New Order formed in Manchester in 1980 from the ashes of Joy Division, and over four decades became one of the defining bands of new wave and alternative dance, from "Blue Monday" — still the best-selling 12-inch single of all time — through "Bizarre Love Triangle" and "True Faith". Their last full US tour was in 2023; since then they have played America only at festivals, including Cruel World and Kilby Block Party in 2025.

The March 2027 run comes at a significant moment for the band: the announcement landed days before their induction into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in Los Angeles on November 14. It will also be a different-looking New Order onstage — longtime drummer Stephen Morris and keyboardist-guitarist Gillian Gilbert are sitting out the run while Gilbert undergoes treatment for cancer, with touring musicians filling in. Before the US dates, the band head to South America for six shows across Brazil, Chile, Argentina, Peru and Colombia, starting November 23, 2026 in São Paulo.
