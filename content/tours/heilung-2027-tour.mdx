## Quick answer

**Yes** — Heilung are touring in 2027, on their biggest run yet: the ELDGEITHFARDHA World Tour, 25 dates in two legs. First, seven shows in Australia and New Zealand in March, opening with two nights at the Sydney Opera House (March 1–2) and closing in Auckland on March 16. Then, after a two-year break from European stages, an 18-date European arena tour from Copenhagen on November 10 to Hamburg on December 16, including London's OVO Arena Wembley on December 8.

**November 2026 check:** all 25 dates are confirmed — the European leg was announced November 25, 2026, with general sale November 27. No further legs have been announced yet.

## The tour so far, for context

Heilung call their work "amplified history": a ritual collective that rebuilds Iron Age and Viking Age sound with reconstructed instruments, throat singing and chants in old Norse and other early languages. A Heilung show is staged as a ceremony rather than a concert — performers in ritual dress, bone and hide instruments, fire and smoke — and the following it built through the LIFA live recordings turned a niche experimental project into an arena act.

That is what makes the 2027 routing notable. The March leg takes the ritual to Australia and New Zealand for the first time at this scale, including two nights at the Sydney Opera House. The European leg, announced November 25, 2026 — the announcement behind the "heilung tour 2027" Trends breakout — is the band's return to European arenas after a two-year break, and the largest tour they have ever staged: 18 cities in five weeks, from Copenhagen's Royal Arena to Hamburg's Sporthalle, under the title ELDGEITHFARDHA, "The Journey of the Fire Goat".

## Ticket and presale info

European presales run from November 26, 2026, with general sale on Friday, November 27, through local promoters and Ticketmaster. The Australia and New Zealand dates are on sale now through Destroy All Lines, Moshtix and Ticketmaster. Note that heilung.com is not the band's website — it belongs to an unrelated German online-courses company — so buy only through the promoters and ticket sellers listed in the tour announcement. The London Wembley date and the two Sydney Opera House nights are the likeliest to sell out first.
