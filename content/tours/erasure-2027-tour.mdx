## Quick answer

**Yes** — Erasure have officially announced the Radio!! Radio!! Tour for 2027: 20 dates from April 25 to May 28, 2027, across the UK, Ireland, Germany, Denmark and Sweden. The tour supports their twentieth studio album, *Radio!! Radio!!*, released March 12, 2027. Ladytron open all twelve UK and Ireland dates, and in a notable twist, Vince Clarke will serve as musical director but will not perform onstage.

**October 2026 check:** All 20 dates are confirmed and on general sale since October 2, 2026. No US or additional European dates had been announced.

## The tour so far, for context

2026 marked forty years since Erasure's debut album, and the duo have chosen to celebrate by pushing forward rather than looking back. *Radio!! Radio!!* is their twentieth studio album, described by Andy Bell and Vince Clarke as a reflective, personal record about love, loss and injustice. Clarke has called the songwriting partnership "a sacred space," and the album transposes the duo's trademark hugeness onto more intimate material.

The tour setup carries a genuinely unusual story: Clarke, who moved to California, told Bell that touring had taken too much of a toll — "I never seemed to recover from it. I just thought 'I don't want to do this anymore.'" Bell's solution was to make him musical director: "He's always too modest but he's so much more than the musical director... everything we do as Erasure we decide together." The result is an Erasure tour unlike any before it, with Bell carrying the stage while Clarke directs the music from California.
