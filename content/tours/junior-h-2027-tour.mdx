## Quick answer

**Yes** — Junior H is touring in 2027. His Estadio Tour is confirmed, with 12 stadium dates across Mexico from January 8 to February 13, 2027, opening at Estadio Jalisco in Guadalajara and closing at Walmart Park in Monterrey. Mexico City gets two nights, January 30 and 31, at Estadio Ciudad de los Deportes. **November 2026 check:** all 12 dates are listed on Bandsintown and corroborated by multiple concert listings; no dates outside Mexico have been announced.

## Why a stadium tour makes sense in 2027

Junior H, born Antonio Herrera Pérez in Guanajuato, is one of the progenitors of corridos tumbados — the trap-inflected take on regional Mexican music he helped define alongside Natanael Cano, appearing on Cano's landmark 2019 album *Corridos Tumbados*. He recorded his debut album, *Mi Vida en un Cigarro*, as a teenager in 2019, signed to Rancho Humilde, and built a following on sad sierreño ballads that made him one of the genre's defining voices.

The step up to stadiums follows a heavy 2026: a collaborative album with longtime writing partner Gael Valenzuela, released in February 2026, debuted at No. 1 on Billboard's Top Regional Mexican Albums chart, and he toured Latin America and Spain through the year. A Mexico-only stadium run is the natural next move — his core audience is at home, and the venues, from baseball stadiums in Puebla and Monterrey to soccer grounds in Guadalajara and Tijuana, match the scale his streaming numbers now support.
