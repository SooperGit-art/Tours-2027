## Quick answer

**Yes** — Hayley Williams is touring in 2027. Her solo run, The Hayley Williams Show, began September 3, 2026 in West Palm Beach and ends July 3, 2027 in London. The 2027 leg covers Mexico in January, Australia in February, and a European run through June and July, with 14 headline dates listed below.

**November 2026 check:** all dates below are on sale through Ticketmaster. No US dates are listed for 2027 — the North American leg ran in fall 2026.

## The tour so far, for context

The Hayley Williams Show is the former Paramore frontwoman's solo tour, running separately from the band — Paramore have announced no 2027 dates of their own. The tour opened in the US in September 2026, played Mexico in November 2026, and resumes in January 2027 with a return to Mexico City's Auditorio Nacional plus Monterrey and Guadalajara. February takes her to Australia for headline shows in Sydney and Melbourne around Laneway Festival appearances, then the tour pauses until June, when a European run opens in Milan and Rome, crosses Brussels, Paris and Amsterdam, and closes with the UK dates in Glasgow, Cardiff and two nights at Alexandra Palace.
