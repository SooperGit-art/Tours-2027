## Quick answer

**Yes** — the Disney Worlds Collide Concert Tour is officially set for February 2027: 11 arena shows across 10 cities in 7 countries, announced on June 9, 2026 by Disney Concerts and AEG Presents. The run opens February 6 at 3Arena in Dublin and closes February 24 at Movistar Arena in Madrid, with two nights at The O2 in London. **October 2026 check:** no additional 2027 dates have been announced beyond these 11 shows.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

The 2027 run is built on a genuine success story. Last year's "Descendants/ZOMBIES: Worlds Collide Tour" was a North American breakthrough: sold-out crowds in over 40 arenas, named Top Family Tour of 2025 by Billboard magazine and nominated for the same honor by Pollstar. It also spawned a Disney+ Concert Special and the "Descendants/ZOMBIES: Worlds Collide Tour – The Live Album."

Momentum never stopped. Disney Concerts announced a second North American leg beginning September 25, 2026, and the 2027 U.K./European run marks the production's first time leaving North America — 10 cities and 7 countries in just 19 days, from Dublin to Madrid.

The 2027 edition is a reimagined production rather than a repeat: Camp Rock joins the Descendants/ZOMBIES lineup for the first time, and the cast spans all three franchises — Malachi Barton ("ZOMBIES 4," "Camp Rock 3"), Liamani Segura ("Descendants: Wicked Wonderland," "Camp Rock 3"), Dara Reneé ("Descendants: The Rise of Red"), Mekonnen Knife and Swayam Bhatia ("ZOMBIES 4"), Hudson Stone ("Camp Rock 3"), plus Kiara Romero and Alexandro Byrd ("Descendants: Wicked Wonderland").

## Ticket and presale info

The general sale began Friday, June 12, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time through the official tour site. VIP packages — including cast Q&A opportunities — were offered via AEG Presents alongside standard tickets. As with most AEG-promoted arena shows, tickets are also available through Ticketmaster and the individual venue box offices (The O2, AO Arena, Accor Arena, Ziggo Dome and others). Prices vary by market, and Disney has not announced any additional presale waves — the official tour site is the place to watch for added shows.
