## Quick answer

**Yes — Oasis is touring in 2027.** Oasis Live '27 was officially announced in September 2026: 43 dates across the UK, Ireland, Europe, and the US, running May 21 through September 26, 2027. Richard Ashcroft is the special guest across the tour.

This page was rebuilt from scratch when the announcement landed — it previously tracked the rumor phase, including Liam Gallagher's on-record "maybe" and the fabricated "Etihad residency" claims that circulated before anything was official. The real thing is now here, and it's bigger than the rumors suggested: eleven nights at Manchester's Etihad Stadium alone.

## The announcement

The tour was announced around September 15, 2026, weeks after the premiere of the reunion documentary *Oasis: Don't Look Back in Anger*. It follows the Live '25 reunion run — 41 shows that grossed a reported $525 million — and extends the reunion well beyond what most fans expected. The routing reads like a victory lap: an eleven-night hometown stand at Manchester's Etihad Stadium, a return to Knebworth 31 years after the defining 1996 shows, and a first-ever Irish castle double-header at Slane.

## Ticketing — read this before anything else

There is **no general on-sale** for Oasis Live '27. The only way to buy tickets is the official registration process:

- Fan registration closed **September 17, 2026** — including for existing mailing-list members
- Successful applicants receive a unique, non-transferable code by email for an allocated on-sale window (US windows ran September 25, 2026)
- Standard ticket prices are fixed from $122.65 to $541.05 including fees and taxes
- Resale is only legitimate through Ticketmaster or Twickets (UK), at the original price paid — anything above face value is not authorized

If you missed registration, the resale channels above are the only safe route. Do not buy from third-party sites listing "guaranteed" Oasis 2027 tickets.

## Knebworth, in context

The six September nights at Knebworth aren't just tour dates — they're a deliberate callback. Oasis's two 1996 Knebworth shows drew 250,000 people and became the defining moment of Britpop. Returning in 2027, thirty-one years later, for six nights is the band planting a flag: this is where the story is being closed out, at least for this chapter. Expect these to be the hardest tickets of the entire run.
