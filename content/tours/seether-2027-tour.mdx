## Quick answer

**Yes** — Seether will tour in 2027 as a special guest on the second leg of Staind's Break the Cycle 25th Anniversary Tour, playing 27 North American arena dates from March 4 in Manchester, NH through April 16 in Cleveland, OH. Seether appears at every stop, with Hoobastank also on the full run and Hinder joining all dates except the Cleveland finale. The leg was announced on October 6, 2026, produced by Live Nation, with the general on-sale following on October 9, 2026.

**October 2026 check:** the 27-date itinerary is confirmed and on sale. Seether has not announced a separate headlining 2027 tour of its own.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Seether, the South African rock band formed in Pretoria in 1999, has spent 2025 and 2026 on a heavy touring schedule. They co-headlined a US amphitheater run with Daughtry in fall 2025, then toured behind their ninth studio album, The Surface Seems So Far (released 2024). With nine studio albums and a catalog that includes chart-toppers like "Remedy," "Fake It," "Country Song," and "Tonight," the band remains one of active rock's most reliable draws — Billboard's rankings have placed them eighth on the all-time Mainstream Rock chart.

The Staind pairing isn't new: Seether opened the first leg of the Break the Cycle 25th Anniversary Tour in October 2026 alongside Hoobastank and Hinder, and that leg sold out. The second leg extends the same package to new cities across the US plus three Canadian arena stops in Vancouver, Calgary, and Edmonton. Expect sets leaning on the big radio singles alongside newer material from The Surface Seems So Far.
