## Quick answer

**Yes** — Vanessa Williams is touring in 2027, and it is a first: her first full UK concert tour, nine dates from February 18 to 28, 2027. Announced in August 2026, the run opens at Glasgow's Royal Concert Hall and closes at London's Dominion Theatre, where she recently finished her West End run as Miranda Priestly in *The Devil Wears Prada*.

**November 2026 check:** all nine UK dates are confirmed and on sale. Three US one-off concerts are also booked around the tour (Walnut Creek and Las Vegas in January, Englewood in April), but no full US tour has been announced.

## The tour so far, for context

Vanessa Williams has had one of the most varied careers in entertainment — Miss America 1984, a string of pop hits led by the 1992 No. 1 "Save the Best for Last", acclaimed Broadway roles, and television work — but a full concert tour of the UK is something she had never done until now. Announced in August 2026, the February 2027 run is built around her two worlds: her chart hits and her musical-theatre favourites.

The timing is no accident. Williams spent a year-long West End residency playing fashion editor Miranda Priestly in the stage adaptation of *The Devil Wears Prada* at the Dominion Theatre, finishing her run in October 2026 and handing the role to Teri Hatcher. The tour finale on February 28, 2027, brings her back to that same Dominion stage — this time as herself, in concert. The breakout in "Vanessa Williams tour 2027" searches in November 2026 follows renewed coverage of the tour as the February dates approach.
