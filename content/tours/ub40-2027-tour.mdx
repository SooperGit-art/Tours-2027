## Quick answer

**Yes** — UB40 are touring in 2027 on the Unstoppable World Tour, named after their new studio album *Unstoppable*, released April 23, 2027. The run opens with a one-night-only London Palladium show on April 23, continues through Australia and New Zealand in May, and closes with European festival dates in France and Switzerland in July.

**November 2026 check:** 10 dates are confirmed. More dates could still be added, particularly in the UK and Europe.

## The tour so far, for context

UB40 formed in Birmingham in 1978, and the Unstoppable World Tour marks a milestone year: the band's new album *Unstoppable* arrives April 23, 2027, nearly five decades after they started, following their 45th-anniversary album *UB45*, which reached No. 5 on the UK Official Albums Chart. The London Palladium show is billed as a one-night-only album launch, with pre-ordering the album granting access to an exclusive ticket presale.

The routing is classic UB40: Australia and New Zealand in May, where the band have toured heavily for decades, then European summer festivals in July — Pause Guitare in Albi, Moon and Stars at Locarno's Piazza Grande, and a headline night at the Zénith de Toulon. The set is expected to mix the new material with the hits — "Red Red Wine," "Kingston Town" and "Can't Help Falling in Love" — plus reworked classics from *UB45*.

## Ticket and presale info

For the London Palladium show, album pre-orders gave access to an exclusive ticket presale, with remaining tickets on general sale from Tuesday, December 1, 2026.
