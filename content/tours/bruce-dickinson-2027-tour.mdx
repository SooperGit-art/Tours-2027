## Quick answer

**Yes** — Bruce Dickinson has announced a full solo tour for 2027. The PsychoGod Tour plays 28 confirmed headline dates across the UK and Europe, opening June 14, 2027 at Ulster Hall in Belfast and closing December 10, 2027 at L'Olympia in Paris. The announcement, made October 27, 2026, also includes three July festival appearances whose exact performance dates are still to be announced. **October 2026 check:** the headline dates are confirmed and general on-sale is October 30, 2026; only the festival performance dates remain unannounced.

## What's confirmed vs. what's still coming

<Callout type="confirmed" title="✅ Confirmed for 2027">
- PsychoGod Tour announced October 27, 2026, with 28 headline dates in the UK and Europe.
- Summer leg: June 14–29, 2027 (Belfast, Dublin, Milan, Zurich, Zagreb, Belgrade, Sofia, Istanbul) plus July 7 in Luxembourg City.
- Autumn leg with Evergrey: November 10 – December 10, 2027, from Warsaw to Paris.
- General on-sale Friday, October 30, 2026 via brucedickinsonhq.com.

<Callout type="rumor" title="⚠️ Not yet announced">

- No North American or other non-European dates have been announced for this tour.
</Callout>

## The tour so far, for context

These are Dickinson's first solo outings since The Mandrake Project Tour wrapped up in Brazil in September 2025. The PsychoGod Tour promises a mix of new material alongside fan favorites and deep cuts spanning his entire solo career. His own comment on the announcement drew the line sharply: what his band does is real — a real drummer, bass player, guitarists and keyboards — and, in his words, AI can only copy and pretend, never play.

The routing tells the story of a working European tour rather than a handful of festival cameos. The summer leg moves from Belfast and Dublin through Milan, Zurich and the Balkans to Istanbul, then the autumn leg — with Swedish progressive metal band Evergrey in support — works steadily north and west from Warsaw through Helsinki, Stockholm, Oslo and Copenhagen, across Germany and the Netherlands, and through the UK before finishing in France.

## Ticket and presale info

General on-sale is Friday, October 30, 2026 via brucedickinsonhq.com. Presales began October 28 in some markets, including the UK listings, so fans who missed a presale window should go straight to the general sale. Ticket prices and VIP packages have not been detailed in the announcement coverage so far; the official site is the place to check for those.
