## Quick answer

**Yes** — Saxon are touring hard in 2027 behind their 25th studio album, Gods of Thunder (out January 22, 2027). The Gods of Thunder World Tour opens with an 11-date UK and Ireland leg in February, with Armored Saint supporting, then continues with a 17-date European leg in March — 28 dates in total, from Wolverhampton on February 13 to Tilburg on March 24. **October 2026 check:** both legs are confirmed; UK and Ireland tickets are on sale, and European tickets go on sale in stages from October 27–30, 2026.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Saxon are one of the New Wave of British Heavy Metal's last bands standing, and they have never slowed down. Frontman Biff Byford produced the new album himself, with Judas Priest guitarist Andy Sneap mixing — a pairing that underlines the band's standing among metal's old guard. Gods of Thunder is the band's 25th studio album, and the tour's staging leans into history: the legendary 30-foot mechanised Eagle, built in the early 1980s by Midlands-based LSD using airport runway landing lights for the Denim and Leather era, flies again for the UK and Ireland shows and at Brussels, Zurich, Cologne and Tilburg.

The routing is classic Saxon: a full UK and Ireland sweep in February, then a fast, dense European club-and-theatre run in March, from Brussels to Tilburg via Scandinavia, Central Europe and Italy. For a band formed in Barnsley in the late 1970s, a 28-date start to 2027 is business as usual — and the Eagle's return makes it an event, not just a tour.
