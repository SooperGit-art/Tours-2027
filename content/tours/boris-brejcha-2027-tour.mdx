## The tour so far, for context

Boris Brejcha has spent nearly two decades building one of electronic music's most distinctive identities: the German DJ and producer's self-described High-Tech Minimal sound, his FCKNG SERIOUS label (founded with Ann Clue and Deniz Bul), and the Joker mask he performs in. His 2026 calendar ran through clubs and festivals on multiple continents — from Amsterdam and Budapest to Miami, Toronto, and Bali — and the 2027 dates continue that festival-headliner pattern rather than a traditional tour.

The Australian run is the centerpiece: a return headlining the Dangerous Goods XXL Festival Tour after his earlier appearance at the event, with four dates across Perth, Sydney, Brisbane, and Melbourne in a single January week. The Montreal and Miami dates bracket it as the year's early festival anchors, and his European summer festival season — historically his busiest stretch — has yet to be announced.
