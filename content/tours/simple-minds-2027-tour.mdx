## Quick answer

**Yes** — Simple Minds have officially announced the **V Tour 2027**, a full arena tour celebrating 50 years since the band formed in Glasgow in 1977. The run covers 37 announced dates from 13 February to 17 May 2027: eight shows across Australia and New Zealand, 20 across mainland Europe, and nine in the UK and Ireland, with Gary Numan as special guest on the UK and Australia/NZ legs. **October 2026 check:** all announced dates are official and on sale; no North or South American dates have been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Simple Minds have spent the last few years as one of the hardest-touring legacy acts in rock. Their 2025 touring reached more than 1.5 million people, including a North American run that frontman Jim Kerr and guitarist Charlie Burchill say reconnected them with the art-rock and new-wave charge of early material like *Sons And Fascination* — energy that fed directly into the new record. The band has sold more than 60 million records worldwide, scored five UK No.1 albums, and taken 'Don't You (Forget About Me)' past one billion Spotify streams.

The new album *Sacrosanct* is their first studio record since 2022's *Direction Of The Heart*, and its lead single 'Beginner Boys' draws its subject from Kerr watching his twin grandsons grow up. The album also turns its attention to the Kerr–Burchill friendship on 'Our Secrets Are The Same' — a bond that began on a Glasgow street when the pair were eight years old, and which now anchors a 50-year career.
