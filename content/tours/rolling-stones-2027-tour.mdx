The Rolling Stones' last run was the Hackney Diamonds '24 Tour, a 20-date North American trek that closed on July 21, 2024, in Ridgedale, Missouri. Since then the band has been in studio mode: their 25th studio album *Foreign Tongues*, produced by Andrew Watt, arrived on July 10, 2026, and became their 15th UK number-one album — proof the band still has a hungry audience.

Jagger's July 2026 La Nacion interview is the strongest signal: he talked about how many of the new songs would work on stage, said touring was unlikely in 2026, and expressed his hope to tour in 2027. Richards echoed a similar timeline to Billboard that summer, saying the band decided to "get the record out" first and that he saw "no reason why next year" shouldn't work — though his version leaned toward residencies or one-off shows rather than a full tour.

Then came the cold water. Wood's October 2026 NME interview laid the split bare: he and Jagger want to play, Richards doesn't, and Wood has "no plans yet" — though he'd play Glastonbury 2027 "in the drop of a hat" if the band agreed. For now, Wood is touring his own Ammo band. The tension is familiar: the band called off its 2026 European stadium run reportedly over Richards' reluctance to commit to a four-month schedule, with the 82-year-old guitarist managing arthritis.
