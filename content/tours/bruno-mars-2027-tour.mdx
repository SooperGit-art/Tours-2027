## Quick answer

**Yes** — Bruno Mars will tour in 2027. His first headlining tour in nearly a decade, The Romantic Tour, officially extends into 2027 with 40 announced stadium dates: 12 in Japan starting January 4, 10 across Australia and New Zealand in February and March, and 18 more across Singapore, Thailand, Taiwan, Hong Kong, the Philippines and South Korea through May 26. Anderson .Paak joins every show as DJ Pee .Wee, and Tyla is the special guest in Australia and New Zealand. **October 2026 check:** all announced 2027 dates are on sale; no US, Canadian or European 2027 dates have been announced.

## The tour so far, for context

The Romantic Tour launched in April 2026 with a two-night stand at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas, Bruno Mars's first headline tour in nearly a decade. It is his first stadium-scale trek since the 24K Magic era, built around his fourth solo album, *The Romantic*, which features singles "I Just Might" and "Risk It All."

Demand has been historic. The January 2026 onsale delivered the largest single-day ticket sales in Live Nation history across North America, Europe and the UK, and set a Ticketmaster record with 2.1 million tickets sold in a single day. Extraordinary demand prompted the expansion that pushed the tour past 115 stadium concerts worldwide, including 14 sold-out stadium shows across five Brazilian cities in 2026 and a record-setting run of seven consecutive Tokyo Dome sellouts back in 2024.

The August 26, 2026 announcement added 26 dates and took the tour into 2027 for the first time, with markets in Japan, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Thailand, Taiwan, Hong Kong, the Philippines and South Korea. Extra shows were added September 2 in Melbourne, Brisbane and Auckland after the initial allocation sold through. Notably, the 2027 announcement included no North American dates — a normal routing choice for a tour that had already worked its way through the US, Canada and Mexico across 2026.

## Ticket and presale info

For the Australia and New Zealand dates, an artist presale ran September 2, 2026 (sign-up required via Ticketmaster by August 31), with general onsale September 4 via Ticketmaster. For the Asian dates, artist presale sign-ups ran through brunomars.com until September 7, with presale and general onsale times varying by market. Bangkok tickets ranged from THB 2,800 to THB 9,300 and were mobile-only through Ticketmaster — no physical tickets or ThaiTicketMajor outlets were involved.

With every date now past its initial onsale, check the official ticket links on brunomars.com for your city before buying anywhere else. Be wary of resale screenshots and unverified "VIP" packages: several markets used mobile-only ticketing, and third-party offers outside the official platforms have been a known source of scams on this tour.
