## Quick answer

**Yes** — Marc Anthony is touring North America in spring 2027. The Last Dance World Tour is a 14-show arena run announced November 9, 2026, opening April 1 at Scotiabank Arena in Toronto and closing May 16 at the Chase Center in San Francisco. **November 2026 check:** presale ran November 12 and general on-sale opened November 13, 2026, so tickets are on sale now via Ticketmaster.

## The tour so far, for context

Marc Anthony is the top-selling salsa artist of all time, with four Grammy Awards, eight Latin Grammy Awards and 29 Lo Nuestro Awards, and more than 12 million albums sold worldwide. He announced the tour himself on X in partnership with promoter CMN Events, writing: "Mi gente, I invite you to be part of this US tour so we can celebrate like never before." The announcement drove "marc anthony tour 2027" up 190% on Google Trends.

The routing is an arena run through the biggest rooms in North America — Toronto, Montreal, Brooklyn, Chicago, Houston, Denver and San Francisco — and it runs alongside his Las Vegas residency rather than replacing it. Vegas... My Way! opened at Fontainebleau Las Vegas in September 2026 and returns for five February 2027 dates and five more in September 2027, so fans have two very different ways to see him next year: the big arena tour in spring, or the residency in Las Vegas.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets are available through Ticketmaster. A presale began Thursday, November 12, 2026, at 9am local time, with general on-sale opening Friday, November 13, 2026, at 10am local time. Fans could also register for early access through CMN Events' official website. The arena shows are single-night stops, so the largest rooms — Toronto, Brooklyn, Chicago, San Francisco — are the ones to book early.
