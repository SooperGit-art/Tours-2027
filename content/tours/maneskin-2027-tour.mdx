## Quick answer

**No** — Måneskin has no 2027 tour announced. As of November 12, 2026, no tour dates exist. What does exist is a reunion: after pausing group activities in 2024, the Eurovision 2021 winners return to the stage together in 2027, as special guests at the Sanremo Music Festival in February and on the Coachella bill on April 10 and 17. **November 2026 check:** both festival appearances are confirmed bookings, not a tour, so this page stays rumored until the band announces a standalone run.

The reunion is real, but it is a festival reunion, not a tour. Måneskin won Eurovision 2021 and became one of the biggest rock bands in the world, then paused in early 2024 so Damiano David, Victoria De Angelis, Thomas Raggi and Ethan Torchio could pursue solo projects. The Coachella 2027 booking, announced November 10, 2026, is their first official commitment since then — two Saturday sets on the days headlined by Tame Impala, back on the desert stage where they played in 2022.

## Why a 2027 tour is expected

A band does not reunite for Coachella and Sanremo without the tour question following immediately, and the "maneskin tour 2027" searches surging 600% this week show fans are already asking it. The Sanremo return carries extra weight in Italy, where the festival is the band's home stage, and a February Sanremo appearance followed by April Coachella dates is exactly the shape of a comeback being staged in public before a tour is announced.

For now, though, the honest position is that nothing beyond the two festivals exists. No album has been announced, no routing has leaked from a credible source, and the band's team has said nothing about dates after April 17. The reunion is confirmed; the tour is not.
