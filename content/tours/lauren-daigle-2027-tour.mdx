Daigle's last full tour was the Kaleidoscope Tour in 2024, which covered the US from February through September plus select dates in December. Since then she has kept a lighter live schedule built around the annual Behold Christmas Tour, now in its tenth year. The November 2026 move to Interscope Capitol — a major-label signing announced alongside new Christmas recordings — is the kind of reset that usually precedes a new album cycle, and a new album cycle is what produces a tour. None of that has been announced yet, which is why this page stays rumored.

## The fake 2027 tour site, debunked

A site at laurendaigletour2027.us presents itself as the "Official Site" for a Lauren Daigle Tour 2027, describing an extensive "Revelations Light of the World" world tour across North America, Europe and Australia, with stops at Madison Square Garden, the Hollywood Bowl, the Sydney Opera House and Paris's La Seine Musicale. None of it is real. The site's own text admits its last update was December 2023 and that it has no access to real-time data — it says plainly it is creating "an educated guess based on her touring patterns". There is no tour by that name, no dates, and no ticket sales through any legitimate seller. If you see those dates shared on social media, they trace back to this page.
