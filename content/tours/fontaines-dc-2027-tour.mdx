**Yes** — Fontaines D.C. have officially announced a full 2027 touring calendar: 29 dates spanning April to October 2027, including their biggest UK headline shows to date at stadiums and parks (London Stadium, St James' Park, Heaton Park), a 19-date North American tour in two legs, and five European shows in Italy and Germany. All dates support their fifth album, *Dopamine Chamber*, released 16 October 2026. **October 2026 check:** all 29 dates are confirmed; the two expected Slane Castle shows in Ireland and any further legs are still unannounced.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Fontaines D.C. are coming off the biggest two years of their career. Their 2024 album *Romance* reached No.2 on the UK Albums Chart and became 2024's best-selling vinyl album by an international group, and the band took home the BRIT Award for International Group of the Year in 2025. Live, they just headlined Reading & Leeds 2026, drew 45,000 people to Finsbury Park in summer 2026, and sold out their entire November 2026 UK arena tour on the morning the 2027 stadium dates were announced — a run of milestones that explains the step up to outdoor stadiums and parks.

The 2027 dates arrive alongside their fifth album, *Dopamine Chamber*, produced by James Ford and led by the singles "Marianne" and "Tongue". The album continues the band's move away from the guitar language of their early post-punk records toward something more atmospheric, and the tour routing — theatres and civic halls in North America, stadium-scale outdoor shows in the UK — is built to showcase that evolution at two very different scales.
