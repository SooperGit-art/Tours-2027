## Quick answer

**Yes** — Staind's 2027 tour is officially confirmed. On October 6, 2026, the band announced a second North American leg of the Break The Cycle 25th Anniversary Tour: 27 dates from March 4 to April 16, 2027, hitting arenas and amphitheaters across the US and Canada with Seether, Hoobastank, and Hinder supporting. **October 2026 check:** every announced date is real and on sale — no additional 2027 legs have been revealed yet.

## Why the anniversary tour keeps rolling

Staind's 2001 album Break The Cycle went seven-times platinum in the US and defined their career — five consecutive albums debuted in the Billboard 200 Top 5, with three straight No. 1 entries. The 2026 anniversary campaign kicked off with the live album Break the Cycle (Live) and a first touring leg that ran through the year, including a front-to-back album performance at Mohegan Sun Arena.

The comeback has real momentum: 2026 gave the band its biggest streaming year yet, with over a billion annual streams fueled by a TikTok resurgence of the deep cut "Warm Safe Place." Pollstar reported the current tour grossed over $11 million across 14 headline shows, averaging roughly 17,000 tickets per night — arena-filling numbers that explain why a second leg was extended into 2027.

Frontman Aaron Lewis, guitarist Mike Mushok, bassist Johnny April, and drummer Jon Wysocki are celebrating the catalog that sold over 15 million records worldwide, and the 2027 leg packages them with a touring bill of fellow early-2000s rock staples.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets went on sale the week of the announcement. The presale structure: a Spotify Reserved presale (for eligible Spotify Premium top fans, via Ticketmaster), an artist presale, and a Citi cardmember presale all began October 7, 2026, with general on-sale starting October 9, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time. Given the per-show averages reported on the 2026 leg, early dates could sell briskly — check the official site and Ticketmaster's Staind page for remaining inventory.
