## Quick answer

**Yes** — the Broilers are touring in 2027, and it is a big one: the 11-date Banda Live tour, announced November 25, 2026, running July 29 to September 25, 2027, from the Kasematten Open Air in Graz to the Olympiahalle in Munich. The tour supports the band's new studio album *Banda*, released July 23, 2027 — their first album since 2021's *Puro Amor* — and moves from open-air stages in July and August into arenas in September.

**November 2026 check:** all 11 dates are confirmed, with presale starting November 27, 2026 at 12:00 CET. No further dates have been announced yet.

## The tour so far, for context

The Broilers are Düsseldorf punk rock, formed in 1992, and one of Germany's biggest rock draws — the Waldbühne in Berlin and the Olympiahalle in Munich are the kind of rooms they now close tours in. *Banda* ends a six-year gap since *Puro Amor* (2021), and the band are treating the release as an event: the album arrives July 23, 2027, and the tour starts six days later in Graz, carrying the new songs straight onto the biggest stages of their career.

One thing to get straight: search results for "Broilers tour 2027" mix up three different acts. Broiler (no "s") are a Norwegian electronic duo playing Oslo in February 2027, and Bros are Matt and Luke Goss, touring the UK in November and December 2027. None of those dates belong to the Broilers — the 11 dates on this page are the German band's announced routing.

## Ticket and presale info

Presale for the Banda Live tour begins Friday, November 27, 2026, at 12:00 CET via Eventim, with album pre-orders opening at the same time. The band reserves a contingent of Sozialtickets — €19.90 in Germany and Austria, €29.00 in Switzerland — for fans who could not otherwise afford a ticket; eligibility details are on broilers.de. Buy only from Eventim or the band's official channels.
