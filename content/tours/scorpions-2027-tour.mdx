## Quick answer

**Yes** — Scorpions are touring in 2027. The German hard rock legends announced the Love, Peace & Rock 'N' Roll Tour on November 2, 2026: 35 dates from April 29 in Barcelona to September 25 in Las Vegas, with Europe as special guests on the European leg and a rescheduled eight-show Coming Home to Las Vegas residency closing the run.

**November 2026 check:** the full itinerary below is officially announced. No US dates beyond Las Vegas, and no dates in Asia or South America, have been announced yet.

- Rock Zone Community presale from November 4, 2026, 11 a.m. CET; general sale November 6, 2026

Beyond the announced run, nothing else is confirmed. The band has not announced any further US dates, festival appearances beyond the Oslo Tons of Rock stop, or a second leg.

## The tour so far, for context

Scorpions' 2027 run is a comeback shaped by a disrupted 2026. That September, the band postponed all of its remaining 2026 dates, including an eight-date Las Vegas residency, citing unforeseen circumstances, and also cancelled a planned April 2026 India tour. The 2027 announcement effectively rebuilds that calendar: the postponed Vegas residency moves to September 2027, and the band adds a full European summer run plus three previously announced Mexico dates in Guadalajara, Mexico City, and Monterrey.

The routing leans on the band's strongest markets. Germany gets four arena shows, the UK and Ireland get four, and the itinerary mixes big arenas — London's O2, Paris' AccorHotels Arena, Milan's Unipol Arena — with distinctive stops such as the Dalhalla open-air stage in Rättvik, Sweden, and the Tons of Rock festival in Oslo. Europe as special guests gives the European leg a co-headliner feel for fans of classic hard rock.

## Ticket and presale info

Rock Zone Community members get first access to tickets starting Wednesday, November 4, 2026, at 11 a.m. CET via The-ScorpionsCommunity.com. General sale begins Friday, November 6, 2026, via the band's official tour page and Ticketmaster. The London show also has a separate Live Nation presale on November 5. Prices and VIP packages had not been published at announcement time.
