## The tour so far, for context

Roy Bianco & Die Abbrunzati Boys are one of the German live scene's most beloved cult acts. Formed in Augsburg in 2016, they play Italian-flavoured schlager and pop while frontman Roy Bianco performs as a fictional veteran of a 1980s Italo-pop career that never existed — an affectionate spoof complete with roses handed out at the end of every show. Their albums include *Greatest Hits*, *Mille Grazie* and *KULT*, with songs like "Brennerautobahn" and "Bella Napoli".

The Mille Grazie Tour took them through concert halls across Germany, Austria and Switzerland, and the KULT Tour 2024 played the really big stages, including Rock am Ring and Rock im Park. Since then the band has been quiet on the arena front for nearly two years — and fan forums have been buzzing with speculation about a new-album tour for autumn 2027. The Zurich announcement, which broke in October 2026, looks like the first domino.
