**Yes** — Hans Zimmer is touring in 2027. The Academy Award-winning composer officially announced **Hans Zimmer Live – The Next Level**, a 30-date North American arena tour with his 19-piece band, running **February 17 to April 11, 2027**, from Hartford, CT through to Portland, OR.

**October 2026 check:** all 30 North American dates are announced and on sale; no European or other international dates have been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Hans Zimmer's live show has grown into one of the most distinctive concert productions in the world — a film-score composer fronting a 19-piece band in arenas, blending orchestra, rock band, and electronic elements with large-scale visuals. The "Next Level" branding is a continuation of the Hans Zimmer Live production he has been refining over years of touring, including a well-reviewed February 2025 Brooklyn stop. Zimmer himself has described his relationship with touring in unusually candid terms: in a Consequence interview he spoke about overcoming longtime stage fright after conversations with Pharrell Williams and Johnny Marr convinced him he couldn't "hide behind the screen for the rest of [his] life."

The 2027 North American routing also follows a clear demand signal — his arena shows have been selling out, and the announced tour spans seven weeks across 30 cities, his biggest single-region run to date. Zimmer closed his announcement statement with the intent behind the staging: "I want fans to feel like they're at a rave party or riding a roller coaster. For me, every show is a new voyage of discovery."

One word of warning he added himself: "If people in the first four rows turn up late, we can't help it, but we have to start heckling them for the rest of the show. So try not to turn up late."

Tickets went through the standard Zimmer sale pattern: American Express cardholder early access on Wednesday, September 23, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time, an artist presale on September 24, and the general on-sale on Friday, September 25, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time via Ticketmaster.
