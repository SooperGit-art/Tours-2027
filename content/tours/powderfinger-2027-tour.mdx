## Quick answer

**Yes — confirmed.** Powderfinger announced on October 12, 2026 an 18-show Australian reunion tour for September-October 2027, their first tour since the Sunsets Farewell Tour ended in Brisbane in November 2010. Brisbane, Sydney and Melbourne each get four nights; Cairns, Perth and Adelaide get two. Artist presales start October 14 and the general on-sale is October 19.

**October 2026 check:** all 18 dates, venues and on-sale times are official. Still to come: the rotating all-Australian support lineup, due during AusMusic Month in November.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Powderfinger were one of Australia's biggest bands: formed in Brisbane in 1989, five ARIA No. 1 albums in a row, 18 ARIA Awards, and songs such as My Happiness, These Days and Sunsets that became national fixtures. They called it quits at their peak, finishing with the Sunsets Farewell Tour in Brisbane on November 13, 2010, and apart from the remote One Night Lonely charity performance in 2020 they had not played live since.

The reunion was teased for days with billboards of coded letters and numbers referencing the band's discography and final 2010 show, before Bernard Fanning, Darren Middleton, Ian Haug, John Collins and Jon Coghill appeared together in an Instagram video on October 9, 2026. True to the promise, the full routing landed on Monday, October 12.

The shows will play in a stadium-sized big top pitched in central city locations — the band describes it as a travelling rock and roll circus, here today and gone tomorrow. After nearly 17 years away, demand in Australia is expected to be enormous.

## Ticket and presale info

Sign up at powderfinger.com now for presales from Wednesday, October 14 (artist presale plus Telstra Plus). The Live Nation presale follows on Friday, October 16, and general on-sale is Monday, October 19 — all times local: Brisbane 10am AEST, Cairns 11am AEST, Perth 11am AWST, Sydney 1pm AEDT, Adelaide 1pm ACDT, Melbourne 10am AEDT.

All shows are all ages. Under-14s are recommended to buy seated tickets where available, and patrons under 18 must be accompanied by a responsible adult holding a valid ticket. Ticketmaster is the only authorised seller, with mobile tickets through the app.
