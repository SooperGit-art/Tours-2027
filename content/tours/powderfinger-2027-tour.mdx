## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Powderfinger were one of Australia's biggest bands: formed in Brisbane in 1989, five ARIA No. 1 albums in a row, 18 ARIA Awards, and songs such as My Happiness, These Days and Sunsets that became national fixtures. They called it quits at their peak, finishing with the Sunsets Farewell Tour in Brisbane on November 13, 2010, and apart from the remote One Night Lonely charity performance in 2020 they have not played live since.

The reunion was teased for days with billboards of coded letters and numbers referencing the band's discography and final 2010 show, before Bernard Fanning, Darren Middleton, Ian Haug, John Collins and Jon Coghill appeared together in an Instagram video on Friday morning, October 9. The announcement itself says the band will build something special for the September 2027 run — after nearly 17 years away, demand in Australia is expected to be enormous.
