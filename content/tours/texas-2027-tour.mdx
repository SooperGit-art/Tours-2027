## The tour so far, for context

Texas are a Scottish pop-rock band fronted by singer Sharleen Spiteri, and one of the UK's defining bands of their era. White On Blonde, their fourth album, was released in February 1997 and has sold more than four million copies worldwide, spawning hits like Say What You Want, Halo, and Black Eyed Boy. Spiteri has recalled making the record in Glasgow — vocals recorded in the coat cupboard of her house, with nothing to lose — and that unbothered attitude has become part of the band's story.

The 2027 tour pairs the anniversary with a special reissue of the album, due February 5, 2027, almost exactly 30 years after the original release, featuring the new track Another Way To Cry and liner notes by comedian Peter Kay. The routing is a homecoming in structure: Aberdeen opens the run, Glasgow's OVO Hydro on October 21 is the hometown show, and the band then cross the UK and Ireland before a European leg through Copenhagen, Amsterdam, Brussels, Paris, Lausanne, Lyon, and Madrid. Del Amitri joining as support makes the UK and Ireland dates a double bill of Glasgow bands.
