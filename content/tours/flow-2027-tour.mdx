## Quick answer

**Yes** — FLOW are touring the world in 2027. The Japanese rock band announced the FLOW WORLD TOUR 2027 "ONCE IN A CENTURY" ~ANIME THE MAX!!!~ on November 6, 2026: 20 dates listed so far, opening January 29 at Spotify O-WEST in Tokyo, running through North America in February and returning to Japan through April 23 in Sendai, with more dates promised in every region.

## The tour so far, for context

FLOW are one of the defining anime-theme bands of the last two decades: "GO!!!" opened Naruto, "COLORS" opened Code Geass, and their songs have soundtracked Eureka Seven, Naruto Shippuden and more. The "Once in a Century" billing frames the 2027 tour as a celebration of everything the band has accomplished, mixing the iconic anime hits with tracks from their latest album. The routing tells the story of their audience: club and hall dates across Japan, a full East Coast and Southern US run, and a Latin American stop in Managua, Nicaragua — regions where anime fandom has carried their music far beyond Japan.

One point of confusion to clear up: FLOW (all caps, Japanese rock band) are not FLO (the British R&B trio). FLO announced their own 2027 Therapy At The Club tour in October 2026. Search results for "flow world tour 2027" mix the two acts together, so check the artist name carefully before buying tickets.
