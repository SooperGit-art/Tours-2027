## Quick answer

**Yes** — Sevendust will tour the US in spring 2027. The Grammy-nominated Atlanta quintet announced the 30 Years Of Dust tour on November 18, 2026: 25 dates from March 12 in Tulsa, Oklahoma, to April 15 in Charleston, South Carolina, performing their 1997 self-titled debut album in full alongside fan favorites. SOiL and Drift Mothership support.

**November 2026 check:** all 25 dates are confirmed and tickets are on sale. One venue note: early listings named The Cassidy for the April 2 St. Louis show, but the venue's own announcement confirms The Hawthorn.

## The tour so far, for context

Sevendust released their self-titled debut in 1997, and thirty years on the Atlanta five-piece — Lajon Witherspoon, Clint Lowery, John Connolly, Vince Hornsby and Morgan Rose — are still one of hard rock's most reliable live draws, with a Grammy nomination and a catalog built on groove-heavy riffs and Witherspoon's soulful vocals. Anniversary tours built around a single album have become a proven format for veteran rock bands, and playing the debut front to back gives longtime fans a rare chance to hear the record exactly as it was released.

The routing is a classic spring club-and-theater run: it opens in the Southwest, works up the West Coast, crosses the Midwest and Northeast, and closes down the Southeast. The band close out the spring on the water, joining the Summer of '99 & Beyond Cruise from Miami on April 17, two days after the tour finale in Charleston.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for all 25 dates went on general sale Friday, November 20, 2026, at 10:00 AM local time in each market, following presales earlier that week. Band VIP packages and ticket information are available through the band's official site at sevendust.com. Standard theater and club pricing applies across the run; the larger rooms, including Marathon Music Works in Nashville and The National in Richmond, are the likeliest to sell out first.
