## Quick answer

**Yes** — Luke Combs is confirmed touring in 2027. His **My Kinda Saturday Night Tour** continues with 12 North American stadium shows, kicking off April 3 in Arlington, Texas, and closing June 26 in San Diego. This follows a massive 2026 run that ended with three sold-out nights at London's Wembley Stadium.

<Callout type="confirmed">
- 12 confirmed stadium shows, April 3 – June 26, 2027, across the U.S. and Canada
- Every venue is a football stadium with 60,000+ capacity
- Opening acts: Treaty Oak Revival, Avery Anna, Shenandoah, and Wyatt McCubbin
- Bootleggers Pre-Sale: Tuesday, August 18, 2026, 10am local venue time, 20-ticket limit per fan
- General on-sale: Friday, August 21, 2026
- The in-the-round stage setup returns for 2027
- Several dates already sold out after the August 2026 on-sale (Philadelphia, Foxborough, Edmonton, San Diego), with low ticket warnings in Detroit and Vancouver
</Callout>

- These 12 dates are the only 2027 shows on his official calendar right now — no additional cities have been announced
- Exact per-city ticket pricing wasn't published alongside the announcement
</Callout>

## Full 2027 schedule

The table above lists all 12 confirmed dates. A few things worth knowing about the routing:

- **Deliberately compact**: Combs has been explicit that this is a size-over-quantity approach — 12 shows instead of a sprawling 40-city run, prioritizing family time. Other major country artists (Morgan Wallen among them) have leaned into similar stadium-scale, fewer-shows touring models recently.
- **Two Canadian stops**: Edmonton (Commonwealth Stadium) and Vancouver (BC Place) are the only non-U.S. dates.
- **Closes at Petco Park**, a baseball stadium rather than a football stadium — the one venue on the list that breaks the football-stadium pattern.

## Why this tour, and what came before it

This is a direct continuation of the 2026 My Kinda Saturday Night Tour, which was genuinely record-breaking: Combs became the first country solo artist to headline (and sell out multiple nights of) Wembley Stadium, set records at Slane Castle in Ireland and Ullevi Stadium in Sweden, and drew over 90,000 fans to a single show at Ohio Stadium — his biggest headline show ever. The tour is named after "My Kinda Saturday Night," a track from his sixth studio album, *The Way I Am*.

## Ticket and presale guidance

<Callout type="warning" title="🚫 Watch for scalper markups around presale">
The official Bootleggers Pre-Sale requires registering in advance through Combs' fan club to receive a code — there's no guaranteed access just by showing up on August 18. Buy only through lukecombs.com/tour or each venue's official ticketing partner; be especially cautious of third-party sites claiming "guaranteed presale access" for a fee, which isn't how the official Bootleggers system works.
</Callout>

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Luke Combs touring in 2027?**
Yes, confirmed — 12 North American stadium shows, April 3 to June 26, 2027, continuing his My Kinda Saturday Night Tour.

**Why only 12 shows?**
Combs has been explicit that this is a deliberate size-over-quantity approach, balancing his touring career with family time — he has young children at home. Every venue is a 60,000+ capacity football stadium.

**Is this a new tour or a continuation?**
A continuation of the 2026 My Kinda Saturday Night Tour, which wrapped with three shows at London's Wembley Stadium. Same tour name, fresh opening-act lineup for 2027.

**Who's opening?**
Treaty Oak Revival, Avery Anna, Shenandoah, and Wyatt McCubbin — all teased their involvement by posting Luke Combs covers in the week before the announcement.

**When is the presale?**
The official Bootleggers Pre-Sale starts Tuesday, August 18, 2026, at 10am local venue time, capped at 20 tickets per fan. General on-sale for all 12 shows is August 21, 2026.

**Is Luke Combs bringing back the in-the-round stage?**
Yes — Combs confirmed the in-the-round stage setup returns for the 2027 shows.

**Are these the only 2027 shows on his calendar?**
As of this writing, yes — these 12 dates are the only shows listed on his official tour calendar for 2027.
