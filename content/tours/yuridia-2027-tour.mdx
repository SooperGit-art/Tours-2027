## Quick answer

**Yes** — Yuridia is officially touring the U.S. in 2027. The Las Cartas Sobre La Mesa Tour's American leg covers 19 dates from February 4 (Seattle) through March 28 (National Harbor, Maryland), hitting California, the Southwest, Texas, Chicago, Brooklyn, and the D.C. area. The tour was announced September 8, 2026, and tickets have been on general sale since September 11, 2026.

**October 2026 check:** All 19 U.S. dates are confirmed and on sale. No 2027 dates in Mexico or elsewhere in Latin America have been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 U.S. tour makes sense for Yuridia

Yuridia comes into 2027 at a career peak. On September 3 and 4, 2026, she played two sold-out nights at Mexico City's Estadio GNP Seguros, becoming the first Mexican woman to sell out the stadium on two consecutive nights. The U.S. leg was announced just days later, turning that momentum into a coast-to-coast American run.

The tour follows her Sin Llorar Tour, which visited more than 80 cities across Mexico, the United States, Canada, and Central America in 2024 and 2025. That run established her as a reliable arena draw on both sides of the border, so a February–March 2027 U.S. leg is a natural continuation rather than a gamble.

Musically, she is in transition: the August 2026 single "Que Se Vaya" previews a forthcoming studio album, and the Las Cartas Sobre La Mesa production blends her classic pop-ballad catalog — songs like "Ángel," "Ya Te Olvidé," and "Sin Llorar" — with the regional Mexican sound of her recent albums. The Mexico City shows drew on ballads, regional Mexican songs, cumbia, and mariachi alike, so U.S. fans should expect a long, career-spanning performance rather than a singles-only set.
