## The fake 2027 schedule, debunked

Search results for a Lana Del Rey 2027 tour surface lanadelreyontour2027.com, which presents itself as a tour site with a "2027 Scheduled Dates" table: May 20, 2027 in Nashville, June 12, 2027 in San Francisco, July 5, 2027 in Paris and August 18, 2027 in Toronto, each with a price. None of this is real. Lana Del Rey's official site is lanadelrey.com, Ticketmaster shows no upcoming concerts for her, and Bandsintown lists no upcoming shows. The lookalike site invents dates and prices to capture ticket searches — the same pattern seen with other artists' unofficial "tour 2027" sites. Do not buy anything from it, and treat any social-media poster repeating those four dates as fake.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Lana Del Rey's tenth studio album, Stove — her first country record — is expected later in 2026, with three singles already out: "Henry, Come On," "Bluebird" and "White Feather Hawk Tail Deer Hunter." An artist with that demand and a new album does not stay off the road long — but until her official channels announce something, every "2027 tour" date online is speculation or fabrication.
