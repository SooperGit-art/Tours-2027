## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Tool's last album cycle is now seven years old: *Fear Inoculum* arrived in 2019 and debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200, the third straight Tool album to do so. The band toured it extensively through arenas and festivals, and its most recent run closed in December 2025 with shows in Australia, New Zealand, and Hawaii. A band that tours in multi-year cycles and has publicly said it is writing new material is a natural candidate for a 2027 return — especially if, as Carey suggested, the new record lands that year.

The Sphere angle fits Tool's live identity unusually well. The band's shows are built around Adam Jones's video imagery and a deliberately immersive, visuals-first staging, and the Sphere is the one venue designed for exactly that kind of presentation. Whether that becomes a residency, a run of special shows, or nothing at all is unconfirmed — but it is the single most concrete 2027 signal the band has given.
