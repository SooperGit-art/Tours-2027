## Quick answer

**Yes** — Alice Cooper is touring Europe in June 2027: nine shows from June 9 to June 27, announced on November 23, 2026. The run opens at Sweden Rock Festival in Sölvesborg and takes in Denmark, Poland, Germany and France, mixing festival appearances with standalone headline shows. It follows his Welcome to Our Nightmare residency at Planet Hollywood in Las Vegas, which runs November 27 to December 6, 2026 with illusionist Criss Angel.

**November 2026 check:** the nine European dates are confirmed. No US dates for 2027 have been announced, and more European shows could still be added.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Alice Cooper has never really stopped touring, but the summer of 2027 has a specific spark behind it: The Revenge of Alice Cooper, released July 25, 2025, reunited the surviving members of the original Alice Cooper band — Michael Bruce, Dennis Dunaway and Neal Smith — for their first full album together since 1973's Muscle of Love, produced by Bob Ezrin and led by the single Black Mamba featuring Robby Krieger of The Doors. A European summer run is the natural way to take that reunion on the road, and the festival-heavy routing fits a 78-year-old showman who has headlined Sweden Rock and Tollwood before.

The announcement also explains why "Alice Cooper tour 2027" broke out on Google Trends the morning after the dates were revealed: the shock-rock veteran's first batch of 2027 shows landed just as his Las Vegas residency with Criss Angel was opening. The residency, Welcome to Our Nightmare, closes out 2026 at Planet Hollywood, and the European run picks the story up six months later.

## Ticket and presale info

No single on-sale date has been announced for the 2027 run. Tickets are sold through alicecooper.com and each festival's own ticketing — Sweden Rock Festival, Tollwood Festival and Printemps de Pérouges among them — with the standalone headline shows (Aalborg, Gliwice, Rastatt, Tettnang, Halle and Hanover) sold through their local promoters. Check the official site for links as each date's ticketing goes live, and buy only from official sellers: festival dates in particular attract resale listings before tickets are actually on sale.
