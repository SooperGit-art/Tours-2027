## Quick answer

**Yes** — Tame Impala are touring in 2027. Kevin Parker's project officially announced the **Deadbeat EU/UK Tour 2027** in August 2026: 14 confirmed dates across the UK and Europe, running **May 28 to July 10, 2027**, with Wet Leg supporting all headline arena shows. The run ends with three major festival slots in Poland, Belgium, and Portugal.

**October 2026 check:** the European dates are fully announced and on sale. No North American 2027 dates have been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Tame Impala's fifth studio album *Deadbeat* arrived via Columbia Records in October 2025 and became the biggest commercial era of Kevin Parker's career. Lead single "End of Summer" won the Grammy for Best Dance/Electronic Recording in February 2026, "Loser" reached No. 1 on Spotify's global chart after appearing in *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*, and the JENNIE remix of "Dracula" peaked at No. 5 on the Billboard Hot 100. The Deadbeat tour played sold-out arenas through 2025 and 2026, including a UK arena run and a North American leg with Dominic Fike opening.

The 2027 announcement extended that same touring cycle back into Europe rather than launching a new one. Parker has described *Deadbeat* as a return to his roots in Australian rave culture, and the live show's center-stage "in the round" format was built around the album's club-psych sound. Adding three extra shows within days of the first on-sale — a third night in Rotterdam, a second in Manchester, and a third in London — reflects how fast the initial 11 dates sold.

## Ticket and presale info

The UK and European presale opened Wednesday, August 26, 2026 at 9am BST / 10am CEST, with Co-op member presale for Manchester and O2 Priority access for the two original London dates. General sale followed Friday, August 28, 2026 at 9am BST. Paris dates ran on a separate schedule: presale August 31, general sale September 2. Tickets are sold through tameimpala.com and Ticketmaster. When last checked, several shows (both Manchester nights and all three London nights) were flagged as limited or low availability on Ticketmaster, so the second-wave dates added for 2027 are already in demand.
