## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Nickelback's touring pattern makes a 2027 run highly likely even though nothing has been announced. The band's most recent major outing, the Get Rollin' Tour behind their 2022 album *Get Rollin'*, carried them through 2023 and 2024, and they have spent their career cycling between records and arena tours — as Mike Kroeger put it, "record, tour, record, tour." With a new album (*Everything Under the Sun*) in the pipeline and a new single ("Rattle the Cage") already out, the pieces for the next touring cycle are falling into place.

The band's own words are the strongest signal available. In a July 2026 interview with Loudwire Nights, Kroeger did not hedge: "You're going to get some Nickelback in 2027." He tied it directly to their love of playing live, adding "we'll be out there." That is about as close to a tour confirmation as a band can give without naming dates.

What we do not have — and what fans should not invent — is any routing. No cities, no venues, no tour name, and no presale schedule have been published by the band or their promoters. The one hard data point, the February 27, 2027 Hondo Rodeo Fest appearance in Tampa, shows the band is booking 2027 dates, which is consistent with a broader tour announcement following later.
