## Quick answer

**Yes — 70 shows confirmed.** Nickelback have announced the Everything Under The Sun Tour, a 70-date world run from February 6, 2027 in Pittsburgh to October 16, 2027 in Pokolbin, Australia. English rock band Don Broco support the headline dates across North America, the UK, Europe and Australia, behind the new album *Everything Under The Sun*, out October 30, 2026.

**October 2026 check:** the full routing is announced and presales begin October 13, 2026. What has not been published yet: ticket prices, support acts beyond Don Broco, and any Asian or further Latin American dates.

## How the 2027 tour came together

The band had been signalling this for months. In a July 2026 interview, bassist Mike Kroeger said fans would get Nickelback in 2027, describing the band's rhythm as "record, tour, record, tour" and adding that they love playing live. The October 9, 2026 announcement made it official: the Everything Under The Sun Tour, named for the upcoming eleventh album, with the routing published in full.

The pairing with Don Broco grew out of a recording collaboration. Don Broco's 2026 album *Nightmare Tripping* features Nickelback's Chad Kroeger and Ryan Peake on its title track, and the two bands are now taking that connection on the road for the largest touring commitment of Don Broco's career — 69 of the 70 dates, skipping only the Tampa festival stop.

The routing is arena-scale throughout: PPG Paints Arena, Chicago's United Center, Toronto's Scotiabank Arena and Vancouver's Rogers Arena in North America; The O2 in London, Dublin's 3Arena, Amsterdam's Ziggo Dome and Munich's Olympiahalle in Europe; then a seven-show Australian finish including two outdoor estate dates at Geelong's Mount Duneed Estate and Pokolbin's Roche Estate.

## Ticket and presale info

North American presales begin Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time, with the general sale on Friday, October 16, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time through Live Nation and Ticketmaster. Australian shows run presales from October 13 (artist presale October 13–15, Live Nation presale October 15) with a general sale also on Friday, October 16, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time; the Geelong and Pokolbin outdoor shows are handled by Face To Face Touring. VIP packages with premium seating and lounge access are available in select markets.

UK and European on-sale times follow the same general pattern — check the official event pages for your city before the presale windows open. Buy only through official channels linked from nickelback.com; ignore resale listings or social-media posters tied to dates that are not on the official routing.
