**Yes** — Ben Folds Five are officially touring in 2027. All three original members are back: Ben Folds on piano, Robert Sledge on bass and Darren Jessee on drums. **October 2026 check:** the initial nine dates are confirmed and on sale; more dates are expected but have not been announced yet.

## The 15-year road back

Ben Folds Five formed in 1993 in Chapel Hill, North Carolina — three musicians, zero guitars, and a sound frontman Ben Folds once described as built on piano-driven power pop. Their 1995 self-titled debut and 1997's Whatever and Ever Amen (home of the enduring ballad "Brick") made them one of the defining alternative acts of the late '90s. The band released a third album in 1999, split, reunited for 2012's The Sound of the Life of the Mind — their highest-charting US album at No. 10 on the Billboard 200 — and then went quiet as a unit in 2013.

Folds himself never left the stage, touring relentlessly as a solo artist (most recently behind 2024's Christmas-themed Sleigher), while the trio's legacy kept growing in their absence. The 2027 reunion doubles as an anniversary celebration: the band released a 30th-anniversary deluxe edition of their debut earlier in 2026, and 2027 marks 30 years since Whatever and Ever Amen. It is the first time all three members have toured together since the band disbanded.
