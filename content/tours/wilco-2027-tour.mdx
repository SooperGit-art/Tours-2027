## Quick answer

**Yes** — Wilco are touring in 2027, and the announcement landed the day before this page was written: the Wilcoworld Tour 2027, 34 dates announced on November 25, 2026, running from a three-night residency in Marrakech, Morocco (March 25–27) through a 17-date US leg (April 20–May 15) and a 14-date European leg (June 12–July 3). The centrepiece is a five-night stand at The Fillmore in San Francisco, May 9–14, and the run closes at Rock Werchter in Belgium on July 3.

**November 2026 check:** all 34 dates are confirmed and tickets go on general sale November 27, 2026 at 10am local time. No further legs have been announced yet, and more dates could still be added.

## The tour so far, for context

Wilco have been one of American rock's most reliable touring bands since forming in Chicago in 1994, and the Wilcoworld Tour 2027 is a classic Wilco routing: mid-size theatres and amphitheatres in the US, a residency in an unusual location — this time Marrakech, where the band already ran a getaway event — and a summer festival circuit in Europe. Frontman Jeff Tweedy's latest solo release, *Twilight Override*, arrived in 2025, following Wilco's 2024 release *Hot Sun Cool Shroud*.

The timing explains the Trends breakout. The band announced the tour on Thanksgiving, November 25, 2026, before they had even finished their 2026 run — four sold-out hometown Winterlude shows in Chicago in early December, at the Chicago Theatre and Thalia Hall. The announcement also follows news earlier in November that Tweedy is joining Punch Brothers for a February 2027 run billed as the PB&J Tour; that is a separate Tweedy project, not Wilco, though it fills the gap between the Winterlude shows and the Marrakech residency.
