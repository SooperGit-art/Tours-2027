Their Power Up Tour, in support of the 2020 album Power Up, was a genuine comeback run: 89 shows over six legs, from Gelsenkirchen in May 2024 through South America, Europe, Australia and North America, ending at MetLife Stadium on October 2, 2026, with Dave Grohl on stage for "Highway to Hell". The summer 2026 North American leg was billed as a chance to "power up one last time" — but the band has not said it was the last time, and no retirement has been announced.

That ambiguity is exactly what the fake date sites exploit. AC/DC almost always tour behind a new album, and their last three arrived about six years apart, so a 2027 tour would need either a new record or a break with that pattern. Until the band says something, every 2027 date list with prices attached should be treated as fiction — including the tribute acts, like "The AC/DC Show Canada" and "Noise Pollution", whose 2027 theatre dates are sometimes misread as the real band's.
