## The tour so far, for context

The Lumineers' most recent era belongs to *Automatic*, their fifth studio album, released February 14, 2025 via Dualtone. The record debuted at #16 on the Billboard 200 (and #2 on Top Rock Albums, Americana/Folk Albums and Independent Albums), and its lead single "Same Old Song" reached #1 on Billboard's Adult Alternative Airplay chart — the duo's eighth song to top that chart.

The Automatic World Tour was the band's biggest undertaking since the Brightside World Tour, which sold over 1.1 million tickets across 120 cities, 29 countries and six continents, including record-breaking stadium shows at Coors Field in Denver and Wrigley Field in Chicago. The Automatic cycle brought them back to Australia and New Zealand in January 2026 — their first visit since the sold-out 2022 run — with dates in Christchurch, Auckland, Melbourne, Brisbane, Sydney, Adelaide and Perth, and continued through US shows and festival appearances into August 2026.

Wesley Schultz and Jeremiah Fraites founded the band in 2005 and have built one of folk rock's most durable live draws: 24 #1 hits across radio formats, two Grammy nominations, and headlining slots at Bonnaroo, Glastonbury and Fuji Rock. That track record is exactly why a 2027 return to the road is plausible — but plausibility is not an announcement, and none has been made.
