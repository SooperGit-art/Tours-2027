## Quick answer

**Yes** — Will Young has officially announced **The Evergreen 25th Anniversary Tour**, a 16-date full-band UK tour marking 25 years since his 2002 Pop Idol win. The tour runs from 20 February 2027 at Manchester's O2 Apollo to 13 March 2027 at Cardiff's Utilita Arena, covering England, Scotland and Wales.

**October 2026 check:** all 16 UK dates and venues are confirmed and tickets went on general sale on 2 October 2026. No international dates, support acts or additional shows have been announced yet.

## Why a 25th-anniversary tour makes sense

Will Young's 2002 Pop Idol victory was one of the defining pop-culture moments of the early 2000s, and his career since has outlasted the format that launched it. His debut double A-side "Anything Is Possible / Evergreen" sold more than 1.7 million copies in the UK, making it one of the best-selling UK singles of all time, and he has since collected two BRIT Awards, four UK No. 1 albums, eleven Top 10 albums and eleven Top 10 singles.

His most recent album, "Light It Up", reached the UK Top 5 in 2024 and was supported by a sold-out acoustic tour. The anniversary shows are being positioned as the counterpart to that run — intimate sets replaced by a full-band celebration of every era of his catalogue.

In the announcement Young framed the tour as a thank-you to the fans who have stayed with him: "To reach 25 years is a privilege and an honour... People voted and trusted me and that has forever been a dream come true." A new live performance of "Evergreen", recorded as part of The Evergreen 25th Anniversary Sessions, was released alongside the announcement.
