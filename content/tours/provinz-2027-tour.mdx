## The tour so far, for context

Provinz are one of the German indie scene's steady growers rather than overnight sensations. Formed in Vogt, near Ravensburg, in 2012 and fronted by Vincent Waizenegger, they broke through with emotionally direct German-language songwriting — "Was uns high macht" and "Diego Maradona" became their signature songs. Debut album Wir bauten uns Amerika was followed by Zorn & Liebe, and the Zorn & Liebe Tour carried them through concert halls across Germany, Austria and Switzerland, plus full-band festival sets at Rock am Ring and Rock im Park.

The breakout Google Trends spike in October 2026 is the interesting part: with no announcement behind it, the most plausible driver is that Burg Clam listing — a castle-venue summer show, co-billed with Zartmann, appearing on aggregators as "on sale." It may well be real, but until Provinz or the venue says so, it stays in the rumor column.
