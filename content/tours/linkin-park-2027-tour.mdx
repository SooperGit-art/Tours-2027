## The tour so far, for context

Linkin Park returned in September 2024 after a seven-year hiatus following Chester Bennington's death in 2017, unveiling Emily Armstrong (Dead Sara) on vocals, Colin Brittain on drums, and the comeback album From Zero, released in November 2024. The From Zero World Tour ran from late 2024 through 30 June 2026, closing with a special LP Underground event at Stadion Letzigrund in Zurich after final European stops in Lisbon, Madrid, Florence and Werchter.

The comeback was commercially emphatic: near-instant sellouts, added second dates (including a second Spanish show after 30,000 tickets went in minutes), and a setlist that blended Hybrid Theory and Meteora staples with new From Zero material. Reviews of both the album and Armstrong's performances were broadly positive, and the band's own LPTV series described the Zurich finale as the end of the two-year run — "thank you for an incredible 2 years."

So far, the band has said almost nothing publicly about what comes next. A Rolling Stone India interview with Mike Shinoda ahead of the band's first India shows in early 2026 only vaguely hinted at post-From Zero plans, and no new album has been confirmed. That silence is exactly the vacuum the fake 2027 "announcements" have filled.
