## Quick answer

**Yes** — the Eros Ramazzotti 2027 tour is real, officially announced, and on sale. The *Una Storia Importante World Tour* has 25 dates listed for 2027 across 12 countries: a European arena leg running April–May 2027 and a rescheduled Americas leg running September–October 2027.

**October 2026 check:** dates are confirmed via Ticketmaster and official venue announcements, but the itinerary is being reshuffled after his August 2026 vocal cord surgery — some European dates that were listed for spring 2027 now appear as 2028 dates in ticket listings, so re-confirm your city with the official seller before buying.

## Why the 2027 tour is a rescheduled one

The *Una Storia Importante* era began with his 16th studio album, released in November 2025 and reported to have debuted at No. 1 in Italy. The world tour launched with a 2026 European leg that sold out repeatedly — including Prague's O2 arena, where demand was so strong that the venue and organizer Charmenko Czechia announced a second date, May 20, 2027, as part of the same tour.

Then came the surgery. In late August 2026, Ramazzotti underwent vocal cord surgery — the second such procedure — which he said "went great." His medical team, however, prescribed an extended rehabilitation period before he could return to the demands of consecutive arena shows. As a result, the entire late-2026 run across the United States, Canada and Latin America was moved to 2027, while the European dates were left unaffected at the time.

That reschedule is still settling. The Americas leg is now listed for September–October 2027 (Toronto, Laval, Boston, New York, Rosemont, Miami, Inglewood, Monterrey and Mexico City), and the European leg for April–May 2027 — but some listings for both legs still show as postponed, and a handful of European dates appear to have shifted into 2028. Fans should treat the 2027 itinerary as confirmed but fluid.

## Ticket and presale info

2027 tickets are on sale now through Ticketmaster and venue-official sellers (Ticketmaster and Ticketportal for the Prague date). There is no new general presale campaign — these are existing on-sale dates, many of them carried over from the postponed 2026 run.

If you hold tickets from an original 2026 date, check your Ticketmaster account for the new date or refund options; Costa Rica ticket holders are being refunded automatically through the local promoter and Eticket.cr, with no replacement date announced. Avoid third-party resale markups where official inventory is still available, and — given the reshuffle — verify each date's status on the official seller's page before purchasing.
