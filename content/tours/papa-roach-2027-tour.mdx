## Quick answer

**Yes** — Papa Roach will tour the US in 2027. The band announced the North American leg of the Rise Of The Roach Tour in October 2026: 30 arena and amphitheater dates from May 11 in Biloxi, Mississippi to June 27 in Auburn, Washington, with Ice Nine Kills and Ninth Key as support. Presales ran October 20–22, 2026 and general sale opened October 23, 2026. **October 2026 check:** all 30 dates are officially announced and on sale.

## The tour so far, for context

The Rise Of The Roach tour is not a new production — it began in the UK and Europe, where it sold out venues including London's Wembley Arena, and the 2027 run brings it to North America. The timing follows the band's 25th anniversary of their breakthrough album Infest in 2025, and their long-running suicide-prevention work around "Leave A Light On (Talk Away The Dark)," which they recorded with Carrie Underwood.

Papa Roach remain one of hard rock's most durable live acts: two-time Grammy nominees with 10 studio albums, most recently Ego Trip (2022) on their own New Noize Records, which has passed 850 million global streams and produced four No. 1 singles, taking the band's career totals to 26 Top-10 hits and 12 No. 1s. A 30-date US amphitheater run in summer 2027 fits exactly how this band has always worked — long, loud, and relentless.

## Ticket and presale info

General sale opened October 23, 2026 via Ticketmaster, after presales on October 20–22 that included a Citi cardmember window and a Spotify Reserved allocation for eligible Premium top fans. $1 from every ticket goes to the American Foundation for Suicide Prevention, continuing the band's support of the cause since 2023. If a date shows as sold out, check the venue's official resale options rather than unofficial ticket sites.
