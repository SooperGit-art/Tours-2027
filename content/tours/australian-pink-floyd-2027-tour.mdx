**Yes** — The Australian Pink Floyd Show are touring in 2027 on the Greatest Hits World Tour, billed "Echoes of '77." ## The tour so far, for context

The Australian Pink Floyd Show formed in Adelaide in 1988, when five friends started playing Pink Floyd songs for fun in a rented school room. Nearly 40 years on, they are one of the world's most popular Pink Floyd tribute bands, touring arenas with a spectacular live show built around state-of-the-art lighting, lasers and specially designed imagery projected onto a huge screen. Their credibility with Floyd fans is unusual for a tribute act: they performed at David Gilmour's 50th birthday celebration, with Gilmour and Richard Wright joining them on stage.

The 2027 tour has a theme: "Echoes of '77" revisits the era of the album *Animals*, released in 1977, alongside a handpicked selection of Pink Floyd's greatest hits spanning 1967 to 1994. The French leg alone runs to nine shows — Amiens, Paris, Rennes, Poitiers, Bordeaux, Grenoble, Lyon, Toulon and Nice — before the band head east to Prague and Kraków, then return to the UK in the autumn for their biggest British run of the year.
