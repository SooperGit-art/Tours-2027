## Quick answer

**Yes** — Kraftklub officially announced five exclusive 2027 stadium and open-air shows under the banner "Das war ja keine Phase," billed as an "Eras Tour" through their five-album career. The run opens June 5, 2027, in Basel and closes August 14, 2027, at Munich's Königsplatz. **October 2026 check:** all five shows are announced and tickets have been on sale since the September 2026 presale.

## The tour so far, for context

Kraftklub are playing the biggest concerts of their career in 2027 — and they built up to it deliberately. The Chemnitz five-piece spent the Sterben in Karl-Marx-Stadt tour scaling up, including a sold-out Trabrennbahn show in Hamburg for 35,000 fans in August 2026, plus stops at Berlin's Wuhlheide and Munich's Olympiahalle. The 2027 leap to stadiums like Hamburg's Volksparkstadion is the natural next step.

The "Das war ja keine Phase" concept — roughly "it wasn't just a phase" — leans into the band's own history. In their announcement video the band walked through each musical and fashion era: the college jackets of the 2012 debut Mit K, the Harringtons of In Schwarz, the red jackets of Keine Nacht für Niemand, the KARGO parkas, and the all-white suits of the current record. The band calls the tour its "East German eras" show, and fan forums had already guessed the Eras-Tour format before the announcement. Notably, Chemnitz is absent from the five announced cities — a talking point among fans, though the band has said nothing about additional dates.

## Ticket and presale info

The official online presale started September 16, 2026, at 16:00 CET via the usual German ticket outlets, with official hard tickets also sold through authorized partners. The band's own pop-up sales in Leipzig, Munich, and Hamburg let early buyers get tickets in person — and meet band members — before the online sale. With only five shows on the itinerary, demand is expected to be heavy; stick to official outlets and the band's announced channels.
