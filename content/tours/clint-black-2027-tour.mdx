## The tour so far, for context

Clint Black is one of the defining voices of '90s country, and the Number 1s Live on Tour is built directly on that legacy: a night of the chart-topping hits that made him a mainstay of the genre, with what his team describes as a nostalgic element taking audiences back to the golden era of '90s country. "I'm excited to hit the road again in 2027," Black said when the tour was announced in November 2026.

The routing is classic Clint Black territory — theatres, casinos and rodeos across the American South and West, including a February 23 stop at the San Antonio Stock Show & Rodeo and a March 6 night at the Westgate in Las Vegas. It follows his "Back On The Blacktop Tour," which is still running through November 2026, so the 2027 dates keep him on the road with barely a pause. The announcement drove "clint black tour 2027" to breakout status on Google Trends.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for select dates are on sale now at clintblack.com, and general on-sale for several dates began November 13, 2026 at 10am EST.
