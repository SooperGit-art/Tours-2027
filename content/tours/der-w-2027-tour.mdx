## Quick answer

**Yes** — Der W is touring in 2027. Stephan Weidner announced 15 Germany concerts for autumn 2027, running September 29 in Hannover to October 16 in Offenbach — the largest solo tour of his career — with the tour poster carrying the title *VI*, the name of his announced sixth studio album.

**November 2026 check:** all 15 dates below are officially announced. No dates outside Germany have been announced for this tour.

## The tour so far, for context

Der W is the solo project Stephan Weidner launched after Böhse Onkelz split in 2005, following a career as the band's bassist, songwriter, and leader since 1980. As Der W he steps to the microphone himself, with guitarist Dirk Czuya at his side since 2010, and the repertoire runs from the straight Deutschrock of his early solo albums to the Kafka settings on *III*. His solo debut, *Schneller, Höher, Weidner* (2008), went gold in Germany.

The recent touring pattern explains the scale of the 2027 run. In 2024 Weidner played twelve acoustic evenings with a string ensemble, documented on a live album released in November 2025; in March 2026 he returned to electric club shows for fourteen dates. The 2027 rooms — Haus Auensee, Astra, Turbinenhalle 1, Theater am Tanzbrunnen — are a step up from those spring 2026 clubs, and the routing runs north to east to southwest across Germany. The previous solo album, *V*, is now four years old, so the *VI* billing signals new material alongside the back catalogue.

Presale begins Wednesday, November 4, 2026, at 10 a.m. via myticket, and Eventim lists tickets from Friday, November 6, 2026, at 10 a.m. Prices and ticket types had not been published at announcement time, and the spring 2026 club tour nearly sold out, so early booking is advisable.
