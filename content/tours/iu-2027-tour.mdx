## Quick answer

**No** — IU has no confirmed 2027 tour dates. But this is one of the more credible "rumored" cases on this site: in 2026 she postponed her Korean concerts and her 6th full album specifically so she could recover and return in 2027, telling fans "we can definitely begin 2027 together." **October 2026 check:** still zero announced 2027 dates on any ticketing platform; anything labeled a "2027 IU world tour" online right now is fan speculation, not an announcement.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

IU (Lee Ji-eun, born 1993) is one of Korea's most bankable live acts: her first tour *Real Fantasy* ran in 2012, followed by the Asian tours *dlwlrma* (2018) and *Love, Poem* (2019), the stadium-scale *The Golden Hour* at Jamsil Olympic Stadium in 2022, and the *H.E.R. World Tour* across Asia, Europe and North America in 2024 with encore shows at Seoul World Cup Stadium. The 2026 postponement was framed entirely around recovery — she described symptoms that made it "repeatedly difficult to regain my form while singing" — and her message to fans was a promise, not a goodbye. If treatment succeeds, a 2027 return tour built around the delayed 6th album would follow her established pattern of album-then-tour cycles.
