## Quick answer

**Yes** — Calvin Harris is touring Australia in February 2027, and it is a genuine milestone: his first-ever standalone headline tour of the country, announced September 16, 2026. Eleven outdoor dates run from February 17 to February 28 across Perth, Brisbane, Sydney and Melbourne. **October 2026 check:** no 2027 dates outside Australia have been announced.

## What's confirmed vs. what's still coming

<Callout type="confirmed" title="✅ Confirmed for 2027">
- Calvin Harris's first-ever Australian headline tour: 11 dates, February 17–28, 2027 — Perth (Langley Park, Feb 17–18), Brisbane (RNA Showgrounds, Feb 19), Sydney (The Domain, Feb 20–24), Melbourne (Flemington Racecourse, Feb 25, 26, 28).
- The run is presented by Untitled Group, Full Circle and Live Nation, and billed by Ticketmaster as his first full Australian headlining tour after nearly two decades of festival-only visits.
- All shows are 18+, general admission, in large outdoor venues (parks, a showground, a racecourse), with doors listed around 5–6pm and a scheduled 11pm finish.
- Tickets went through Telstra Plus presale (Sep 21, 2026), artist presale (Sep 23) and general sale (Sep 24), digital-only via the Ticketmaster app with a 6-per-transaction limit.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Calvin Harris is one of the few DJs who can fill stadiums and racecourses on name alone. The Scottish producer behind "We Found Love," "Summer," "One Kiss" and "Miracle" has spent the better part of two decades as a fixture of the global festival circuit and Las Vegas residencies, but Australia has always been a festival-only market for him — Big Day Out in 2010, multiple Stereosonic editions, and most recently Festival X in 2022 alongside John Summit, Green Velvet and Sub Focus.

The announcement framed the 2027 run as a deliberate change of strategy: four cities, his name as the central axis of each night, and large-scale outdoor production rather than a festival slot. The initial four dates sold well enough that the run quickly grew to eleven shows, including five straight nights at Sydney's Domain and three at Melbourne's Flemington Racecourse — a strong signal of demand.

February is peak Australian summer, and the tour lands in an already crowded 2027 live calendar — but a first-ever headline tour from an artist of this scale is the kind of event that pulls interstate travel, which is exactly what the promoters are counting on.

## Ticket and presale info

All ticketing runs through Ticketmaster Australia. The sale sequence was: Telstra Plus presale from 11am local on Monday, September 21, 2026; artist presale from 12pm local on Wednesday, September 23; general onsale from 1pm local on Thursday, September 24. You need a My Ticketmaster account, tickets are digital-only in the Ticketmaster app (no printed tickets), and purchases are capped at 6 tickets per transaction — Ticketmaster says orders exceeding the limit may be cancelled. Every show is strictly 18+. Accessible tickets are handled separately through Ticketmaster's Accessible Bookings help centre or their Accessible Booking Line.
