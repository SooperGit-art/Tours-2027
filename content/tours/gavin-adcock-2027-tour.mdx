## Quick answer

**Yes** — Gavin Adcock officially extended The Day I Hang It Up Tour into 2027 with 15 announced dates. The run opens with two Australian festival appearances at Meatstock in March, then hits 13 US dates from April 1 in Lexington, Kentucky through May 28 at Floores Country Store in Helotes, Texas, with Blake Whiten supporting every show. Artist presale starts October 13, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time; public on-sale is October 16. **October 2026 check:** these 15 dates are the complete announced 2027 leg — no further dates confirmed yet.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Gavin Adcock is country music's fastest-rising headliner. The Georgia native's 2024 major-label debut *Actin' Up Again* set the record for the biggest streaming major-label country debut from a solo male artist that year, and his 2025 follow-up *Own Worst Enemy* debuted as the top new country album on the Billboard Top 200 Country chart and the top-streaming new album on the all-genre Billboard 200 for its release week.

The 2027 leg extends a tour that is already running hot: the 2026 stretch of The Day I Hang It Up Tour runs through mid-November, with a December 10 Las Vegas date, so the March–May 2027 dates keep the same production and band rolling with barely a pause. Doubling up rooms like The Factory in St. Louis and the legendary Floores Country Store in Helotes shows real demand.
