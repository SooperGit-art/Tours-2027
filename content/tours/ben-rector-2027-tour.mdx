## Quick answer

**Yes** — Ben Rector is touring in 2027. The Nashville singer-songwriter brings the Ben Rector and Friends format back on the road with Jon McLaughlin and Josh Jenkins: 17 confirmed dates from February 26 in Maricopa, Arizona, to September 10 in Waukegan, Illinois, with ticket listings showing the run continuing into October 2027.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Ben Rector is one of the most successful independent artists of his generation, and the Ben Rector and Friends format — built around his long collaboration with Jon McLaughlin and Josh Jenkins — is a proven draw in the theater circuit. His ninth studio album, "The Richest Man in the World" (2025), launched his most recent full-band tour, which included a sold-out show at Salt Lake City's Delta Center, the largest headline show of his career. The 2027 run extends that momentum into the rooms where his audience is strongest: historic theaters and amphitheaters across the South, Midwest and West.
