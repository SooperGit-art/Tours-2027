## Quick answer

**Yes — 21 dates.** Five Finger Death Punch have confirmed 21 arena shows across the UK, Ireland and Europe from January 16 to February 22, 2027, as the European leg of their 20th Anniversary World Tour. Lamb of God and Bleed From Within support most dates.

**October 2026 check:** all 21 dates below are announced and on sale. Further legs (South America, Australia, Asia) have been mentioned by the band but not dated yet.

## What's confirmed vs. what's still coming

<Callout type="confirmed" title="✅ Confirmed for 2027">
- 21 arena dates: UK/Ireland January 16–23, mainland Europe January 26 – February 22, 2027
- Support: Lamb of God and Bleed From Within (Dymytry replaces Lamb of God in Prague; Copenhagen without Lamb of God)
- General on-sale from May 8, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time
</Callout>

<Callout type="rumor" title="⚠️ Not yet announced">
- Further 2027 legs beyond Europe — South America, Australia and Asia are "in the talks" per guitarist Zoltan Bathory
- Any festival dates beyond the arena run — none announced
- A 20th-anniversary North American leg for 2027 — the NA tour is summer/fall 2026
</Callout>

## Why this tour is a milestone

2027 marks twenty years since the Las Vegas band's debut The Way of the Fist, and the anniversary show is built to cover the full arc: the band says the setlist reaches from that 2007 debut through the re-recorded Best Of volumes to the forthcoming tenth studio album. The run also includes their biggest UK headline shows ever — London's O2 Arena and Manchester's AO Arena — recognition of a fanbase that has stayed with them through two decades of lineup changes and chart runs.

The European leg follows the 48-city North American tour of summer/fall 2026 (with Cody Jinks and Eva Under Fire), keeping the anniversary celebration on a rolling world-tour schedule the band says could stretch two or three years.

## Ticket and presale info

General on-sale began Friday, May 8, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time through national Ticketmaster sites and local promoters. France followed its own window: artist presale May 6, Spotify presale May 7 at noon, promoter presales May 11–12, general sale May 13 at 10 a.m. local. With most shows now weeks from selling through, buy through official channels — and watch for the band's own 20th-anniversary VIP packages at select arenas.
