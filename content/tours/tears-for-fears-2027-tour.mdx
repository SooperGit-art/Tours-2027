## Quick answer

**Yes — 3 dates.** Tears for Fears have three confirmed 2027 shows: August 25, 27 and 28 at Wembley Stadium in London. These are not headlining Tears for Fears dates — the duo (Roland Orzabal and Curt Smith) are the support act for Harry Styles' Together Together residency at Wembley.

**October 2026 check:** no standalone Tears for Fears 2027 headlining tour has been announced.

## Why these dates make sense

Tears for Fears have been selective about live work since their 2022 album The Tipping Point, pairing a co-headline North American tour in 2022–23 with a Las Vegas residency in fall 2025 rather than non-stop touring. A high-profile Wembley support slot fits that pattern: three stadium nights in London without committing to a full touring cycle of their own.

Wembley is familiar ground for the band — they played there with Harry Styles in 2022-era billing cycles and returned to the stadium on his recent residency run. For fans hoping for a full Tears for Fears tour, the honest signal is patience: the band's last full headlining announcement cycle took over a year from hint to dates.

## Ticket and presale info

These shows are Harry Styles Together Together dates, so tickets go through Harry Styles' official ticketing channels and Wembley Stadium. Ticket listings for the three nights are already live through standard aggregators, with prices varying by section — typical Wembley stadium pricing applies. Do not buy from resale listings advertising standalone "Tears for Fears tour" shows that are not on the official schedule.
