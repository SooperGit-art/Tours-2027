## Quick answer

**Yes** — Manowar's 2027 tour is officially confirmed and on sale. The **Kings Of Metal Fighting The World Tour 2027** runs from January 15 to March 27, 2027, with **28 announced shows** across Czechia, Hungary, Germany, and Italy. Each night features alternating setlists built around a full-album performance of either *Kings of Metal* or *Fighting the World*, plus other epic anthems in a brand-new stage production.

**October 2026 check:** all announced dates are European. The band promised "more battlegrounds" beyond the initial Germany run, and Czech, Hungarian, and Italian dates have since been added — but no North American shows have been confirmed.

## Why this tour makes sense

Manowar essentially skipped 2026 and announced the 2027 run back in November 2025, quoting the band directly: the founders and defenders of true metal were "answering the call of their loyal Army of Immortals" after the triumphant Blood Of Our Enemies Tour, which had celebrated *Hail To England* and *Sign Of The Hammer*. The new concept is a straight continuation of that album-anniversary formula — this time honoring *Fighting the World* and *Kings of Metal*, the records that shaped generations of metalheads, in the band's own words.

The rollout has followed the announcement's promise. After the initial 21 German dates, the band confirmed four Czech shows — Ostrava, Brno, Pardubice, and Prague, the Golden City returning to the itinerary for the first time in over a decade — a Budapest show marking Manowar's first performance in Hungary in more than two decades, and a two-night Bergamo stand where the band will play *Kings of Metal* in full on March 26 and *Fighting the World* in full on March 27.

Bassist and band leader Joey DeMaio framed the Budapest return personally: "This album belongs in Budapest! A thousand-year-old capital that has survived occupation, siege and upheaval, and has always risen again. A city and a people who refuse to fall, whatever the cost — that is the very same spirit that *Fighting the World* is about." That emotional, city-by-city framing is exactly how Manowar has kept a four-decade touring career — and the Army of Immortals — alive.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for the confirmed dates are on sale now through the official tour page at manowar.com/tour, with venue-level sales through local outlets for stops like Budapest and Bergamo. Army Of Immortals fan-club members got an exclusive presale from November 21–25, 2025, and Ultimate Fan Experience upgrades have been offered on select dates. For the Bergamo double-header at ChorusLife Arena, the promoters are selling both a discounted two-night season ticket and individual-day tickets, and each evening gets a completely different setlist.
