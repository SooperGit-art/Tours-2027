## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Gilmour tours on his own glacial schedule: major solo runs in 2006 (On an Island), 2015–2016 (Rattle That Lock) and 2024 (Luck and Strange), each tied to a new album and each deliberately limited to a handful of residencies rather than a sprawling world tour. The Luck and Strange run was a triumph — six nights at Rome's Circo Massimo, six at London's Royal Albert Hall, three at the Hollywood Bowl, a night at the Intuit Dome, and four at Madison Square Garden — followed in 2025 by The Luck and Strange Concerts live album and the Live at the Circus Maximus concert film.

That pattern suggests what a 2027 run would require: new music. Luck and Strange hit No. 1 in the UK, Germany, Poland, the Netherlands and several other countries in September 2024, so the demand is unquestionably there. But with no new album announced and Gilmour's history of long gaps between tours, 2027 is plausible only as speculation — the calendar fits his rhythm, but nothing official supports it yet.
