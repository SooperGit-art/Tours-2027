## Why a 2027 tour would be a surprise — and what he has actually said

Howard Carpendale, born in Durban, South Africa in January 1946, became one of German-language pop's most durable stars after his 1970 breakthrough with "Das schöne Mädchen von Seite Eins", scoring era-defining hits like "Ti Amo", "Hello Again", and "Nachts, wenn alles schläft". His promoter's farewell-tour copy for the 2026 run framed it plainly: after 60 years on stage, this was the last major tour — with a 15-piece band revisiting six decades of hits through Germany, Austria, and Switzerland.

Carpendale has retired before, though. He marked an announced retirement with a 2003 farewell concert at the Kölnarena, then came back in 2007 and resumed touring — so fans know that "farewell" in his case has meant a farewell to major tours rather than to the stage entirely. The 2026 farewell itself grew out of the 2024 "Let's Do It Again!" run, which he said made him want to stay on tour "much longer" — a wish the promoter then made real with the "Let's Do It Again, Again!" extension.

What he has actually said about the future: he will not completely leave the stage, and select appearances and special projects remain possible, but the chapter of major tours is closed. That framing makes one-off 2027 appearances conceivable while a named 2027 tour with a full routing looks unlikely — unless his team announces otherwise.
