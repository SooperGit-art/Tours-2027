## Quick answer

**One 2027 concert confirmed — no full tour.** George Strait will headline **Boots in the Park** at Albuquerque's Balloon Fiesta Park, May 14–16, 2027 — announced September 1, 2026. It's his first New Mexico show since 2013, and he's framing it as returning to the state "one final time" (New Mexico only — he has *not* announced any retirement from performing).

He's joined on the bill by Jason Aldean, Brooks & Dunn, Zach Top, and Jon Pardi. Presale ran September 10, 2026, with general sale September 11.

Beyond that festival headline, Strait — who has played only select one-off shows since his 2014 farewell tour — has announced nothing else for 2027.
