## Quick answer

**No** — no Meshuggah tour has been formally announced for 2027 yet, but one is clearly coming. Reports say the Swedish extreme metal pioneers will tour North America in 2027 to celebrate the 40th anniversary of their inception, under the banner "The Forty-Year Anomaly Tour," with Testament and Blood Incantation tipped as support. Separately, one 2027 appearance is fully confirmed: Meshuggah headline Friday at Milwaukee Metal Fest. **October 2026 check:** the tour itself remains unannounced; only the festival headline slot and leaked listing dates exist so far.

Separately confirmed, and not to be confused with the tour: Meshuggah headline Friday at Milwaukee Metal Fest 2027, May 14–16 at The Rave/Eagles Club in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, alongside Testament, Crowbar, YOB, Pig Destroyer and Repulsion. The festival is celebrating its fifth year since its return, and Meshuggah's booking is a homecoming of sorts — the band played Milwaukee Metal Fest XII on July 25, 1998, during their first visit to the United States.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Meshuggah turn 40 in 2027, and few bands have a stronger claim to a milestone tour: the Umeå band's polyrhythmic, down-tuned approach reshaped extreme metal, and their live shows remain a rite of passage for metal fans. A 40th anniversary run with Testament and Blood Incantation would pair three generations of heavy music — thrash veterans, extreme metal innovators and modern death metal standard-bearers — in one package.

The band's recent history supports a big 2027: they played London's Royal Albert Hall in 2022 and have stayed a major festival draw, including the Milwaukee Metal Fest headline booking. The leaked listing dates (Detroit in February, Sonic Temple in Columbus in May) suggest a spring North American routing, but until the band or a promoter confirms the itinerary, this page stays marked rumored rather than dressing leaks up as fact.
