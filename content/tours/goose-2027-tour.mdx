## Quick answer

**Yes** — Goose have announced their first 2027 dates: a seven-show Spring Tour 2027 across Georgia and Florida, opening with two nights at Ameris Bank Amphitheatre in Alpharetta on April 9 and 10, then Hollywood on April 13, three nights at St. Augustine Amphitheatre on April 15–17, and closing April 18 at The BayCare Sound in Clearwater. **October 2026 check:** the spring run is confirmed and presale signup is open now; no further 2027 dates have been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Goose arrive in 2027 at a career peak. The Wilton, Connecticut, quartet wrapped a summer 2026 run with a sold-out two-night stand at Madison Square Garden — grossing $2,890,271 from 28,455 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice — plus a surprise Central Park SummerStage show and a run of sold-out Colorado headline dates. Their newest album, BIG MODERN!, is the focus of a fall 2026 tour that runs through November, followed by the annual Goosemas celebration in Atlantic City on December 11–12 and two top-billed sets at Dave Matthews & Tim Reynolds' Riviera Maya destination event in January 2027.

The spring 2027 routing plays to the band's strengths: multi-night stands in rooms where the setlist can change completely night to night, which is exactly what Goose's jam-driven audience travels for. Alpharetta and St. Augustine are proven markets for the band, and the Georgia-to-Florida swing keeps the run tight and high-demand.

## Ticket and presale info

Fans have until October 27, 2026 to sign up for the artist presale at goosetheband.com/tour; those who register receive a unique code for the presale beginning October 28 at 10 a.m. ET. The Live Nation and Ticketmaster presale begins October 29 at 10 a.m. ET, and general on-sale is October 30 at 10 a.m. ET via Ticketmaster. All presales end October 29 at 10 p.m. ET.
