## The tour so far, for context

Dion's Paris return is one of the most significant comebacks in recent pop history. Her Courage World Tour was cut short in 2020, and she stepped back from performing while managing stiff-person syndrome; her last performance before the residency was singing from the Eiffel Tower at the Paris Olympics opening ceremony in 2024. She announced the Paris residency on her 58th birthday, March 30, 2026, in a video filmed beneath the Eiffel Tower, calling it "the best birthday gift of my life."

Demand was extraordinary: the run began as 10 shows, grew to 16 dates for 2026 after an April 2026 announcement, and then added the 10 May 2027 dates on June 1, 2026. More than nine million fans registered for the ticket lottery, and the 2026 shows sold out within hours of general sale. The dates are deliberately spaced days apart — rarely two nights in a row — as a medical accommodation, giving her recovery time between performances. That spacing is exactly why a residency, rather than a touring itinerary, is the format of her return.

## Ticket and presale info

The May 2027 dates went to previously registered fans first, with dedicated artist and venue sale windows on June 3–4, 2026, and ticket and hotel experiences available from June 3 via eventtravel.com. With the 2026 shows sold out and demand for the 2027 extension running into the millions of registrations, remaining availability is limited — check celinedion.com and official sellers rather than resale listings, and be wary of unofficial "tour" packages for cities she has not announced.
