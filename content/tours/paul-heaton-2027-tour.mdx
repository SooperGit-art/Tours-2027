## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Paul Heaton is in the middle of one of the strongest runs of his four-decade career. His 2026 album *Jenius* gave him his first-ever solo UK No.1, produced by Lightning Seeds frontman Ian Broudie and recorded in Manchester. The record's 15 new songs arrive almost exactly 40 years after his first album with The Housemartins, *London 0 Hull 4* — and it has clearly re-energised his live schedule.

Heaton's back catalogue does most of the heavy lifting for ticket sales. As frontman of The Housemartins (1985–1988) he scored the Christmas No.1 with "Caravan of Love" plus "Happy Hour"; with The Beautiful South (1989–2007) he added "Rotterdam (Or Anywhere)", "Perfect 10", "Song for Whoever" and "A Little Time". His shows reliably sell out arenas across the UK and Ireland on the strength of those singalongs, and the 2027 dates lean into that: a stadium debut in his adopted home city of Leeds, two Irish arena nights, and a festival headline to open the run.
