## The tour so far, for context

Riley Green is coming off the biggest stretch of his career. His fourth studio album, That's Just Me, arrived September 18, 2026, and he recently earned his seventh No. 1 at country radio. He was announced as a 2027 Stagecoach headliner, crowned NSAI's 2026 Artist Songwriter of the Year, and picked up a CMA Awards nomination for Musical Event of the Year for his collaboration "If I Don't Leave I'm Gonna Stay." He's also currently serving as a coach on NBC's The Voice.

The That's Just Me Tour follows his 2026 Cowboy As It Gets Tour, and the step up in scale — arenas and amphitheaters across the US plus Canadian dates in Edmonton, Calgary, Saskatoon, and Toronto — reflects how far his live draw has grown. The announcement came September 28, 2026, via Live Nation.

## Ticket and presale info

Back 40 Duck Club presales began September 29, 2026, with additional presales through the week and the general on-sale on October 2, 2026, at 10 a.m. local time via rileygreenmusic.com/tour. VIP packages — premium tickets, meet-and-greets, photo ops, and VIP lounge access — are available at select shows via vipnation.com.
