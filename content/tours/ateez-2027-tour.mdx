## Quick answer

**Yes** — ATEEZ announced a 2027 world tour, ZERO TO ALL, on October 10, 2026, via their agency KQ Entertainment. It opens with three shows in Incheon on January 22–24, 2027, then moves to Tokyo (February 2–3) and Kobe (February 13–14). Europe is set for March–April, North America for July–August, and South America for September, with more dates still to be revealed. **October 2026 check:** venues, show times, and ticket-sale details have not been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

ATEEZ — Hongjoong, Seonghwa, Yunho, Yeosang, San, Mingi, Wooyoung, and Jongho — debuted in October 2018 under KQ Entertainment and have built one of the most relentless touring schedules in K-pop. They sold out a global tour just four months after debut, followed by The Expedition Tour (2019), The Fellowship: Beginning of the End (2022) and Break the Wall, and Towards the Light: Will to Power, which took them to Coachella, BMO Stadium in Los Angeles, and Citi Field in New York in 2024.

Their previous world tour, IN YOUR FANTASY, set the pattern ZERO TO ALL is following: the Incheon shows sold out across both days immediately, and every Kobe performance sold out before extra tickets were released. Demand forced added shows in Los Angeles, Orlando, Melbourne, and Sydney on the last run.

Commercially, ATEEZ are firmly in million-seller territory — THE WORLD EP.1: MOVEMENT made them million sellers, THE WORLD EP.2: OUTLAW debuted at No. 2 on the Billboard 200, and THE WORLD EP.FIN: WILL hit No. 1. With a November comeback and a freshly announced world tour, 2027 is shaping up as their biggest touring year yet.
