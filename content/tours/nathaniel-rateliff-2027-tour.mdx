## Quick answer

**Yes** — Nathaniel Rateliff is touring in 2027 on the Endless Horizon Tour: 25 North American dates, announced November 17, 2026. The run opens March 30 at the Uptown Theater in Kansas City and closes May 15 at the State Theatre in Minneapolis, with stops at New York's Beacon Theatre, Chicago's Auditorium Theatre and Toronto's Massey Hall in between.

**November 2026 check:** the full 25-date routing is confirmed and general sale opens November 20, 2026. New music was promised alongside the announcement but no album title or release date has been given yet.

## A new chapter after the Night Sweats

The Endless Horizon Tour is being billed as the next chapter for Rateliff, who has spent much of the past decade performing and recording with Nathaniel Rateliff & The Night Sweats. His most recent solo studio album, *And It's Still Alright*, came out in 2020, while the Night Sweats' 2024 album *South of Here* was named Album of the Year at the 2025 Americana Honors & Awards. "There's been a great deal of change in the last two years in my personal life and I've spent a lot of time writing about it," Rateliff said in the announcement. "I'm excited to get back out on the road and share my experience."

The routing leans into theatres rather than arenas — the Beacon, the Auditorium, Massey Hall, the Fox in Detroit — rooms that suit the big-voice, gospel-edged sound he built with the Night Sweats. The two-night stands in Atlanta (May 1–2) and Minneapolis (May 14–15) are the clearest sign of where demand is strongest. The announcement also explains the breakout: "nathaniel rateliff tour 2027 dates" jumped more than 1,300% on Google Trends the day after the news landed.

## Ticket and presale info

The artist presale began Wednesday, November 18, 2026, at 10 a.m. local time using the code HORIZON, with additional presales running through the week. General sale opens Friday, November 20, 2026, at 10 a.m. local time via nathanielrateliff.com and Ticketmaster, with VIP packages available for select dates. The two Jason Isbell co-headline shows (April 24–25) and the festival dates have separate ticketing arrangements, so check those listings individually rather than assuming the presale code covers them.
