## The tour so far, for context

Satinder Sartaaj is an internationally acclaimed Punjabi singer, poet, and composer — a PhD scholar in Punjabi music from Panjab University, Chandigarh — whose live shows blend classical Punjabi ragas, Sufi philosophy, and original poetry. His recent hits Jaiye Sajna, Guzarish, and Tere Bin Na Guzara have driven what venues describe as phenomenal worldwide success, and he follows a five-date UK tour in 2025 with this return.

Between that tour and the 2027 announcement he kept recording, releasing the Liberation EP in December 2025 and The Perfumed Moments in June 2026. The Philosophy of Resonance framing fits his stage identity: the Leeds arena listing promises a show shaped by his gift for storytelling and the rhythmic energy of his live performances, with timeless favourites like Udaarian, Jalsa, and Sai alongside the newer hits. He has previously played London's Royal Albert Hall and the Sydney Opera House, so the April 2027 arena and concert-hall rooms are a step up in scale for his UK audience.
