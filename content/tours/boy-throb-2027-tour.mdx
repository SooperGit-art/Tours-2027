## Quick answer

**Yes** — Boy Throb are touring in 2027. The viral boy band announced the Real Tour on November 2, 2026: 20 dates across the US and Canada, opening March 10 in Minneapolis and closing April 10 in Denver, with two Canadian stops in Toronto and Montreal.

**November 2026 check:** the full 20-date itinerary below is officially announced. No dates beyond North America, and no additional legs, have been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Boy Throb's story is inseparable from their 2027 tour. The group began posting videos in October 2025, with Anthony Key, Evan Papier, and Zachary Sobania performing in matching pink tracksuits while fourth member Darshan Magdum joined remotely from India. Bringing Magdum to America became the central thread of their videos, which mixed pop covers with appeals for help supporting his visa application, and the campaign built them more than 2 million followers across TikTok and Instagram.

The wait ended in 2026: Magdum's O-1 visa was approved on August 3, and on October 16 he performed with the group in person for the first time at American Dream in East Rutherford, New Jersey, at an event dubbed Darshan Day. The band then set out on a Great American Road Tour of meet-and-greets, signed with Kesha Records, and celebrated their one-year anniversary with a Los Angeles event they called the Throbiversary. The Real Tour is the payoff — their first proper headlining run with all four members on stage together.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets go on sale in three waves, all at 10 a.m. local time: a Day 1 Throbbers fan club presale on Wednesday, November 4, 2026, an artist presale on Thursday, November 5, and general sale on Friday, November 6, via Ticketmaster and the band's official website.
