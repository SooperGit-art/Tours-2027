**Yes** — Xavier Naidoo returns to arenas in spring 2027 with his Live 2027 run: six dates from February 21 in Vienna to March 7 in Hamburg, taking in Munich, Stuttgart, Zurich and Mannheim along the way. ## The tour so far, for context

Naidoo's return to the stage is one of the more remarkable comeback stories in German pop. Once one of Germany's most successful soul singers, he stepped away from live performance for six years amid controversy over conspiracy content, before promoter Live Nation GSA announced a single exclusive comeback concert at Cologne's Lanxess Arena for December 16, 2025. Demand exploded that one show into a 13-date arena run across Germany, Austria and Switzerland under the title Bei Meiner Seele, selling more than 150,000 tickets, with the run closing in his hometown of Mannheim at the SAP Arena in January 2026.

Live 2027 is the direct successor to that comeback: the same arena scale, the same German-speaking markets, and several of the same rooms — Munich's Olympiahalle, Stuttgart's Schleyer-Halle, Zurich's Hallenstadion and Mannheim's SAP Arena all hosted the comeback run too. The difference is the billing: this is no longer a one-off return stretched by demand, but a planned arena tour announced in advance, with a presale date set before the first show is three months away.

## Ticket and presale info

The presale starts on Thursday, November 5, 2026, at 10:00 on eventim.de, according to CTS Eventim, with Ticketmaster Switzerland confirming the same start time for the Zurich date. Secondary marketplaces are already listing the six dates, but their prices are set by sellers and may sit above face value, so fans should buy through the official presale first. No ticket prices have been published in the announcement coverage, so treat any price quoted before the presale as a resale listing, not a face value.
