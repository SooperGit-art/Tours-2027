## Quick answer

**Yes** — Teddy Swims has officially confirmed his biggest-ever European and UK headline run: **The UGLY Tour**. The EU/UK leg now spans 24 arena shows from March 2 to April 25, 2027, opening in Oslo and closing at The O2 in London — after second nights were added in Manchester and London due to presale demand selling out initial allocations within hours.

<Callout type="confirmed">
- 24-date EU/UK arena tour, March 2 – April 25, 2027
- Opens Oslo (Unity Arena), closes London (The O2)
- Second nights added: Manchester (Apr 17–18, three nights total), London (Apr 21, 22, 24, 25 — four nights); second nights also added in Stockholm, Cologne, Amsterdam, Antwerp, and Birmingham
- Artist presale: 10am local, Wed July 29, 2026. General sale: 10am local, Fri July 31, 2026, via Ticketmaster/AXS
- Confirmed pricing so far: Glasgow £38.55–£319.00, Dublin €59.90–€89.90
</Callout>

<Callout type="rumor" title="⚠️ Not yet announced">
- Support acts for the EU/UK leg
- Ticket pricing for London, Birmingham, and Manchester
- Setlist — no official setlist has been released for this tour
</Callout>

This follows a sold-out 32-date North American leg and lands right as Swims has been dominating festival stages — Latitude, Isle Of Wight — off the back of his latest singles "Mr. Know It All" and "Break Up In Reverse."

## Full EU/UK tour schedule

The complete 24-date list is in the table above, sourced directly from Official Charts and cross-checked against Ticketmaster's own listing for the London date. A few standouts:

- **Two-night stands** in Stockholm and Cologne — a sign of strong demand in those markets
- **UK leg** runs Birmingham → Dublin → Glasgow → Manchester → London, all in the second half of April
- Every EU date lands on a Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday, or Saturday — no Monday or Friday shows on this leg

The tour arrives on the back of Swims' most successful chart run yet, following his 2025 album *I've Tried Everything But Therapy (Part 2)*. Announcing the run, Swims told fans directly he was looking forward to seeing them in March and April.
