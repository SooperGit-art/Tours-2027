## The tour so far, for context

Steps — Claire Richards, Faye Tozer, Ian "H" Watkins, Lee Latchford-Evans and Lisa Scott-Lee — are celebrating 30 years since their debut single "5, 6, 7, 8" was released in November 1997, and Steps 30 Live is billed as their biggest tour ever. The group last toured in 2021, and Claire Richards says they have been planning this anniversary run since then: "it's going to be our biggest and best yet. You can expect ALL the hits." The setlist promises the full catalogue — "5, 6, 7, 8", "Tragedy", "One for Sorrow", "Stomp", "Deeper Shade of Blue" and "Scared of the Dark" — across 14 Top 10 singles including two Number 1s, and four Number 1 albums spanning four consecutive decades.

The support bill is a full-circle moment: B*Witched and A1 both supported Steps on their very first tour, and all three acts return together for the anniversary run. The tour is only one part of the 30th anniversary plans — Steps have also signed a three-part documentary deal with Amazon, produced by Blast Films, premiering exclusively on Prime Video in more than 240 countries after the tour, and they will headline BBC One's The Big NYE Party alongside Blue, hosted by Rylan and Michelle Visage, to kick off the anniversary year. Before that, they perform a greatest hits megamix at the Royal Variety Performance at London's Royal Albert Hall in front of King Charles and Queen Camilla.

The anniversary follows the success of the group's jukebox musical HERE & NOW, which brought their catalogue to the stage for more than 250 performances across the UK and Ireland during 2025 and 2026.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets go on general sale Friday, November 20, 2026, at 10am GMT via stepsofficial.co.uk, with a presale from Wednesday, November 18, 2026, at 10am GMT for fans who sign up on the band's site. Standard tickets start from around £62.50, with premium tickets and VIP packages — including soundcheck access, Q&A sessions and photo opportunities — available at higher price points. O2 Priority members can also buy early on November 18 without a separate code. The London O2 date on November 26 and the Manchester AO Arena date on November 10 — which falls on the 30th anniversary of "5, 6, 7, 8" month — are the likeliest to sell out first.
