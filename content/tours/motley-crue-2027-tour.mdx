## The band's 2026 run and 45th anniversary, for context

Mötley Crüe spent 2026 celebrating their 45th anniversary with the "Return of the Carnival of Sins" tour — a 33-show North American run from July 17 (Burgettstown, Pennsylvania) to September 26 (Ridgefield, Washington) that marked the 20th anniversary of the original 2005–2006 Carnival of Sins outing. The tour was the band's big statement of the post-Mick Mars era: with guitarist John 5 now firmly installed alongside Vince Neil, Nikki Sixx and Tommy Lee, the Crüe played some of their biggest headline shows in years.

On the release side, the band put out the three-track *Cancelled* EP in 2024 — John 5's recorded debut with the group — and signed with Big Machine Records in 2025. Their last full studio album remains *Saints of Los Angeles* (2008), and Sixx told Louder in August 2026 that fans shouldn't expect another full-length: the plan going forward is singles and short EPs "whenever inspiration strikes," since, in his view, most listeners only ever hear a single track off an album anyway.

That stance, plus Sixx's mid-2026 comments about possibly stepping off the road in 2027 ("I love playing live but never have been a fan of being on the road for any real length of time"), is why the 2027 calendar currently holds just the one Oslo festival date. With the Tons of Rock 2027 lineup also featuring Judas Priest, Turnstile, Lynyrd Skynyrd and Helloween, the band clearly isn't closing the door on 2027 live work entirely — they're just not committing to a full tour yet.
