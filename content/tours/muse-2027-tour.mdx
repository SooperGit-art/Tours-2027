## The tour so far, for context

Muse's 2027 dates are the second leg of the touring cycle behind their tenth studio album, The Wow! Signal, which arrived in June 2026 and drew strong reviews. The band spent summer 2026 touring North America, wrapping up at the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles at the end of August, and launched the European cycle with an intimate Brixton Academy show earlier in 2026 — their first performance at the London venue since 2001.

The UK and mainland European arena leg runs through November and December 2026 (Manchester's Co-op Live, The O2 in London, Berlin, Milan, Düsseldorf, Paris, Amsterdam, Montpellier and Zurich) before the tour picks back up in January 2027. Matt Bellamy has teased an ambitious space-themed stage production for these shows, telling the press this summer the band is building a spaceship set with new laser work that has "never been done before."

The announcement came just days after the band wrapped North America, which suggests the 2026-2027 cycle could keep growing — but the January 2027 arena dates and the Eurockéennes headline slot are the only 2027 appearances officially confirmed so far.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for the eight January 2027 arena dates went on sale via a registered-fan presale starting October 6, 2026, with general sale beginning Thursday, October 8, 2026 at 9am local time through the usual Live Nation ticketing channels. Eurockéennes 2027 festival tickets go on sale October 9, 2026 via the festival's website. As with the band's previous European runs, the multi-night arena stands (two shows each in Bordeaux, Lyon and Madrid) mean second dates can open up if the first nights sell through, so registering for the band's mailing list via the official site is the best way to catch added shows.
