## Quick answer

**Yes** — Till Lindemann, frontman of Rammstein, has announced his World Tour 2027: more than 50 concerts worldwide, with a confirmed 33-date European leg running October 2 in Riga through December 12 in Prague. The tour is billed as his biggest solo run to date, following a 2025 solo tour that sold more than 200,000 tickets in Europe. **October 2026 check:** the European dates are confirmed and on sale from October 28, 2026; dates on other continents, where the tour begins in spring 2027, have not been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Lindemann's solo career has grown steadily since he launched the Lindemann project with Peter Tagtgren in 2015, releasing the albums Skills in Pills (2015) and F & M (2019). After that collaboration ended in 2020, he continued solo, releasing his first full solo album Zunge in November 2023 and touring Europe extensively, including a Paris show at the Accor Arena in December 2023 and a return to the French capital in November 2025. Each solo run has been bigger than the last — promoter Handwerker Promotion's first Lindemann tour was 18 shows, the next 25, and now 33 European dates in 22 countries.

The scale reflects demand: his previous "Meine Welt" solo tour sold more than 200,000 tickets across Europe, and the theatrical, high-intensity stage production has become a draw in its own right, distinct from the Rammstein machine. A world tour in 2027, opening on other continents in the spring before the European autumn leg, is the logical next step.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for the German, Austrian and Swiss dates go on sale October 28, 2026 at 4 p.m. exclusively at till-lindemann.myticket.de. Helsinki tickets are priced from 65.50 euros, and that show is restricted to attendees aged 18 and over — check the age policy on your city's listing before buying, as restrictions vary by venue.
