## Why a spring 2027 MercyMe tour makes sense

MercyMe is one of Christian music's most durable touring acts. Formed in 1994 and signed in 2001, the band broke mainstream with "I Can Only Imagine," a song that later inspired a No. 3 box-office film in 2018 and became the first Christian song to go double-platinum digitally. They have won eight Dove Awards and racked up dozens of No. 1 Christian radio singles.

The 2027 run follows a heavy 2025–26 cycle: their 12th studio album Wonder & Awe arrived in August 2025, the fall 2026 leg featured Jeremy Camp as support, and new single "Holding On to Me" landed in 2026. Rather than taking a break, the band is rolling straight into a spring 2027 arena run with two younger Christian acts — Ben Fuller and Peter Burton — a classic MercyMe move of pairing their headline draw with rising worship artists.
