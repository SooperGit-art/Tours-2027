## Quick answer

**Yes** — Tinashe is officially touring in 2027. The Show Must Go On Tour covers 18 North American dates from April 14 (Dallas) to May 15 (Los Angeles), hitting theaters and mid-size halls across the South, Midwest, East Coast, Mountain West and Pacific Coast, plus a Vancouver, Canada stop. **October 2026 check:** all dates are confirmed and on sale; a reported May 8 Portland stop still has no venue attached and is not on official ticketing listings yet.

## Momentum into 2027

Tinashe enters this tour with genuine momentum. Her "Popstar" era delivered a strong Billboard debut and a run of sold-out 2026 headline shows — including the One Night With a Popstar dates in Brooklyn and Hollywood — built on the choreography-forward, staging-heavy live presentation that has become her signature. The combination of R&B songwriting, precise dance performance and theatrical staging has made her one of the most compelling live performers in contemporary pop-R&B, and The Show Must Go On is designed to scale that presentation up across a full North American theater run.

The routing itself tells the story of her audience: Texas gets three dates to open the tour, the East Coast corridor runs DC–Philadelphia–Boston in three nights, and the West Coast closes with two Oakland nights and a Los Angeles finale at the Shrine Expo Hall. The Vancouver inclusion keeps Canadian fans in the picture after the 2026 momentum.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets are on sale now. The artist presale began October 20, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time, with general sales opening October 21 at 10 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster. Note for Vancouver buyers: the May 10 show was moved to Freedom Mobile Arch after initial listings showed a different venue — confirm the venue on your ticket before traveling. The added second Oakland night (May 14) is the clearest signal of demand, so the remaining single-night theater dates are the ones to prioritize.
