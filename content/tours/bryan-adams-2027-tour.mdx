## Quick answer

**Yes** — the Bryan Adams Tour 2027 is officially announced. His Roll With The Punches World Tour extends into 2027 with 10 confirmed shows across Australia and New Zealand, running from February 23 in Perth to March 12 in Christchurch. Natalie Imbruglia joins as special guest on every date. **October 2026 check:** no North American, European or other 2027 legs have been announced beyond the Australia/NZ run, though his official site also lists Moldova, Georgia and Kazakhstan legs for 2027 with dates still to come.

## The tour so far, for context

The Roll With The Punches Tour is named after Adams' 16th studio album, Roll With The Punches, which reached the top three on the UK charts. The Canadian rock veteran played 140 shows worldwide in 2025 and is running the tour through the USA, Canada and Europe across 2026 before bringing the show back Down Under in 2027. The scale of the schedule matches Adams' reputation as one of rock's busiest touring artists — he has said he keeps taking on more shows because "the wheels are in motion and everybody knows what they're doing and we have a good time."

New music is also on the way. Adams' official site is promoting How's It Goin', a new album due November 6, and he has released "51st State," a standalone track written for an upcoming documentary that he described as a love letter to Canada. Fans at the 2027 shows can expect a setlist spanning more than four decades — from "Summer of '69," "Run to You" and "Heaven" to "(Everything I Do) I Do It For You" and "Have You Ever Really Loved a Woman" — alongside material from the new album.

## Ticket and presale info

General sale tickets are on sale now. In Australia, tickets are available through Ticketek and in New Zealand through Ticketmaster, with the official Frontier Touring page carrying full pricing and VIP package details. Australian reserve seating ranges from $109.90 to $189.90 AUD, with VIP packages up to $750.65 for the Life is Beautiful Q&A Experience, which includes early entry, an intimate Q&A with an acoustic performance, a production tour and an autographed deluxe vinyl of the album. The promoter notes all shows are licensed all ages, with a limit of 8 reserve seats or 4 VIP packages per purchaser per show. Buy only through the authorised sellers — Frontier Touring explicitly warns it cannot guarantee tickets purchased anywhere else.
