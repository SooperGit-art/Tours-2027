## Quick answer

**Yes** — Billy Strings has 25 shows confirmed for 2027 across two runs: a 10-show Winter tour from February 4 to 21, and a 15-date Spring tour from April 6 to June 11. The Winter leg is built around multi-night stands in Asheville, Duluth and Nashville, while the Spring leg adds new markets like Tampa and St. Louis. **November 2026 check:** both legs are fully announced with venues and on-sale dates; the only 2027 dates still to watch are any summer or fall additions.

## The tour so far, for context

Billy Strings, the three-time Grammy-winning guitarist and singer, has become one of the biggest live draws in American roots music, selling out arenas with a bluegrass sound stretched through jam-band improvisation. His fifth studio album, *So Much for Goodbyes*, arrived August 28, 2026 via Reprise Records, and the Winter 2027 tour was announced the same day — a deliberate pairing that turned the album release into the launch of a full touring cycle.

The routing shows how his audience has grown: the Winter leg leans on multi-night stands in rooms he has already sold out, including five nights in Asheville and a closing night at Nashville's Ryman Auditorium, while the Spring leg, announced November 10, 2026, pushes into fresh arena and amphitheatre markets like Tampa and St. Louis. It is his biggest 2027 schedule yet, and the November announcement drove the "billy strings tour 2027" query to breakout status on Google Trends.

## Ticket and presale info

Spring tour tickets work differently from a normal on-sale. General admission pit and floor tickets are available only through Ticketmaster Request, except at the St. Augustine Amphitheatre, where the entire venue uses Fair AXS. Sign-ups for both programs closed November 11, 2026 at 11:59pm ET. Presales for all other sections begin November 18 at 10am local time, with general on-sale following on Friday, November 20 at 10am local time.

Winter tour tickets went on presale in September 2026, with general on-sale on September 11, 2026. Resale prices for the Asheville and Ryman nights are already running high, so the official Ticketmaster Request and Fair AXS routes are the safest way in.
