## Quick answer

**Yes** — Carlos Santana has announced a full 2027 tour: the Planet of Free Will Global Tour, a 13-date European run opening June 30, 2027, at London's The O2 and closing July 31, 2027, in Helsinki, Finland. The announcement came October 26, 2026, and runs alongside his ongoing Las Vegas residency, which continues into its 14th year with January and May 2027 blocks.

**October 2026 check:** all 13 European dates are confirmed, with presales underway and most general sales opening October 30, 2026. No North American tour dates beyond the Las Vegas residency have been announced for 2027.

## The tour so far, for context

Carlos Santana is a Grammy-winning, Rock and Roll Hall of Fame guitarist whose band has carried his signature Latin-infused rock sound to arenas and festivals for more than five decades. The Planet of Free Will tour is his first newly announced global run for 2027, and it leans on the European festival and arena circuit: after London it moves through Paris, a Swiss festival stop at Kestenholz, German arenas and open-air stages, Italian summer festivals at Pordenone and Lucca, and on through Austria, the Czech Republic, Poland, Norway, and Finland.

The tour sits alongside one of the longest-running residencies in Las Vegas. Santana's An Intimate Evening with Santana: Greatest Hits Live residency at the House of Blues inside Mandalay Bay resumed in November 2026 and continues with two 2027 blocks — January 20-31 and May 12-23 — meaning fans have two very different ways to see him in 2027: the intimate Las Vegas room or the big European summer stages.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for most European dates went on fan presale October 27, 2026, ahead of general sale on October 30, 2026, at 10am local time. Some shows were already on sale at announcement, including Pordenone, Lucca, and Helsinki, while Paris general sale begins November 20. London O2 tickets were released through Planet Rock Tickets at 9:30am on October 30, 2026. Check Santana's official site and the venue's own listing for each city, as on-sale timings vary by market.
