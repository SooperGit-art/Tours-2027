## The tour so far, for context

Dashboard Confessional began as Chris Carrabba's solo acoustic side project while he played in Further Seems Forever, and the 2002 MTV *Unplugged* performance turned it into a defining moment for the emo generation. That set — just Carrabba, a guitar and a room full of fans singing every word — brought the band to a mainstream audience, earned their first RIAA platinum certification and their first No. 1 on Billboard's Heatseekers and Top Independent Albums charts, and made songs like "Hands Down" and "Vindicated" permanent fixtures of 2000s rock.

Carrabba has kept the unplugged spirit alive with small solo acoustic shows in Nashville, New York and Los Angeles in 2026, and the 2027 tour scales that intimacy back up: the band arrangements from the original *Unplugged* performance, played in rooms like Massey Hall, the Hollywood Palladium and The Masonic. It is a deliberately backward-looking tour — a 25th anniversary celebration rather than an album cycle — which is exactly why the rooms are the size they are.
