## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Josiah Queen has become one of the defining voices of a new generation of Christian music, with more than 2.1 billion global streams. His breakout single "The Prodigal" passed 200 million streams, and "Dusty Bibles" became his first Billboard Hot 100 hit, earning RIAA Gold certification in under six months and landing among the Top 3 Christian/Gospel streaming songs in the US. The song also charted on Spotify's Viral 50 in more than 15 countries — a sign his audience has pushed well beyond the Christian music core.

The Damascus Road Tour follows his new album Damascus Road, which explores what it means to encounter God and live differently after that encounter. Queen said he wants the shows to feel like more than a concert — an invitation for people to walk in one way and leave changed. That framing, plus the leap from clubs and theaters to arenas for the first time, makes this his biggest touring statement yet.

The spring timing fits the genre's calendar too: Christian arena tours often anchor around spring and fall windows when festivals and church-based promotion run strongest. With public tickets on sale since September 2026, the long lead time gives the tour a full winter of demand-building.

## Ticket and presale info

The tour presale began Monday, September 14, 2026, with venue presales rolling out through the week (Dickies Arena in Fort Worth, for example, held its venue presale September 17). Tickets went on sale to the general public on Friday, September 18, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time via josiahqueen.com and major retailers. Shows are scheduled for 7:00 p.m. local time.
