## Quick answer

**Yes, with an important distinction** — Dave Matthews has official 2027 dates, but they are solo shows, not a full Dave Matthews Band tour. An Acoustic Evening With Dave Matthews plays four Japanese cities January 6–11, 2027: Tokyo, Osaka, Kyoto and Nagoya. The full band's 2027 summer tour has not been announced.

**November 2026 check:** the Japan dates are confirmed on the band's official site. The other confirmed 2027 event is the Dave & Tim Riviera Maya getaway, January 20–23, 2027 in Cancún — a duo event, not a band tour.

## The tour so far, for context

The Japan run is a genuine first: Dave Matthews has never performed in Japan before, and the four January 2027 shows are solo acoustic evenings rather than band shows. They were announced September 29, 2026, with tickets through a presale lottery and general sale in mid-October. The Riviera Maya event, meanwhile, is a decade-old tradition — Matthews and guitarist Tim Reynolds, collaborators for more than three decades, return to Moon Palace Cancún for four nights, headlining three of them on a beachfront stage, with Goose opening the festivities with two sets on the first night.

The band's main business remains the summer amphitheatre run, and 2026 was a full touring year for them. A 2027 full-band announcement would follow the usual pattern on davematthewsband.com — and as of this check, there is nothing there.

## Ticket and presale info

Japan show tickets went on general sale October 17, 2026 at 10 a.m. Japan time via eplus, after a presale lottery that closed October 12. Riviera Maya is sold as all-inclusive packages — accommodation, performances, airport transfers, food and drink — through the event's official site, daveandtimrivieramaya.com. For a full-band tour, the only safe source is davematthewsband.com/tour; third-party listings claiming 2027 DMB summer dates are not verified.
