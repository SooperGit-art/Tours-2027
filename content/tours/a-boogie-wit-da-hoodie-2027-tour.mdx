## The tour so far, for context

A Boogie Wit Da Hoodie — Bronx rapper Artist Julius Dubose — has been one of hip-hop's most consistent hitmakers since his 2016 breakout mixtape *Artist* and the single "My Shit". Signed to his own Highbridge the Label and Atlantic, he has stacked multi-platinum singles and albums, including *The Bigger Artist* (2017), *Hoodie SZN* (2018) and *Artist 2.0* (2020), and headlined arenas in North America and Europe.

The 10 Years of Artist tour is a career retrospective as much as a tour: the North American leg runs through the end of 2026, with dates in Philadelphia, Boston, Toronto, Orlando, Minneapolis, New York and Hartford, before the run crosses the Atlantic. The two UK dates are his biggest British headline rooms yet — the O2 in London and the Co-op Live in Manchester — a step up from the Better Off Alone Tour, which visited the UK in 2024.
