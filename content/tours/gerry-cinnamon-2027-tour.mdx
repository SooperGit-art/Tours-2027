## The tour so far, for context

Gerry Cinnamon's rise remains one of the great independent success stories in British music. From Glasgow's Castlemilk, he built his audience without a major label — crowdfunding his debut Erratic Cinematic in 2017, launching his own label Little Runaway, and taking the acoustic-guitar-and-loop-pedal show from the John Peel Stage at Glastonbury 2019 to a full UK and Ireland arena tour. The Bonny (2020) went to number one in the UK and Ireland and was the UK's fastest-selling vinyl of the year.

The summer of 2022 was the peak: two nights at Hampden Park, 50,000-plus crowds, the biggest Scottish shows of his career. Then the 2024 festival appearances, the Sheffield Academy show in November 2023 — and quiet. The October 2026 search surge suggests the appetite for a comeback is very much alive; whether Cinnamon himself feels it is anyone's guess.
