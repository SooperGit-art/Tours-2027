## Quick answer

**Yes** — Foo Fighters are confirmed touring in 2027, as the continuation of their sprawling **Take Cover Tour**. The 2027 portion now covers nine shows: three closing dates on the Australia/New Zealand run (Christchurch, Auckland, Perth in January), two India dates, two Brazil stadium shows (Belo Horizonte and São Paulo in February), and a four-date South American stadium leg (Montevideo, Buenos Aires, Santiago, Lima) announced in August 2026.

## Two things worth correcting before anything else

<Callout type="warning" title="🚫 Rock in Rio is 2026, not 2027">
Foo Fighters headline Rock in Rio's Palco Mundo on **September 4, 2026** — confirmed by Wikipedia's dedicated Take Cover Tour article and four separate Brazilian outlets (Billboard Brasil, TMDQA, Folha Vitória, Terra), all in agreement. Some tour-tracker sites report this as a 2027 date; it isn't.
</Callout>

<Callout type="confirmed" title="✅ South America leg verified (August 2026)">
The four-date South American stadium leg — Montevideo (Feb 23), Buenos Aires (Feb 25), Santiago (Feb 28), Lima (Mar 3) — was officially announced in early August 2026 via foofighters.com, with tickets on sale from August 13. Each date has regional support acts (Niña Lobo, Gamepad, Usted Señalemelo, Pacifica, Adelaida, Aurora Voraz, Cometa a la Deriva, Lucy Perez, and others). This page previously flagged it as unverified; it is now confirmed and listed in the table above.
</Callout>

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Foo Fighters touring in 2027?**
Yes, confirmed — five shows: the January tail of their Australia/New Zealand run (Christchurch, Auckland, Perth) plus two Brazil stadium shows in February (Belo Horizonte, São Paulo).

**Is Foo Fighters playing Rock in Rio in 2027?**
No — Rock in Rio is September 4, 2026, not 2027. Some low-quality sites report this incorrectly as a 2027 date; it's confirmed for 2026 across Wikipedia and multiple Brazilian music outlets.

**Is Foo Fighters touring South America (Uruguay, Argentina, Chile, Peru) in 2027?**
Not confirmed. One site (toursetlist.com) claims a four-country South America leg with specific opening acts, but this isn't corroborated by any official source, Wikipedia, or reputable music press — treat it as unverified.

**What is the Take Cover Tour?**
Foo Fighters' current touring cycle, supporting their twelfth studio album, Your Favorite Toy (released April 24, 2026). It's a massive, multi-leg run spanning Europe, North America, Oceania, and South America across 2026–2027.

**When do Brazil 2027 tickets go on sale?**
Fan presale: August 17, 2026. Santander Select/Private presale: August 18. General Santander presale: August 19. General public sale: August 20 — all at 10am local time.

**Who's opening in Brazil?**
Karen Dió and Deb and the Mentals, on both Brazil dates.

**Is this Foo Fighters' full Australia/New Zealand tour?**
No — this page covers only the January 2027 tail end. The tour's first leg ran November–December 2026 (Brisbane, Townsville, Sydney, Newcastle, Melbourne, Adelaide), which falls outside this site's 2027 scope.
