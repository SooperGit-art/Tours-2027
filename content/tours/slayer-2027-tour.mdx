## The tour so far, for context

Slayer formed in Huntington Park, California, in 1981 and became one of thrash metal's "Big Four" alongside Metallica, Megadeth and Anthrax, defining extreme metal with albums like *Reign in Blood* (1986) and *South of Heaven* (1988). In January 2018 the band announced a farewell world tour, played their final show in November 2019, and disbanded — and Kerry King said in a 2024 interview that Slayer would "never tour again".

The reunion has instead been deliberately limited: a pair of huge UK comeback shows in Cardiff and London in 2025, festival appearances, and the short late-2026 run of arena and stadium dates in the US and South America. That pattern — a few high-profile shows a year, no full tour — is the honest context for 2027. In March 2026, talent agency Independent Artist Group signed Slayer, Mastodon and King's solo band for representation outside the US and Canada, and noted King would tour both with Slayer and as a solo artist. So far only the solo tour has a 2027 window attached.
