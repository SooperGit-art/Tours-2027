## Eminem's touring reality, for context

Eminem has not been a regular touring act for years. His last full-scale outing was the Rapture tour in 2019, and since then his live appearances have been curated one-off events: a BMO Stadium show in Los Angeles in August 2024 and an appearance in Bahrain in December 2024 are his most recent documented performances. That pattern matters because a "2027 world tour" announcement would represent a major shift in how he does live music — and no such announcement has happened.

His creative output has stayed steady even without touring. The Death of Slim Shady (Coup de Grace), his 12th studio album, released in July 2024, and in October 2026 he surfaced on a guest verse for Kid Rock's Halfway 2 Jesus. Neither appearance came with any touring news. Eminem remains one of the few superstars whose absence from the road is the norm, not a story — so "nothing announced" is the baseline, and a 2027 tour would be genuine news.
