## The tour so far, for context

The Rankin Family hail from Mabou, a small community on Cape Breton Island, Nova Scotia, where nine of the twelve Rankin children played ceilidhs, weddings and dances from the 1970s onward. Their self-titled debut arrived in 1989, and *Fare Thee Well Love* made them a Canadian institution after its 1992 reissue — 2027 marks 35 years since that breakthrough. Along the way they collected 15 East Coast Music Awards and six Juno Awards, and in 2025 they were inducted into the Cape Breton Music Industry Hall of Fame.

The siblings last toured nationally together around their 2007 reunion album and tour, so the 2027 run is a genuine reunion: Jimmy, Cookie and Heather Rankin back on the road together after nearly 15 years apart as a touring unit. The routing is deliberately coast-to-coast, opening with five British Columbia dates in early March before crossing the Prairies, Ontario, Quebec and the Maritimes, and closing April 3 in Sydney, Nova Scotia — bringing the family home to Cape Breton.
