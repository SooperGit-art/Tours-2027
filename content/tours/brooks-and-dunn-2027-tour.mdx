## Quick answer

**Yes** — Brooks & Dunn are confirmed for 2027, and the headline news is Australia: the country duo bring the Neon Moon Tour to Sydney (March 24–25, Afterpay Arena) and Melbourne (March 27–28, Rod Laver Arena), their first Australian visit in 18 years. They also headline the 20th-anniversary CMC Rocks QLD festival (March 19–21), with two US festival dates in Texas and New Mexico to follow. **October 2026 check:** all four arena dates are confirmed and on sale; the exact CMC Rocks QLD performance date has not been published yet.

## Three decades in, still breaking records

Kix Brooks and Ronnie Dunn remain the best-selling duo in country music history, with a Grammy-winning catalog built on "Boot Scootin' Boogie" (5x Platinum), "My Maria" and "Neon Moon" (8x Platinum). 2026 was a landmark year for them: the 35th anniversary of their debut album Brand New Man — now getting its first-ever vinyl pressing — plus a first-ever UK run (headlining C2C to record crowds in Belfast, Glasgow and London), a Stagecoach headline set, nearly a dozen dates with Morgan Wallen, and the longest-running country residency in Las Vegas history.

Their recent REBOOT II project, which invited contemporary stars like Lainey Wilson, Morgan Wallen, Megan Moroney and Jelly Roll to reinterpret their classics, pushed the catalog to a new generation — part of why an 18-year absence hasn't cooled Australian demand. Support act Mack Geiger, the 21-year-old Queenslander whose 2025 single passed 50 million streams, cites Brooks & Dunn as a direct influence, which makes the pairing feel like a deliberate torch-passing.

## Ticket and presale info

The Australian arena dates are on sale now through Frontier Touring: member presales ran October 8, 2026, with the general on-sale opening October 9 at 10 a.m. local time. Demand was strong enough to add second Sydney and Melbourne shows, so remaining inventory will be the added dates. For CMC Rocks QLD, tickets are sold through the festival. The US festival appearances (Two Step Inn, Boots in the Park) sell through their respective festivals. VIP packages are available for select Australian arena shows.
