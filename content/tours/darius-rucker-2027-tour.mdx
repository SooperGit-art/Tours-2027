## Quick answer

**Yes** — Darius Rucker is playing live in 2027, just not on a solo tour. His rock supergroup Howl Owl Howl — Rucker with R.E.M.'s Mike Mills and The Black Crowes' Steve Gorman — announced a seven-date US run for February 2027 on November 12, 2026, running February 3–13 from Milwaukee to Memphis. Separately, Hootie & the Blowfish announced five UK concerts for May 2027 on October 30, 2026, ending at London's Royal Albert Hall. **November 2026 check:** no solo Darius Rucker 2027 tour has been announced; his 2027 dates are the two band runs below.

## The tour so far, for context

Darius Rucker has spent his career moving between two worlds: frontman of Hootie & the Blowfish, one of the best-selling rock bands of the 1990s, and a Grammy-winning solo country star since his 2008 crossover. Howl Owl Howl is his third lane — a roots-rock supergroup with two fellow lifers, R.E.M. multi-instrumentalist Mike Mills and Black Crowes co-founder Steve Gorman — and the February 2027 run is the group's first headline tour, built around small and mid-size rooms like Milwaukee's Rave Hall, Columbus's Newport Music Hall and Memphis's Minglewood Hall rather than the arenas Rucker usually plays.

The Hootie dates tell a different story: a short UK arena run in May 2027, announced October 30, 2026, taking in Glasgow's O2 Academy, Bristol's O2 Academy, Manchester's Albert Hall, Birmingham's O2 Academy and London's Royal Albert Hall, with the Spin Doctors joining as special guests at all five shows. It is the band's first UK run in years and a reminder that the Hootie catalogue — "Hold My Hand", "Let Her Cry", "Only Wanna Be with You" — still draws arena crowds overseas.

Fans searching this week should ignore one prominent result: dariusruckertour2027.us advertises a "Homegrown Harmony Tour" beginning April 8, 2027, with dates at Nashville's Bridgestone Arena, Madison Square Garden, the Hollywood Bowl and The O2 with Ed Sheeran. The page itself admits it created an "imagined scenario" because no real details existed — it is fabricated, and none of its dates are real.

## Ticket and presale info

Howl Owl Howl tickets went on general sale Friday, November 13, 2026, at 10am local time via HowlOwlHowl.com, a day after the tour was announced alongside the "Everybody Wants Her" single. Hootie & the Blowfish UK tickets are on sale through the band's official website. With only seven Howl Owl Howl dates in rooms as small as The Orange Peel and The Mill & Mine, the February run is the likeliest to sell out first. No solo Rucker tickets exist to buy, because no solo tour has been announced.
