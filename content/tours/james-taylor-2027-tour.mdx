## The tour so far, for context

James Taylor has been touring steadily for more than five decades, and the 2027 run follows his familiar pattern: a short US leg in the spring, then a European summer run built around historic venues. The Italy leg is the centrepiece — seven cities in just over two weeks, including the ancient amphitheatre at Pompeii on June 18, a show already being billed locally as one of the summer's headline concerts.

The announcement came a week after Taylor wrapped his 2026 US dates, and the Italian presale moved fast: TicketOne opened presales on November 23, 2026, with Pompeii tickets listed from around €69.57 to €100 before fees. The New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival appearance, announced the same day, puts him on a bill that also features Foo Fighters and The Who.
