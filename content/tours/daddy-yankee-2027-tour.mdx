## The comeback, for context

Daddy Yankee was the first reggaeton artist to take the genre on an international tour, playing more than 29 countries and turning a Puerto Rican sound into a global mainstream force. Then, at the height of his fame, he walked away: at the final show of his farewell tour in December 2023, in front of more than 18,000 people at Puerto Rico's Coliseo, he said in tears that he was leaving reggaeton behind to follow Jesus.

Three years later, the return is taking shape in stages rather than all at once. *La Postal*, his 15-track album exploring different forms of love, arrived October 30, 2026, with Cardi B, Myke Towers, Bizarrap, Danny Ocean and Diljit Dosanjh among the guests. The Latin Recording Academy named him its 2026 Person of the Year. And on November 10, Coachella unveiled its 2027 lineup with Daddy Yankee billed as a fourth headliner — a special production "in the desert," separate from the Friday, Saturday and Sunday headliners.
