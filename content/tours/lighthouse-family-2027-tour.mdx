## A comeback three decades in the making

Lighthouse Family — vocalist Tunde Baiyewu and songwriter, keyboardist and producer Paul Tucker — formed in Newcastle in the early 1990s and became one of Britain's most successful pop-soul partnerships, selling more than 15 million albums worldwide. Their 1995 debut Ocean Drive went six-times platinum in the UK on the strength of "Lifted," "Ocean Drive" and "Goodbye Heartbreak"; the 1997 follow-up Postcards from Heaven matched that success with "High," "Raincloud" and "Lost in Space."

After Whatever Gets You Through the Day (2001), the duo went quiet for nearly two decades, returning in 2019 with Blue Sky in Your Head — their first studio album in 18 years, which reached number three in the UK album chart. The 2027 run is billed as their return to the live stage, and the search surge that surfaced it on Google Trends came straight off the October 19 announcement.
