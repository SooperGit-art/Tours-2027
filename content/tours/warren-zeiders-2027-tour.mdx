**Yes** — Warren Zeiders officially announced The No Brakes Tour for spring 2027. ## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Warren Zeiders is one of country music's fastest-rising stars. Since signing with Warner Records in 2022, the Pennsylvania-born singer-songwriter has amassed more than 4.2 billion global career streams, two billion TikTok views, and 6.4 million monthly Spotify listeners, earning RIAA double platinum and platinum certifications plus No. 1 radio singles in just four years in the industry.

*No Brakes*, his latest album, arrived September 25, 2026 via Warner Records. The 12-track collection — described as his most vulnerable work to date — was written primarily in a two-day, twelve-hour writing session and blends rock, alt, and country with production from Evan Blair, Ben Johnson, Gabe Simon, Jeff Garrison, and KK Johnson. The tour takes its name directly from the record, following the standard album-tour cycle.

Zeiders is currently on the road: he set out on the final leg of his first-ever world tour in the UK in October 2026, and has recently performed on the Video Music Awards and America's Got Talent. On the festival front, he and Jon Pardi were added to the 2027 Gulf Coast Jam lineup (June 3–6, 2027, Panama City Beach, FL) back in August 2026. With the album fresh and momentum high, a spring 2027 US headline run is the logical next step — and now it's official.

## Ticket and presale info

The official on-sale timeline has already happened: artist presales began Tuesday, October 6, 2026 at 10 a.m. local venue time, and general tickets went on sale Friday, October 9, 2026 at 10 a.m. local. At the time of writing, tickets are available through the usual venue and primary ticketing outlets for each date — check the venue's official page for your city for remaining availability and pricing.
