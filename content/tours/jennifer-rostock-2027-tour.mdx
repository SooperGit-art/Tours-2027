## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Jennifer Rostock were one of Germany's biggest German-language rock exports of the late 2000s and 2010s: formed in Berlin, fronted by the outspoken Jennifer Weist, they mixed punk, electro and pop across five studio albums, with 2016's *Genau in diesem Ton* reaching number two on the German albums chart. In November 2017 they announced a hiatus and bowed out with a farewell tour ending in a sold-out Columbiahalle, Berlin, in May 2018 — pointedly calling it a pause, not an end. The June 2026 return, teased with cryptic street videos and the self-aware single title "Wir schon wieder" ("It's us again!"), follows the classic comeback playbook. German punk bands with this kind of catalog and a reactivated lineup almost always tour — the only question is when the dates land.
