## Quick answer

**Yes** — John Mayer has officially announced six 2027 shows at Sphere in Las Vegas on April 8, 10, 11, 15, 17 and 18. Billed as "John Mayer Live at Sphere," each night runs three formats in one evening: solo, full band, and the John Mayer Trio. Tickets go on general sale Friday, October 9, 2026 at 10 a.m. PT. **October 2026 check:** these six Sphere dates are the only confirmed 2027 dates so far — no wider tour or additional cities have been announced yet.

## Why a Sphere residency makes sense for Mayer in 2027

Mayer is no stranger to the venue. He played 48 shows at Sphere with Dead & Company across their 2024 and 2025 residencies — and served as creative director of that production, spending roughly six months on the immersive visuals with Treatment Studio and Industrial Light & Magic. Returning to Sphere under his own name is a natural next step.

The three-format concept has deep roots in his catalog. Mayer used the same solo/Trio/full-band structure on his 2008 concert film *Where the Light Is: John Mayer Live in Los Angeles*, and his announcement caption — "Meet me where the light is" — nods directly to it. The Trio reunion is the headline for longtime fans: Mayer, Jordan and Palladino released the live album *Try!* in 2005 and last played his own major concerts together on the 2017 Search for Everything Tour.

Timing fits as well. His most recent studio album, *Sob Rock*, came out in 2021, and Mayer has said he is deep into work on his first album since — and his first as an independent artist after parting ways with Columbia Records. A signature residency gives him a stage worthy of whatever comes next.

## Ticket and presale info

The sale schedule is confirmed and already underway. Limited VIP packages (premium seating, exclusive merchandise, a date-specific signed lithograph) and Vibee concert-and-hotel packages went on sale Wednesday, September 30, 2026 at 10 a.m. PT. The artist fan presale, run through Seated, opens Wednesday, October 7 at 10 a.m. PT — registration is open at johnmayer.com. SiriusXM subscribers get their own presale Thursday, October 8 via the *Life with John Mayer* channel (Ch. 4). The general public on-sale is Friday, October 9 at 10 a.m. PT via Ticketmaster, with tickets starting at $129 and an eight-ticket limit per event. Separate tickets for each night will also be auctioned for charity, with 100% of proceeds going to the Heart and Armor Foundation.
