## Why these dates matter

Royal Blood spent 2026 launching their fifth album Dead Company with a UK winter run, and the February/March 2027 European dates extend that album cycle into headline venues — including their biggest Paris room yet at the Zénith and a return to Amsterdam's AFAS Live. The duo (Mike Kerr and Ben Thatcher) have been touring steadily since their 2014 debut, and the two-person lineup remains one of rock's most efficient live machines.

Paris ticket interest pushed an extra presale window before the general on-sale, which suggests demand will carry these rooms regardless of whether the band expands the run.
