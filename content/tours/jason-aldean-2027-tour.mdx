## The tour so far, for context

Aldean's most recent full tour was the Songs About Us Tour in 2026, which ran from July 16 in Bangor, Maine, through September 26 in Gilford, New Hampshire. After that tour closed, he lined up a December 11, 2026, stadium night in Las Vegas with Luke Bryan, billed as the Double Down Tour, before the 2027 calendar turns to festivals and one-offs rather than a new headline run.

That pattern fits how veteran country headliners often work a year between album tours: the big festival slots keep him on major stages through the spring and summer, while a full routing announcement, if one comes, would follow separately. The February cluster is telling — a Scottsdale headline night, a Hollywood headline show and a Tampa festival set within three weeks — and then a quieter spring before the May and June festival dates. Nothing in the current listings suggests a wider 2027 tour is hiding behind these dates; they are presented as individual bookings.
