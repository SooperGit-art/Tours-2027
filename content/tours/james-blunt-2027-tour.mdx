## The Airplane Mode Tour, for context

James Blunt, the British singer-songwriter behind "You're Beautiful" and the multi-platinum Back to Bedlam, has settled into a rhythm of major arena cycles, and 2027 continues it. His most recent road work was a Back to Bedlam 20th-anniversary UK arena run in late 2025, where he performed the landmark album in full — the Airplane Mode Tour now extends that momentum into a proper cross-European arena trek.

The routing tells the story of where his live audience lives: a continental leg through western and northern Europe in February and early March, then a full UK and Ireland arena swing to close the tour. The UK leg hits the country's biggest rooms — The O2 in London, Co-op Live in Manchester, OVO Hydro in Glasgow — a sign of how durable his arena draw remains two decades after his breakthrough.
