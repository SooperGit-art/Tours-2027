## The tour so far, for context

Two Door Cinema Club are a Northern Irish trio from Bangor and Donaghadee, and *Tourist History* — their 2010 debut — turned them into indie headliners on the strength of singles like "What You Know", "Something Good Can Work" and "Undercover Martyn". Fifteen years on, the anniversary tour has become one of the band's biggest undertakings: the recent North American leg included their first-ever headlining show at Madison Square Garden, and the May 2027 run is framed as the completion of that celebration, returning to the US for the West Coast, Texas and Mountain West dates the earlier leg missed.

The 2027 routing is built for bucket-list rooms: a Red Rocks headline slot on May 11, the Hollywood Palladium on May 22, and a closing night at Dallas's Bomb Factory on May 29, with a festival stop at Salt Lake City's Kilby Block Party on May 14. Friendly Fires, who supported the earlier anniversary leg, return for the whole run — a fitting pairing of two UK indie acts who broke through in the same late-2000s wave. The band's most recent album is 2022's *Keep on Smiling*, and they have said they "can't wait to get back out and play some even more bucket list venues" on this run.

Ticketmaster also lists January 2027 UK and Ireland dates for the band, including Dublin's 3Arena on January 23 and The O2 in Belfast on January 24, ahead of the US leg in May.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for the May 2027 US run went on general sale Friday, October 30, 2026, at 10am local time via Ticketmaster and the band's website, following an artist presale on Tuesday, October 27. The band's site offers presale registration for individual dates. Red Rocks, the Hollywood Palladium and the Chicago opener are the likeliest to sell out first, so check Ticketmaster for the latest availability — the October 30 on-sale was the first chance at tickets.
