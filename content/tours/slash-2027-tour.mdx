## The Flying Blind tour, for context

The announcement on October 2, 2026 paired two reveals: the fifth studio album from Slash with Myles Kennedy and The Conspirators, *Flying Blind* (out February 26, 2027 on Gibson Records), and a full world tour to support it. The lead single, also titled "Flying Blind," dropped the same day. This is exactly what drove the Worldwide Google Trends breakout on "slash tour 2027" that same week.

The timing makes sense. Slash spent the past several years back on the road with Guns N' Roses, whose 2026 run covered Europe in the summer, North America, and closes with Australia and New Zealand dates from late November through December 17, 2026. With GNR idle in 2027 so far, the window is open for his solo band's first major campaign in years, and the itinerary reflects it: a full South American opening leg, a deep North American theater and casino run, and a European summer mixing headline shows with festival stops like Nova Rock, Azkena Rock and Bospop.
