## Quick answer

**Yes** — Stephen Wilson Jr will headline a 2027 US tour. He announced the Impressiøn of a Human Tour in November 2026: 12 headline dates from March 24 in Oklahoma City to June 20 at Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado, supporting his sophomore double album, which arrives February 26, 2027. Festival stops at Tortuga, Stagecoach, Hinterland and XRoads41 follow the headline run.

**November 2026 check:** all 12 headline dates are confirmed and tickets are on sale. The tour is described as a first leg, so more dates could be added later in 2027.

## The tour so far, for context

Stephen Wilson Jr's path to Nashville was anything but straight: the southern Indiana native was a two-time Indiana State Golden Gloves boxing champion and a food scientist with dual degrees in microbiology and chemistry before he became a songwriter and lead guitarist in an indie rock band. His debut double album, søn of dad, released on Big Loud Records, turned that unlikely backstory into one of country music's most talked-about breakout records, and he spent 2025 and 2026 on the road with the entirely sold-out søn of dad run, the Gary the Torch Tour, and support dates with HARDY, including a Madison Square Garden show.

The 2027 run is the next step up. It opens in the Texas and South club-and-theater circuit he has been building — Oklahoma City, San Antonio and Houston in the first week — then works east through North Charleston, Savannah, Chattanooga, Asheville and Auburn before a June swing through Ohio, Pennsylvania and Wyoming. The booking that says the most about where his career now stands is the finale: a first-ever headline show at Red Rocks on June 20, the kind of date that anchors a whole tour announcement.

## Ticket and presale info

General tickets for the headline dates went on sale Friday, November 20, 2026, at 10:00 AM local time in each market, except for the June 16 Jackson, Wyoming, show at Snow King Mountain, which goes on sale later. Tickets use Ticketmaster's Face Value Exchange. Festival dates at Tortuga, Stagecoach, Hinterland and XRoads41 need a festival pass rather than a headline ticket, so check which kind of ticket you need before buying.
