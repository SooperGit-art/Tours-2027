Fall Out Boy remain one of the defining pop-punk arena acts, and their touring history makes a return plausible whenever they choose it. The So Much For (2our) Dust run kept the band on arena stages well into 2025 on the back of *So Much (For) Stardust*, their eighth studio album, with the US as the anchor market and international legs in between. The band has treated touring as central to its career since its 2000s breakthrough, and demand for their catalogue — from "Sugar, We're Goin Down" to "Centuries" — has not faded.

But plausibility is not an announcement. The band is currently between major tour legs, with no newly added arena dates formally announced beyond the existing cycle, and their official channels are the only source that can confirm a return. Until then, the honest status is: not announced.
