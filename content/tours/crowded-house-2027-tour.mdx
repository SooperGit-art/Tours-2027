## Quick answer

**No** — there is no Crowded House tour announced for 2027 yet. What is real: the band were named on Glastonbury Festival's first 2027 line-up poster on November 18, 2026, so one festival appearance next summer is confirmed, with the exact day and stage still to be announced. Ticketmaster currently lists no upcoming Crowded House concerts at all.

**November 2026 check:** no standalone 2027 tour dates exist. Two conflicting Australia and New Zealand arena-date lists circulating on TikTok are unverified — neither comes from the band or any official channel.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense — and why it still might not happen

Crowded House are the Australia-New Zealand rock band behind "Don't Dream It's Over", formed in Melbourne in 1985 around Neil Finn and now more than 15 million records into a career that includes 13 ARIA Awards, a BRIT Award and an MTV VMA. Their seventh album, *Dreamers Are Waiting*, arrived in 2021, followed by *Gravity Stairs* in 2024 and a full Australia and New Zealand tour behind it. The band have stayed active since, with rare live audio and video still being added to their official Private Universe fan club as recently as November 2026.

The Glastonbury booking is the strongest signal yet that the band are planning around next summer — festivals rarely book heritage acts for a single one-off with nothing else around it. But it is only a signal: no tour dates, no venues and no ticket sales exist, and the two arena-date lists doing the rounds on social media should not be mistaken for an announcement. One set claims a March 2027 run through Perth, Adelaide, Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane and Auckland; another claims a July 2027 run through the same cities plus Christchurch. Both come from the same TikTok account, neither is backed by the band, a promoter or a ticket seller, and they cannot both be true. That is the classic shape of a viral fake poster, and fans should not book travel or buy tickets off either list.

## Ticket and presale info

There are no Crowded House tour tickets to buy, because there is no tour. The only confirmed 2027 appearance is Glastonbury, and that festival sold out in about 42 minutes in October 2026 — before the line-up was even announced — so the announcement changes nothing for ticket buyers. A resale of cancelled and returned tickets is planned for spring 2027 through See Tickets. If a standalone tour is announced, expect tickets through crowdedhouse.com and the usual sellers, and be wary of any site already selling "Crowded House 2027" tickets: with no dates announced, those listings cannot be real.
