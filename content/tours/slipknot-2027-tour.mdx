## Quick answer

**Yes** — Slipknot have officially announced a 19-date global stadium run for summer 2027, billed as the "(sic)est Stadium Tour". It opens July 3, 2027 at London's Wembley Stadium, moves through North America across July and August, and closes with a South American leg in September.

**October 2026 check:** the core itinerary is confirmed and tickets are already on general sale, but exact dates for most North American stadiums and the South American shows have not yet been published beyond the confirmed anchor dates below.

<Callout type="confirmed" title="✅ Confirmed for 2027">

## The tour so far, for context

Slipknot have spent their entire career insisting they are not an arena band — and then kept booking bigger buildings. The 2027 run settles the argument for good: this is a full stadium tour, the band's first at this scale, and it arrives after a global anniversary run that played London's O2 Arena, Madison Square Garden and Los Angeles' Intuit Dome.

The announcement came on the heels of "Arsenal", the band's first new song in three years, which has fans watching closely for signs of a new album cycle. Whether or not a full record follows, Slipknot describe the 2027 shows as featuring a brand new set and new staging spanning their career — the masked nine-piece at their most theatrical.

The support bills are a statement in themselves. Bring Me the Horizon headlining direct support across North America pairs the two biggest modern heavy bands on one stadium stage, while the Wembley opener's bill of Marilyn Manson, Lorna Shore and Hatebreed is one of the heaviest lineups the stadium has ever hosted.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets are already on sale. The rollout followed the band's usual pattern: artist presale on September 29, 2026, a Knotfest presale the same day, and general onsale on October 2, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time, with availability and exact timing varying by market.
