What is real is the show's live history. Danny Go!, created in 2019 by Daniel Coleman with his wife Mindy and childhood friends Michael Finster and Matthew Padgett, built its audience on YouTube — more than 4 million subscribers and nearly 5 billion lifetime views — and then took the show on the road, playing 65 sold-out shows and selling nearly 200,000 tickets over two years. A national tour packed nine shows into nine days across six cities: Boston, Toronto, Rochester, Wallingford, Newark, and New York City, with double dates in Boston, Toronto, and New York.
