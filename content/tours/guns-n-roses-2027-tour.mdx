## Quick answer

**No** — Guns N' Roses are not expected to tour in 2027. On October 12, 2026, Slash told SiriusXM's Trunk Nation With Eddie Trunk that "next year, basically, Guns is off," with the band stepping away from the road to concentrate on finishing new music. Thirteen shows remain on the 2026 schedule through December, but nothing has been announced for 2027. **October 2026 check:** no official Guns N' Roses 2027 dates exist, and any dates you see online are unverified.

## Why the band is sitting 2027 out

Guns N' Roses have spent the better part of two years on the road, playing across Latin America, North America, and Europe, with a final 2026 run through India, Indonesia, Singapore, Australia, and New Zealand closing out in December. Slash called the recent U.S. and European legs the best the band has done since the reunion era began, describing the past year as "really f**king cool." The band has been touring with new drummer Isaac Carpenter, who joined the lineup for the recent run.

The decision to pause is about the music, not momentum. Slash explained that the band has accumulated a large body of unreleased material that is currently "all over the place" and needs to be assembled, sorted, and finished — work that, in his words, "doesn't work on the road." Writing individually is easy for the members; writing together while touring is, per Slash, "next to impossible." The hope is that 2027 becomes the year that material finally gets shaped into a record.

If it happens, that album would be the first full-length studio record from the reunited lineup. A full-length hasn't come from the band since 2008's Chinese Democracy, which featured only Axl Rose from the classic lineup; Slash and Duff McKagan returned in 2016, and the band has since released only the four-song Hard Skool EP in 2022. That makes the 2027 studio pause genuinely significant for fans waiting on new original music.
