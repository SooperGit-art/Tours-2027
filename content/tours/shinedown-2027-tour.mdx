## The tour so far, for context

Shinedown are a multi-platinum hard rock band fronted by Brent Smith, with Zach Myers on guitar, Eric Bass on bass and production, and Barry Kerch on drums. In 2026 they released their eighth studio album, EI8HT, and launched the Dance, Kid, Dance Act II World Tour, a 54-date run across 11 countries that wraps with a European and UK leg in late 2026.

The Lunatic Ball is their own spin on the festival format rather than a standard tour stop. The inaugural 2026 edition ran October 23-25 in Miramar Beach, Florida, with Shinedown playing two headlining sets at the Seascape Resort alongside Bush, Hanson, Flyleaf, Living Colour, and more. Announcing the 2027 return onstage before the first edition had even cooled off signals the band intend it as an annual event — but for now it is a single weekend, not a tour, and fans should treat any supposed 2027 lineup circulating online as unconfirmed until the band names acts.
