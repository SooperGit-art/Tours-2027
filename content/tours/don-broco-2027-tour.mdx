## Quick answer

**Yes — 69 arena dates** — Don Broco are direct support and special guests on Nickelback's Everything Under The Sun Tour 2027, from February 6, 2027 at PPG Paints Arena in Pittsburgh through October 16, 2027 in Pokolbin, Australia, covering North America, the UK, Europe and Australia.

**October 2026 check:** all 69 dates below are announced, with North American presales from October 13, 2026. The only Nickelback date Don Broco do not play is the February 27 Hondo Rodeo appearance in Tampa.

## The tour so far, for context

Don Broco, from Bedford, England, released their fifth album *Nightmare Tripping* via Fearless Records on March 27, 2026, reuniting with producer Dan Lancaster. The title track features Nickelback's Chad Kroeger and Ryan Peake — a collaboration that has now expanded into Don Broco supporting Nickelback across a full world arena tour.

The band spent 2026 headlining their own Nightmare Tripping tour across the UK and Europe. Stepping into arenas as special guests for one of modern rock's biggest-selling acts, for 69 dates on three continents, is the largest touring commitment of Don Broco's career so far.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets are Nickelback Everything Under The Sun tickets — Don Broco are support, so there is no separate Don Broco on-sale. North American presales begin Tuesday, October 13, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time, including artist, Verizon, American Express and Spotify Reserved presales, with the general sale Friday, October 16, 2026 at 10 a.m. local time through Live Nation. Australian tickets also go on general sale October 16, with presales from October 13. VIP packages with premium seating and lounge access are available in select markets.
