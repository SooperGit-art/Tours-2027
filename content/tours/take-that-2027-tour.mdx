**Yes** — Take That officially announced the Dreamers Tour on August 28, 2026: 31 shows across 16 countries, running June 3 to July 23, 2027. It's their biggest European run in years, supporting their tenth studio album, Dreamers. **October 2026 check:** tickets are on sale now, and the band says more dates may still be added.

## The tour so far, for context

The Dreamers Tour announcement came just weeks after Take That wrapped their second Circus Live run — the rebooted production that ended July 4, 2026, at Dublin's Aviva Stadium and became the UK's most-watched livestreamed performance via Amazon Music. Gary Barlow was candid in an Instagram video about the post-tour emotional crash, saying he felt like he'd "fallen off a cliff" — then immediately pivoted to the new album and tour as the next chapter.

The Dreamers album follows three singles that road-tested the new sound on the Circus tour, with "Feel My Love" leaning country-tinged in a collaboration with Michael Patrick Kelly. The 2027 routing is notably continental: festival slots like Odense's Tinderbox, outdoor venues like Berlin's Waldbühne and the Loreley, and stadium-adjacent Irish stops in Belfast (Belsonic) and Limerick. A handful of Spanish dates are still listed with venues TBA — watch those for confirmations — and the band has explicitly said further shows are coming.

## Ticket and presale info

The on-sale sequence was: album presale from 10am local time on September 2, 2026 (via pre-ordering Dreamers), venue presales on September 3, and general sale on September 4. Tickets are on sale now through Ticketmaster outlets and takethat.com. For the Belfast Belsonic date, tickets went via ticketmaster.ie. As with any high-demand arena tour, buy from official sellers and avoid resale markups.
