## Quick answer

**Yes** — the Backstreet Boys have officially announced four 2027 stadium shows in Toronto: February 12, 13, 19 and 20 at Rogers Centre, billed as *Into the Millennium – Homecoming: Live in Canada*. These are their only North American stadium shows of 2027. The announcement came October 2, 2026 via Rogers and Live Nation Canada, days after the group wrapped a 10-show stadium residency in Düsseldorf, Germany.

**October 2026 check:** All four Toronto dates are confirmed. Rogers customer presale starts October 5, 2026; general sale October 9 via Ticketmaster.

## The tour so far, for context

More than three decades in, with over 130 million records sold worldwide, the Backstreet Boys are in the middle of an extended *Millennium* victory lap. The 1999 album — home to "I Want It That Way," "Larger Than Life," "Show Me the Meaning of Being Lonely" and "The One" — became one of the best-selling albums of all time, and the current *Into the Millennium* production was built to stage it in full, first at the Sphere in Las Vegas and then across a 10-show stadium residency in Düsseldorf, Germany in October 2026.

Toronto is a deliberate homecoming. Canada was one of the first markets to embrace the group in North America, and Brian Littrell said as much in the announcement: "Canada was one of the first places to embrace us, and the love and support we've received from our fans there has meant so much from the very beginning. Coming back feels incredibly special."

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets roll out in stages, all at 10am ET: Rogers customers get first access October 5, 2026, followed by the Backstreet Army fan club presale October 6 and the artist presale October 7. General sale begins October 9, 2026 via Ticketmaster. Travel and VIP packages — premium seating, hotel, priority entry and lounge access, but no meet-and-greets — also go on sale October 5.
