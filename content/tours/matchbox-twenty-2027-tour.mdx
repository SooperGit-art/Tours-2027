## Quick answer

**Planned, not announced** — Rob Thomas has confirmed Matchbox Twenty will return to Australia in 2027 for the 30th anniversary of *Yourself or Someone Like You*, but no headline Matchbox Twenty Tour 2027 dates, cities or venues exist yet. Their one confirmed 2027 performance is Stagecoach Festival in Indio, California.

**October 2026 check:** no official 2027 headline dates or on-sales. Sites listing a full 2027 itinerary are building one out of Thomas's podcast comments — the plan is real, the schedule is not.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

*Yourself or Someone Like You*, released in October 1996, debuted at No. 1 in Australia and produced 3AM, Real World and Push — songs Australian radio never really put down. The band's 2024 Australian run, behind *Where the Light Goes*, was their biggest tour of the country to date.

Thomas, who was in Australia and New Zealand on a solo headline run when he gave the interview, said the anniversary plan starts with US festival slots before a full Australian run in 2027. He and guitarist Paul Doucette have also been texting ideas for a Broadway theatre residency — "it just looks better on a T-shirt than Vegas," Thomas joked — but he was clear those talks only start properly once his solo tour ends.
