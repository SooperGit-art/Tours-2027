## Quick answer

**Yes** — From Ashes to New are headlining North America in spring 2027. The Pennsylvania rock band announced the 27-date Reflections Tour on October 9, 2026: it opens March 12, 2027 at Toad's Place in New Haven, Connecticut, and closes April 18 at The Queen in Wilmington, Delaware, with Nevertel, Dark Divine and Hollow Pact in support.

**November 2026 check:** all 27 dates below come from the band's official tour announcement. Tickets are on sale through Ticketmaster and the venues.

## The tour so far, for context

From Ashes to New had a banner 2026: their album *Reflections* arrived in April, "Drag Me" became their first No. 1 on Active Rock radio, "Die For You" went viral, and the album has tallied just shy of 100 million streams. The band spent the year on the road with Shinedown, Coheed and Cambria and Black Veil Brides, played Aftershock in October, and close 2026 with their annual sold-out hometown holiday shows in Harrisburg, Pennsylvania, on December 11–12.

Singer Matt Brandyberry said the band wanted to mount this headline tour in 2026 but couldn't put it together the way they hoped — so 2027 starts with the Reflections Tour instead. A deluxe edition of *Reflections* arrives November 13, 2026, led by the new single "Love Me", giving the spring run fresh material alongside the album's hits.

Tickets for all 27 headline dates are on sale through Ticketmaster and the individual venues.
