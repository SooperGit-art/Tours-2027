## Quick answer

**Yes** — Greta Van Fleet have officially announced the "Into The Beginning" tour for 2027, a 56-date arena run kicking off in Savannah on 3 February and running through a final show in Grand Rapids on 31 July. The routing covers the US, Canada, Europe and the UK, with tickets sold at face value. **October 2026 check:** all 56 dates are confirmed; support acts for the non-UK legs are still unannounced.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Greta Van Fleet have spent the past decade becoming one of rock's defining live acts of their generation: Grammy winners, multiple platinum-selling releases, and sold-out arena tours across the globe. *Palace for the People*, their fourth studio album, arrived on 9 October 2026 via Republic Records with the singles "Tear It Down" and "Saw You Stand", and the band describe it as their boldest artistic statement yet.

The scale of the Into The Beginning tour matches that ambition. After celebrating the new album with an intimate underplay residency at Nashville's The Basement East in October 2026, the band move straight into a full arena circuit — 29 US dates in February and March alone, before Europe and a summer return to North America. The face-value ticketing stance is a deliberate fan-first move in an era of dynamic pricing debates.

## Ticket and presale info

Fans can sign up for the Greta Van Fleet fan presale at GretaVanFleet.com, which runs from 10am local time on Wednesday 7 October 2026, with general on-sale from 10am local on Friday 9 October. Canadian dates follow a separate schedule: presales from 9am local on Tuesday 13 October and general on-sale from 10am local on Thursday 15 October. Tickets are being sold at face value per the band's announcement.
