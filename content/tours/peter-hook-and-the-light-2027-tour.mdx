## The tour so far, for context

Peter Hook was Joy Division's bassist, and after Ian Curtis died in 1980 he carried the band's low end into New Order, which he left in 2007. Peter Hook and the Light, formed in 2010, is his vehicle for keeping the Joy Division catalogue alive on stage — the band has spent fifteen years playing the albums in full, city by city, and the 2027 routing is the most extensive version of that mission yet: five legs on four continents in a single calendar year.

The concept is simple and it is the whole point of the tour. *Unknown Pleasures* and *Closer* are the only two studio albums Joy Division ever made, and Hook plays both of them front to back, in order, with the rest of the set drawn from his career — Joy Division singles and B-sides, and New Order material from his years in the band. For fans who never saw Joy Division — nobody under 45 could have — it is the closest thing to hearing those records played live by someone who made them.

The timing explains the Trends breakout. The US, French and Asian dates were announced through the autumn, but the UK and Ireland leg — fifteen shows, the biggest single block of the year — was only revealed on November 24, 2026, with presale the next day. That is the announcement behind today's surge, and it lands a year after the band's last full UK run.
