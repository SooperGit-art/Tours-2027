## Quick answer

**Yes** — The Red Clay Strays officially announced a 2027 leg of their Grateful Tour on October 1, 2026: 36 headline shows across arenas and amphitheaters in the US and Canada, running January 22 through September 19, 2027, in support of their third studio album *Grateful*. Highlights include three hometown nights at Mobile, AL's Regions Arena (March 25–27), plus arena stops in Rosemont, Cleveland, Edmonton, Oklahoma City, and Austin, with support from Avery Anna, The Bends, and St. Paul & The Broken Bones on select dates.

**October 2026 check:** all 36 dates are officially confirmed via the band's announcement; only international dates outside North America remain unannounced.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

The Red Clay Strays released *Grateful*, their third studio album, in June 2026 via HBYCO Records/RCA Records, produced once again by Dave Cobb — the same team behind their breakout. The album earned a four-star rave from Rolling Stone, and its first single "Demons In Your Choir" continued the band's momentum from *Made by These Moments* (2024), which had lifted the Mobile, Alabama band from Southern circuit favorites to arena headliners on the strength of hits like "Wondering Why" and "Drowning."

The 2026 leg of the Grateful Tour was the band's biggest live run to date, playing arenas coast-to-coast through the fall of 2026 — including two-night stands at Fort Worth's Dickies Arena and Nashville's Bridgestone Arena. Rather than ending the cycle, the band is rolling straight into a 2027 extension, announcing the new leg on Instagram with the message "We're GRATEFUL to be extending the tour into 2027!!"

Given the arena-level demand — multiple multi-night stands on both legs — further 2027 additions, and eventually international legs, remain plausible, but nothing beyond these 36 dates has been announced.
