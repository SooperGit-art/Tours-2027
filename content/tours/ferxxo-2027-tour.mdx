## The tour so far, for context

Feid, born Salomón Villada Hoyos in Medellín, built Ferxxo into a second identity: the green-glasses character who fronts his rowdier, party-starting side, while Feid handles the smoother romantic material. The split became a full concept with the seven-track EP FEID VS FERXXO and the Falxo Tour — billed as "El Mano a Mano Del Año" — which Live Nation took across the United States from April 7 to May 13, 2026, from Buena Vista and Miami Beach through Atlanta, Chicago and Houston to Dallas.

The run kept going after the US leg: Feid brought the Falxo Tour to Australia for the first time in July 2026, and closed out 2025 with stadium shows at home in Colombia, including Estadio El Campín in Bogotá on December 13, 2025 and the Kaseya Center in Miami on December 20, 2025. That is a heavy touring year with no follow-up announced, which is why the 2027 speculation is running hot — and why it needs to be checked against official channels, not fan sites.
