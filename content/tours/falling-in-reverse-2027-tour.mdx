## Quick answer

**Yes — partially.** Falling in Reverse have officially confirmed 2027 dates: they play Rock am Ring on Friday, June 4 and Rock im Park on Sunday, June 6, 2027 in Germany, as part of the festivals' first announced wave (blink-182, Bring Me The Horizon, Faith No More and others also confirmed). No named headlining 2027 tour has been announced. **October 2026 check:** a full 2027 headlining run and any North American dates are still unannounced.

## Why these festival dates make sense for the band

Falling in Reverse, formed in Las Vegas in 2008 by vocalist Ronnie Radke after his departure from Escape the Fate, have grown into one of modern rock's biggest live draws. After the post-hardcore debut *The Drug in Me Is You* (2011), the band steadily blended rap, electronic and metal elements, culminating in 2024's *Popular Monster* era and its arena-sized singles like "Zombified" and "Watch the World Burn."

The band has been a heavy touring act in recent years: the 2022 Rockzilla Summer Tour with Papa Roach, the 2023 Popular Monstour headlining run with Ice Nine Kills, and late-2025 appearances at Warped Tour Orlando and Knotfest Mexico. European festival slots are a natural fit for a band whose sound straddles rock and metalcore, and sharing a first-wave billing with blink-182 and Faith No More at two of Germany's biggest festivals signals they remain a priority festival booking for 2027.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for the confirmed 2027 dates are sold through the official Rock am Ring and Rock im Park websites, with weekend tickets already moving fast (over 100,000 sold combined across both festivals at the time of the lineup announcement). Note that Rock am Ring sells weekend tickets rather than individual day tickets. There are no tickets for a Falling in Reverse headlining 2027 tour because none has been announced — beware of third-party sites selling "tickets" to dates the band has not confirmed.
