## Quick answer

**Yes** — Emeli Sandé has officially announced a 21-date UK and European tour for early 2027, celebrating 15 years of her debut album *Our Version of Events*. The tour opens January 25, 2027 at the Brighton Dome, plays major UK venues including the London Palladium on February 10, and continues through Ireland and mainland Europe into March.

**October 2026 check:** the full itinerary is confirmed and tickets went on general sale September 11, 2026, with several UK dates already showing low availability. No dates outside the UK, Ireland and Europe have been announced.

## The tour so far, for context

*Our Version of Events* is one of the defining British debut albums of the 2010s: ten non-consecutive weeks at No. 1 on the UK Albums Chart and the best-selling UK album of 2012. The same year Sandé performed at both the opening and closing ceremonies of the London Olympics, and in 2013 she won Best British Female Solo Artist and British Album of the Year at the Brits.

Sandé has kept touring steadily through 2026 — including a headline spot at BBC Radio 2 in the Park alongside Chaka Khan, Lulu and The Script — and National Album Day's "Icons" theme saw a special gold vinyl edition of the debut album released on October 17, 2026. The anniversary tour leans into that legacy: intimate theatres and concert halls rather than arenas, fitting for an album built on gospel choirs and powerhouse vocals.

## Ticket and presale info

General sale began September 11, 2026 at 9am BST / 10am CEST via her official ticketing links, and demand was immediate — Ticketmaster flagged low availability on multiple dates including York, Gateshead, Glasgow, London, Manchester, Liverpool and Birmingham.
