## Quick answer

**Yes, at sea** — Creed will headline the fifth Summer of '99 & Beyond Cruise, sailing April 17-21, 2027 from Miami to Great Stirrup Cay, Bahamas aboard Norwegian Joy, with two pool deck headline sets. No land-based Creed Tour 2027 dates have been announced.

**October 2026 check:** the cruise is Creed's only confirmed 2027 performance. Any website listing 2027 Creed arena dates is speculating — nothing on land exists yet.

## The cruise so far, for context

Creed reunited in July 2023 after an eleven-year hiatus, announcing themselves as headliners for the first two Summer of '99 cruises in April 2024, which sold out immediately. They followed with more than 60 tour dates in 2024 and another 30 in 2025, including their own two-day land-based Summer of '99 and Beyond festival, drawing record-breaking crowds.

The cruise has become the band's signature event: four previous sailings have completely sold out. The 2027 edition was announced on April 17, 2026, while that year's sailing was still under way. A separate land-based Summer of '99 and Beyond festival in Tinley Park, Illinois in July 2026 — with Limp Bizkit among the guests — shows the brand now lives both at sea and on land, but for 2027 only the cruise is official.

## Ticket and presale info

Cruise cabins are sold exclusively at summerof99cruise.com. Alumni presales were held April 30-May 1 and May 4-5, 2026, with public on-sale from May 13 at 2:00 p.m. ET. A $100 saving was available during presale. The first 850 cabins booked could choose an exclusive 30-minute Creed acoustic set, a photo with Creed, or a 30-minute Q&A with Breaking Benjamin, subject to availability. Shore excursions for Great Stirrup Cay open 4-6 weeks before sailing.
