## The tour so far, for context

Don Omar, widely known as the King of Reggaeton, remains one of the genre's defining live acts more than two decades into his career. His last US outing, the 2024 Back to Reggaeton Tour, was a commercial powerhouse: over 350,000 tickets sold and more than $40 million grossed, per box office reports submitted to Pollstar — including a single night at the Kia Forum that grossed $1.84 million.

The Last King World Tour launched in 2026 with multiple sold-out dates across the US, and overwhelming demand is what pushed the tour into 2027. The set leans on the catalog that made him essential to Latin music: "Danza Kuduro," "Dale Don Dale," "Salió El Sol," and "Bandoleros," delivered with the large-scale production the tour is built around.

Don Omar broke out with 2003's platinum The Last Don, took King of Kings into the Billboard 200's Top 10, and won a 2012 Latin Grammy for MTO² — the kind of legacy that keeps filling arenas.
