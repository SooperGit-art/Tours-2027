## Quick answer

**Yes** — Def Leppard will tour North America in 2027. The band announced a 45-date run on October 7, 2026, co-headlined in spirit with the Goo Goo Dolls and The Struts as very special guests. The tour runs March 2 to July 2, 2027, covering the US and Canada, with arena stops including Madison Square Garden and the Hollywood Bowl.

**October 2026 check:** the 45 North American dates are fully announced and the band's official site lists presale tickets. No tour name beyond the announcement, and no Europe, UK, or festival dates have been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Def Leppard are coming off a stacked run. Earlier in 2026 they completed their third sold-out Las Vegas residency at Caesars Palace, then played sold-out shows across the UK, Europe, South America, and Mexico. The band's Greatest Hits compilation also charted in four UK charts this summer — two of them in the Top 10 — while their catalog pulls over 11 million monthly Spotify listeners.

The band is also building toward new music. Their single "Rejoice" arrived in early 2026, and guitarist Phil Collen said this summer they've been writing at every spare moment — the working album list had about 18 songs, with frontman Joe Elliott describing it as "nearly two albums" worth of material that they'd pare down to 10 or 11 tracks. An official documentary is set for theatres in early 2027 as well, making this a full-court-press year for the band. The Struts and the Goo Goo Dolls are well-matched touring partners: the Goo Goo Dolls are coming off a heavy summer festival run, and their diamond-certified "Iris" has topped 6 billion global streams.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets go on sale in a staggered rollout: the Def Leppard fan club presale begins Monday, October 12 at 10AM local time, followed by Citi and Verizon presales at 12PM the same day for US dates. Additional presales run through the week, and the general onsale starts Friday, October 16 at 10AM local time via LiveNation.com. $1 from each ticket sold goes to City of Hope for cancer research and treatment. As always, buy through official channels — the band has not announced VIP or fan-package details beyond the standard presale tiers.
