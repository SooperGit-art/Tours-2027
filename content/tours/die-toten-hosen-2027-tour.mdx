## Quick answer

**Yes** — Die Toten Hosen are officially touring in 2027. After the initial 23-date 2026 run sold more than a million tickets, the band announced seven extra stadium and open-air shows for summer 2027, later adding an eighth: a June 29, 2027 return to the VELTINS-Arena in Gelsenkirchen, their first show there in ten years. The 2027 leg runs June 12 through July 10 and closes in the band's hometown of Düsseldorf. **October 2026 check:** all eight dates are confirmed and on sale; no further 2027 shows have been announced.

## The tour so far, for context

Die Toten Hosen launched the "Trink Aus! Wir Müssen Gehen" tour in 2026 with 23 dates across Germany, Austria, Switzerland, and Luxembourg, selling more than one million tickets — at the time, a career-best presale response. Demand was so overwhelming that the band added seven dates for summer 2027, and, citing loud requests from the Ruhr region, an eighth: the VELTINS-Arena in Gelsenkirchen, where Campino said the band has "experienced legendary nights" since its formation.

The tour supports the 2026 album "Trink Aus! Wir Müssen Gehen" (plus the bonus album "Alles muss raus!"), which the band has confirmed will be their final regular studio album. That announcement, combined with the tour's valedictory name, is what feeds the farewell speculation — Rolling Stone reported fans reading the whole run as a goodbye in the band's 45th year, though the band itself stops short of saying it.

Between the two legs, Die Toten Hosen play Argentina one final time (Tandil, Rosario, and Buenos Aires in October 2026), where Feine Sahne Fischfilet joins as support — a nod to the special decades-long relationship the band has with Argentine punks. After the 2027 stadium leg closes in Düsseldorf on July 10, the band returns to Rock am Ring and Rock im Park, which Campino called "an integral part of Die Toten Hosen's history" since the 1990s, for what he described as the "home stretch."

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for the 2027 dates are sold through the band's own shop on dth.de — the official outlet, and the place the band directs fans for the discounted social tickets they traditionally offer. The initial 2027 presale opened in November 2025 and moved fast; the Nuremberg opener is already sold out on the official site, which suggests remaining dates will follow. For the Gelsenkirchen show, VIP tickets for the LaOla section were priced at €329 and sold via tickets.schalke04.de, while standard tickets went through dth.de. If a date shows as sold out, check the official site's exchange channels rather than third-party markups.
