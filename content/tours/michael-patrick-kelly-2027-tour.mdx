## Quick answer

**Yes** — Michael Patrick Kelly brings his Traces Tour back for a short open-air run in Germany in summer 2027. Six dates with venues are confirmed so far, opening June 24, 2027 at Domplatz Fulda and closing August 15 at Schlossplatz Coburg, and the promoter bills them as his last and only German concerts of 2027.

**October 2026 check:** the dates are announced and listed for sale. One listing mentions a seventh show in Heringsdorf without a date or venue, so we have left it out until it is published officially.

## The tour so far, for context

Michael Patrick Kelly — born in Dublin in 1977, first famous as Paddy Kelly of The Kelly Family — has rebuilt a major solo career in Germany with albums such as Human, iD and the No. 1 record Traces, built around his four-octave voice and songs like Beautiful Madness and Throwback. The Traces Tour's 2026 leg played to more than 200,000 people, and the 2027 extension takes the same show outdoors to castle squares, a cathedral square and festival grounds.

That format is the appeal: these are his only German shows of the year, in settings like Dresden's Filmnächte am Elbufer on the riverbank and Coburg's Schlossplatz, rather than another arena lap. It is also why the run is short — six confirmed nights across June and August, not a full routing.
