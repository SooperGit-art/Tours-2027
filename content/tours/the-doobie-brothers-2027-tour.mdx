## Quick answer

**Yes** — The Doobie Brothers are heading to Las Vegas in 2027 for a nine-show residency at Fontainebleau Las Vegas' BleauLive Theater, their debut at the venue. The run is split across four blocks: February 26–27, March 5–6, August 27–28, and September 1, 3 and 4. **October 2026 check:** the residency is the band's first announced 2027 engagement; no other 2027 dates have been confirmed yet.

## The tour so far, for context

The residency caps a remarkable late-career run for a band formed in San Jose, California, in 1970. The Doobie Brothers were inducted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in 2020 and the Songwriters Hall of Fame in 2025, the same year the reunited lineup released Walk This Road — their first album of new songs featuring Michael McDonald since 1980. With more than 48 million albums sold and 16 Top 40 hits, the band's catalog — "Listen to the Music," "China Grove," "Long Train Runnin'," "Black Water" and "What a Fool Believes" — remains one of the defining American rock songbooks.

The band closed its 2026 touring year on October 21 in Estero, Florida, after a summer run that included solo dates and shows with Santana. The Las Vegas residency is a different kind of engagement: nine nights in one room rather than a city-to-city tour, which suits a band more than five decades into its career while still giving fans a destination event built around the full catalog.

## Ticket and presale info

Citi cardmembers get the first presale window, starting October 27, 2026 at 10 a.m. PT, with additional presales running through the week from October 28. General on-sale is October 30, 2026 at 10 a.m. PT through Fontainebleau Las Vegas. Note the residency is split into blocks months apart — the February/March weekends and the late-summer block — so check the date you want before booking travel.
