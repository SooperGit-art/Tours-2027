## Quick answer

**Drake says he's touring in 2027 — but no dates exist yet.** At the Toronto premiere of his FOMO film in mid-September 2026, Drake told CTV's etalk directly: *"Obviously, tour is coming... We'll be out 2027 to see everybody again across the world."*

That's a verbal confirmation from the artist himself — a real step up from the July 2026 "coming soon" tease from a member of his team — but it is not an announcement. No dates, cities, venues, tour name, or on-sale timeline have been revealed.

## Why the confirmation matters

Drake doesn't tour casually — It's All a Blur was one of the highest-grossing rap tours ever, and a 2027 run would be his first major tour in roughly three years, now with three albums' worth of new material to draw from. The FOMO premiere quote is the strongest signal possible short of an actual announcement: the artist, on camera, naming the year.

What we're watching for next: an official tour name and the first leg announcement, which for Drake historically starts in North America. When that lands, this page gets rebuilt around real dates immediately.
