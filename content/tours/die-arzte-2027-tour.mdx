## Quick answer

**Yes** — Die Ärzte's "Eine Gänsehaut nach dem andern! – Tour 2027" is officially confirmed, with 32 dates across Germany, Austria, and Switzerland from April 8 to June 6, 2027. The run opens with two nights at Berlin's Max-Schmeling-Halle and closes with two open-air shows at Berlin's Parkbühne Wuhlheide.

**October 2026 check:** the full schedule is announced and tickets have been on sale since April 15, 2026, via the band's exclusive shop — many dates are already sold out.

## The tour so far, for context

Die Ärzte have been the undisputed giants of German-language punk rock since forming in Berlin in 1982. The trio — Farin Urlaub, Bela B, and Rodrigo González — count 14 studio albums, including the chart-topping "Hell" (2020) and "Dunkel" (2021), and rank among Germany's best-selling artists with over 9.3 million records sold at home. Their tours are famously event-like: the April 2026 presale for this run sold out numerous cities within minutes of opening, and Musikexpress described the demand as one of the biggest in the German concert market of the year.

The 2027 routing is classic Die Ärzte: multiple nights in every major market (three in Dortmund, two each in Berlin, Leipzig, Munich, Cologne, Hamburg, Bremen, Vienna, Frankfurt, Zurich, Stuttgart, Erfurt, and Hanover), plus Chemnitz and Freiburg, before the two-night open-air finale at the Parkbühne Wuhlheide. The tour is produced by Koopmann Concerts and FKP Scorpio, and true to the band's long-standing ethos, it's being run with a sustainability concept and social-ticket pricing.
