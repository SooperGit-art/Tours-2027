## Quick answer

**Yes** — TXT have 25 confirmed 2027 dates as part of their fifth world tour, TOMORROW X TOGETHER WORLD TOUR &lt;STEAL THE WIND&gt;. The 2027 leg spans Japan (January–February: Tokyo, Osaka, Aichi), Southeast Asia (March: Kaohsiung, Kuala Lumpur, Jakarta), North America (May: Tacoma, Inglewood, Mexico City, Fort Worth, Atlanta, Belmont Park, Chicago) and Europe (June: Manchester, Milan, Paris, Amsterdam, Antwerp, Cologne), wrapping with two nights in Hong Kong on June 19–20.

**October 2026 check:** all 25 dates and cities are officially confirmed by BIGHIT MUSIC, but venues and ticket sale details for the 2027 shows have not been announced. More stops are expected to be added.

## The tour so far, for context

"STEAL THE WIND" marks the start of a new chapter for TXT (Tomorrow X Together — Soobin, Yeonjun, Beomgyu, Taehyun and Hueningkai), closing out the four-tour "ACT" series that defined their career so far: "Act: Love Sick," "Act: Sweet Mirage," "Act: Promise" and "Act: Tomorrow." The final ACT tour drew roughly 564,000 attendees across 29 concerts in 17 regions, per Korea JoongAng Daily. According to BIGHIT MUSIC, the title "STEAL THE WIND" reflects the group's ambition to take control of an unpredictable flow and use it to move forward — a theme that extends the "wind" concept from their eighth mini-album.

The timing ties the tour directly to a new release cycle: BIGHIT announced on September 28, 2026 that TXT will return with their ninth mini-album "PERFECT STORM" on November 16 — the day after the tour's Seoul opening run ends. That mirrors how the group has historically used tour launches to anchor comeback eras, and it means the setlist could still change as new material lands.

The rollout also follows the group's established pattern of announcing in waves: the tour was revealed on August 27 with only the Seoul kickoff detailed, the full multi-continent itinerary followed, and venue and ticketing notices have been arriving city by city (Seoul first at the KSPO DOME, then Macau at the Galaxy Arena). BIGHIT's notice for the Seoul shows adds one more wrinkle worth knowing: the concerts are being held both in person and as online livestreams, which the agency has extended to past tours as well.

## Ticket and presale info

Nothing has been announced yet for tickets to the 2027 shows — BIGHIT's official line is that additional information on reservations will follow. Here's how their ticketing has worked for the 2026 legs, which is the best preview of what's coming: for Seoul (November 13–15, KSPO DOME), MOA membership presales ran October 1–2, 2026 with a membership verification window opening September 28, followed by a general reservation on October 6; shows are open to ages 9 and up and include an online livestream option. For Macau (November 21–22, Galaxy Arena), the MOA membership presale ran September 21 with general onsale September 22, via sellers including FANTOPIA, DAMAI and Galaxy Ticketing. Expect the same playbook for 2027: MOA (GLOBAL) membership presale first, then general sale through local promoters per region — KL's March 13, 2027 show is being handled by iMe Malaysia, for example.
