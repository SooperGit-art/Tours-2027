## Why this residency is historic

Vasco Rossi is Italy's foremost rock icon: over 40 albums, 40 million records sold, and a 2017 Modena Park concert that set the world record for a single-artist show with 225,173 paying spectators. The Giubileo di Vasco closes a year-long national celebration of his career that began with his 2026 stadium tour (454,000 spectators across six cities).

The residency format was his own artistic choice, announced in Bari during his 2026 tour: spreading the anniversary across ten nights so hundreds of thousands of fans can attend in comfort and safety rather than crowding into one mega-show. No Italian artist has ever held a ten-night stadium residency before.
