## Quick answer

**Yes** — Billy Ocean is touring in 2027. He announced the 50 Years of Red Light Spells Danger Tour on November 6, 2026: a 10-date UK arena run from October 15 in Leeds to November 5 in Newcastle, with Heather Small as special guest on all dates and Tony Hadley joining on five of them, followed by four dates in the Netherlands.

**November 2026 check:** all 14 dates below are officially announced and listed by Ticketmaster. General sale opens November 13, 2026. Belgium dates were mentioned in the announcement but no venues or dates are confirmed yet.

## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Billy Ocean toured the UK earlier in 2026, and the 2027 run builds on that momentum with a bigger, anniversary-shaped production: arenas instead of theatres, a named celebration of "Red Light Spells Danger" at fifty years, and a guest bill that leans into the era — Heather Small of M People on every date, and Tony Hadley of Spandau Ballet on the five biggest nights. It is a nostalgia package with real drawing power: "Caribbean Queen" and "When the Going Gets Tough, the Tough Get Going" remain radio staples, and the 2026 dates showed the demand is still there.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets go on general sale at 10am on Friday, November 13, 2026 via Ticketmaster UK and Aloud.com. Presales run November 11–12, 2026. The London date at The O2 on November 3 is the likeliest to sell fastest, with Tony Hadley on the bill.
