## Quick answer

**Yes** — Sting has confirmed a 2027 calendar: 37 dates under the STING 3.0 banner, spanning an 11-show Las Vegas residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace in March and October 2027, and a European summer leg of 26 dates from June 2 to July 16, 2027, including a Pinkpop festival set in the Netherlands.

**November 2026 check:** the Las Vegas and European dates are officially announced and largely on sale. No wider US headline tour for 2027 has been announced beyond the Vegas residency, and no further legs have been confirmed yet.

- STING 3.0 European summer leg: 25 dates announced on sting.com on October 19, 2026, running June 2 (Belgrade) to July 16 (Louhans), covering Serbia, Austria, Poland, Germany, Luxembourg, France, Italy and Switzerland.

## The tour so far, for context

STING 3.0 is the third chapter Sting describes in his career, after The Police and four decades as a solo artist — and it is deliberately stripped back. Instead of a full band, he tours as a trio with Miller, his guitarist of more than 30 years, and Maas, a drummer known for work with Mumford & Sons and Maggie Rogers. The format leans on his deep catalogue, from Police staples to solo hits and rarities, rearranged for three players.

The tour resumed in 2025, opening in North America, and has kept extending since — the 2027 European leg announced in October 2026 is the latest stretch, with shows booked into open-air squares, arenas and summer festivals across the continent. The Las Vegas return is equally significant: his previous My Songs residency at the same room ran 22 sold-out shows in 2021–23, and the new 11-show STING 3.0 engagement is his first Vegas run in the trio format.

## Ticket and presale info

The Las Vegas residency went on general sale on October 17, 2026, at 10 a.m. PT via Ticketmaster, after a Sting.com fan-club presale on October 14 and Caesars Rewards and Live Nation presales on October 15. Most European dates went on presale to Sting.com members on October 20, 2026, at 10 a.m. local time; a few shows carried later schedules — Aluna Festival (Ruoms) on October 22, St. Peter at Sunset (Kestenholz) on October 19, and Nuits Bressannes (Louhans) with presale November 14 and general sale November 15. Fans should buy through sting.com, Ticketmaster or the venues' official sellers and be wary of resale listings priced far above face value before general sales open.
