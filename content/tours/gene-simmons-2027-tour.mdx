## The tour so far, for context

Gene Simmons co-founded KISS in New York in 1973, and for five decades the band's fire-breathing, blood-spitting stage show made them one of the biggest live draws in rock. KISS closed the book on touring with their final shows at Madison Square Garden in December 2023, but Simmons never stopped playing: the Gene Simmons Band has toured steadily since, playing KISS classics and solo material in theatres, casinos and festivals across America.

The 2027 dates follow the same pattern — a rolling run of regional shows rather than a single branded tour, mixing casino rooms like Rivers Casino in Pittsburgh and Bally's Twin River in Lincoln with theatres like the Arcada in St. Charles and festival slots like the Fall Music Classic in Utica. Two of the eight dates are shows postponed from 2026, which suggests the 2027 calendar is still filling in around a core of confirmed stops.
