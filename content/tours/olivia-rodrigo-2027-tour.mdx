## Quick answer

**Yes** — Olivia Rodrigo is confirmed touring in 2027. The 2027 portion of **The Unraveled Tour** runs January 12 to May 10, spanning an LA residency (Intuit Dome), a Brooklyn residency (Barclays Center), and a full Europe/UK leg closing at The O2 in London. This is part of a larger 86-date global tour that started September 2026 in Hartford, Connecticut.

<Callout type="confirmed">
- 46 confirmed 2027 shows: LA residency (9 nights), Brooklyn residency (10 nights), then Stockholm → Amsterdam → Munich → London → Paris → Milan → Barcelona → London (closing)
- Tour supports her third studio album, *you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love* (released June 12, 2026)
- General sale sold out within minutes on May 7, 2026
- A limited $20 "Silver Star Tickets" program runs per show for affordable access
- Support acts announced for select dates: The Last Dinner Party, Devon Again, Wolf Alice, Die Spitz, and Grace Ives
- The tour kicked off September 25–26, 2026 in Hartford, CT — both nights sold out
</Callout>

## Full 2027 schedule

The table above covers every confirmed 2027 date. A few things worth knowing:

- **LA and Brooklyn are true residencies** — 9 and 10 non-consecutive nights respectively at the same venue, not separate stops
- **London gets five separate blocks** across the leg (April 5–20, plus a closing pair May 9–10) — 9 total London nights, making it the single most-visited city on the entire tour
- This isn't the full tour — the 2026 portion (September–December) already happened across North America; this page tracks only the 2027 continuation

## Support acts by leg

Support rotates through the tour rather than staying fixed:

- **LA & Brooklyn (Jan–Feb 2027):** Devon Again and The Last Dinner Party
- **Stockholm through early London (Mar 19–Apr 12):** Grace Ives
- **Late London through the close (Apr 14–May 10):** Die Spitz

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Olivia Rodrigo touring in 2027?**
Yes, confirmed — 46 shows across the 2027 portion of The Unraveled Tour, running January 12 (Los Angeles) through May 10 (London), including LA and Brooklyn residencies and a full Europe/UK leg.

**What is The Unraveled Tour?**
Olivia Rodrigo's third headline tour, in support of her third studio album, you seem pretty sad for a girl so in love (released June 12, 2026). It's an 86-date global run spanning September 2026 to May 2027.

**Are tickets still available?**
General sale sold out within minutes on May 7, 2026. Check Ticketmaster's Verified Resale or official secondary markets for remaining availability — avoid unverified resellers.

**Who's opening?**
Support rotates by leg: Devon Again and The Last Dinner Party for the LA/Brooklyn residencies; Grace Ives for Stockholm through the first London block; Die Spitz for the later London dates through the Barcelona/London close.

**How much do tickets cost?**
Reported ranges: Upper Bowl $80–250, Lower Bowl $250–700, Floor around $250, VIP packages $500–600 — prices vary by market and are from general-sale reporting, not a fixed official price list.

**Is there a cheap ticket option?**
Yes — Rodrigo's Silver Star Tickets program reserves a limited number of $20 tickets (or local currency equivalent) per show, released separately from general sale.

**Where does the 2027 portion end?**
May 10, 2027, at The O2 in London — the tour's final show overall.
