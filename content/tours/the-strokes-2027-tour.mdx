## Quick answer

**Yes** — The Strokes have confirmed 2027 dates, though there is no standalone headline tour announced yet. The nine confirmed shows run from Australian headline dates in February — Brisbane, two nights in Melbourne, and Sydney — through the Innings Festival in Tempe on February 27, then Kilby Block Party in Salt Lake City in May and European festivals in June and July, ending at NOS Alive in Portugal on July 8.

**November 2026 check:** nine dates are confirmed via the band's management and festival listings. A July 15 Paris date and August festival appearances are reported but not confirmed through official band channels.

## The tour so far, for context

The Strokes spent 2026 back at the center of the rock conversation. Their Reality Awaits world tour ran from June to October 2026 — opening at Bonnaroo on June 12, crossing North America through September, and closing in Dublin on October 28 — with support rotating between Thundercat, Hamilton Leithauser, Cage The Elephant and others. The run followed the launch of a new album cycle with the single "Going Shopping", their first new music since 2020's "The New Abnormal".

The 2027 dates extend that cycle rather than starting a new one: the Australian leg in February, a US festival stop, and a European festival summer. It is the routing of a band in no rush — headline rooms where they can sell them, festivals where they cannot — and it leaves the door open for a fuller 2027 headline run to be added later.
