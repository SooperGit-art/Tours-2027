## Quick answer

**Yes** — Andrea Bocelli's Romanza – 30th Anniversary World Tour continues into 2027 with 14 confirmed dates. The US leg runs January 29 to February 13, 2027 — nine arena shows from Charlotte to Hollywood, Florida — followed by European dates in Cardiff, Budapest, Prague, Portici and Pollenzo through September.

**November 2026 check:** all 14 dates are confirmed and on sale. The tour, produced by AEG Presents, celebrates 30 years since his 1997 breakthrough album "Romanza".

## The tour so far, for context

"Romanza" was the album that turned Andrea Bocelli from an Italian tenor into a global phenomenon in 1997, and its 30th anniversary tour is built around exactly that catalog — the songs that made him a household name, led by "Con Te Partirò". The world tour began in February 2026 in Columbus, Ohio, and the 2027 dates extend it across the US and Europe, with arena shows in the winter and open-air Italian dates in the summer.

The European dates are characteristically grand: a stadium show in Cardiff with the Welsh National Opera Orchestra and massed choirs, arena dates in Budapest and Prague, and two Italian summer evenings in historic settings — the Reggia di Portici palace near Naples on July 3 and the Langhe wine country at Pollenzo on September 25.

## Ticket and presale info

General sale for the nine US dates began Friday, September 18, 2026, at 10:00 AM local time via Ticketmaster. The European dates are on sale through Ticketmaster and local promoters, with premium ticket and hotel packages available for the Cardiff stadium show. Official premium experiences for the Italian dates are listed on the promoter's pages and Bocelli's official site.
