## Why Warped Tour is back

Warped Tour's original cross-country run wound down after 2018, with founder Kevin Lyman citing the unsustainable logistics of a 30-plus city summer tour. It returned in 2025 after a six-year hiatus as a smaller, city-by-city festival format rather than the old traveling circuit — a pattern the 2027 dates continue. The festival is now run in partnership with Insomniac.

Announcing the DC 2027 date, Lyman said the city has always had a strong music culture.

> D.C. has always had an incredible music culture, and we're thrilled to welcome fans from across the region.
> — Kevin Lyman, Vans Warped Tour founder

## What to expect, based on recent years

While no 2027 lineup exists yet, recent Warped Tour stops (2025–2026) featured 60–130+ bands per city spanning rock, pop-punk, alternative, emo, hip-hop, and ska, alongside skateboarding and action-sports demos. Recent top-bill names have included Jimmy Eat World, Rise Against, Coheed and Cambria, All Time Low, and Sublime — useful context for what kind of scale to expect, not a prediction of who's playing in 2027.
