## Quick answer

**Yes** — Roland Kaiser has two official 2027 runs confirmed. The "Unser Moment – Arena Tour 2027" plays 21 arena dates across Germany, Austria, and Switzerland from April 1 to May 7, 2027, and the "Unser Sommermoment – Open Air 2027" run follows with 12 outdoor shows across Germany from June 25 to August 27, 2027.

**October 2026 check:** both runs are officially announced by promoter Semmel Concerts, and extra shows ("Zusatzshows") were added in Hanover, Oberhausen, and Munich after demand spiked.

## The tour so far, for context

Few artists embody German Schlager like Roland Kaiser. With a career stretching back to the 1970s and signature hits such as "Santa Maria" and "Joana," the 74-year-old remains a touring powerhouse whose arena shows routinely sell out across Germany. His annual "Kaisermania" event in Dresden draws tens of thousands, and his fan base skews intensely loyal — which explains why the 2027 arena announcement immediately generated Zusatzshows (added shows) in Hanover, Oberhausen, and Munich.

The 2027 routing follows his proven two-format playbook: a spring arena run hitting all the major rooms — LANXESS arena Cologne, Olympiahalle Munich, Barclays Arena Hamburg, Uber Arena Berlin, Wiener Stadthalle in Vienna, and Hallenstadion in Zurich — followed by a summer of open airs in castles, domplatz squares, and the legendary Waldbühne in Berlin, where he closes the summer with two nights on August 21–22.

## Ticket and presale info

Ticketing for both runs goes through the official channels listed on roland-kaiser.de and the promoter Semmel Concerts. The arena tour was announced with standard onsales, and the added Zusatzshows indicate strong demand — Munich, Hanover, and Oberhausen each got a second night. For the summer open airs, smaller venues like Eiweiler's Bosenbachstadion and Konstanz's Klein Venedig will be the hardest tickets; book early if those are on your list.
