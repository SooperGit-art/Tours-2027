## The reunion, for context

Pinegrove's last album was *11:11*, released in early 2022 and mixed by Chris Walla, and they toured North America and Europe behind it that year. Then the band went quiet: in April 2023 they announced an indefinite hiatus, explaining they had been "taking some time to regroup" after the 2022 run, and founding drummer Zack Levine decided his time with the group had come to a close. Frontman Evan Stephens Hall said he would play some solo performances, but that the band had "no plans to perform as a band right now."

The Coachella 2027 lineup, unveiled November 10, 2026, changed that. Pinegrove's name is hard to miss on the Friday lineup, placed in the third row for the April 9 and April 16 dates headlined by Dua Lipa. The band later confirmed the return on Instagram, writing that they are "gonna play shows in 2027" — note the plural — while announcing the 11th anniversary reissue of their 2015 compilation *Everything So Far*, with a "Bonus Round" of new old recordings, out November 13.
