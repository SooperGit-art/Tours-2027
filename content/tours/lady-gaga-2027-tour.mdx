Several pages advertise a "Lady Gaga concert in London 2027" with ticket prices and venue guesses — The O2 Arena is named as a likely venue, Wembley Stadium as a possible one — and invite fans to sign up for ticket alerts. Read closely and they all say the same thing: no date is confirmed, the tour route is hoped for rather than booked, and prices are estimated from past tours. There is no London date, no venue booking and no ticket sale.
