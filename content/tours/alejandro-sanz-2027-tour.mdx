## The tour so far, for context

Alejandro Sanz is one of the biggest-selling Spanish-language artists of all time, and the ¿Y Ahora Qué +? Tour is an extension of his 2026 run behind the EP *¿Y Ahora Qué?*, released in 2025. The 2026 US leg ran April 9 to May 17, 2026, and the 2027 dates return to two of the same cities — Brooklyn and Miami — just days after Sanz picked up another Latin Grammy in November 2026.

The announcement pattern fits how Sanz tours: the US leg is short and arena-sized, while the Latin America leg, announced around the same time, is the bigger story for his core audience, with Chile confirmed as his sole stop there on March 6, 2027, and ticket sales opening December 3 via Ticketmaster.
