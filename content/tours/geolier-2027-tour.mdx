## Quick answer

**Yes** — Geolier's 2027 plans are confirmed. The Neapolitan rapper plays six stadium nights at Naples' **Stadio Diego Armando Maradona** on June 9, 10, 11, 13, 14 and 15, 2027, billed as **"Napoli è casa tua"** (Naples is your home). Tickets are on sale through TicketOne. **October 2026 check:** no 2027 dates outside Naples have been announced yet.

## Why a 2027 stadium run makes sense

Geolier — the stage name of Emanuele Palumbo, born in Naples in 2000 — has become the defining voice of Italian rap, performing largely in Neapolitan dialect. His 2019 debut *Emanuele* went platinum within a year, and his 2023 album *Il coraggio dei bambini* cemented him as a mainstream star. In 2026 he released his fourth studio album, *Tutto è possibile*, featuring an international collaboration with 50 Cent, and kept up a heavy live schedule across Italy. A hometown stadium residency is the natural next step: his 2024 show at the Maradona proved he can fill Naples' biggest stage, and six nights in June 2027 would make him one of the first Italian rappers to hold a multi-night stadium run.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets are sold officially through TicketOne Italy. Each customer may buy a maximum of 4 tickets, and TicketOne allows name changes on tickets from one month before the event up to the day of the show. Given the six-night structure, early dates are likely to sell fastest — buy only from TicketOne or its authorized points of sale.
