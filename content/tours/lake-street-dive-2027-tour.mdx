## Quick answer

**Yes** — Lake Street Dive are touring in 2027 on the What About Flamingos? Tour, a 15-date North American run from February 26 in Waukee, Iowa, to March 21 in Peoria, Illinois, with two Canadian stops in Montreal and Toronto. The tour supports their new album *What About Flamingos?*, out February 19, 2027, just a week before the first show.

**October 2026 check:** all 15 dates are confirmed and announced. Artist presale opens October 28, 2026, with general sale October 30.

## The tour so far, for context

Lake Street Dive have built one of the most durable live followings in American indie-soul, and the 2027 routing plays to it: a Midwest, South, and East Coast sweep through mid-size rooms — The Anthem in Washington, Brooklyn Paramount, The Met Philadelphia — rather than a jump straight to arenas. The tour is timed to the new album's release week, with the first show landing seven days after *What About Flamingos?* drops, so early audiences will hear the new material essentially fresh.

The band is releasing the record on its own Dive Not Drive Records imprint, continuing the independent streak that has defined its recent career. The choice of Snacktime as support (everywhere except Greenville) keeps the bill in the same brass-friendly, groove-first world the band occupies. For a group whose reputation rests on live performance, the 2027 tour is the album cycle's main event, not an afterthought.
