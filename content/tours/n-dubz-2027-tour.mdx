## Quick answer

**Yes** — N-Dubz announced The Legacy Tour on October 2, 2026: an eight-date UK arena run from February 25 to March 10, 2027, celebrating twenty years of the Camden trio. For the first time, Tulisa, Dappy and Fazer will perform in the round on a 360-degree stage. The run opens at Glasgow's OVO Hydro and closes at Birmingham's BP Pulse Live, with a London date at The O2 on March 4.

## The tour so far, for context

Formed in Camden Town in 2000, N-Dubz became one of the defining British acts of the late 2000s, fusing hip-hop, grime, R&B and pop with a distinctly London sound. Debut album *Uncle B* (2008) went double platinum on the back of "Papa Can You Hear Me?", "I Swear" and "You Better Not Waste My Time"; *Against All Odds* (2009) hit number six and delivered "I Need You" and the MOBO-winning "Playing with Fire"; their Tinchy Stryder collaboration "Number 1" topped the UK Singles Chart and went double platinum. Official Charts records 13 N-Dubz Top 40 singles.

The trio split in 2011 and reunited in 2022 after an eleven-year hiatus, selling out their Back to the Future Tour — including four O2 Arena nights — before releasing *Timeless* in August 2023, their first album in thirteen years and a UK Top 10 hit that topped the UK Hip Hop and R&B Albums Chart. The Legacy Tour pulls that whole arc together: early underground favourites, chart-topping singles and comeback material, all inside a 360-degree arena for the first time in their career.
