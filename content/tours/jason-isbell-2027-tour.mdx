## Quick answer

**Yes** — Jason Isbell and the 400 Unit are touring in 2027 with Drive-By Truckers on the co-headlining Summer of Joy Tour: 39 dates across North America, announced November 17, 2026. The run opens May 6–7 at The Pinnacle in Nashville and closes September 7 at Cain's Ballroom in Tulsa, hitting Red Rocks, Forest Hills Stadium and the Greek Theatre along the way.

**November 2026 check:** the full 39-date routing is confirmed and tickets go on general sale November 20, 2026. The only date still awaiting a venue is September 3 in Portland, Oregon, listed as TBC.

## The tour so far, for context

This is a reunion with real history behind it. Jason Isbell joined Drive-By Truckers in 2001 and spent six years in the band, writing songs like "Decoration Day" and "Outfit" before leaving in 2007 to go solo. Since then he has built one of the most decorated careers in Americana with the 400 Unit — six Grammy Awards, including Best Americana Album for *Weathervanes* — while the Truckers kept touring as one of America's great road bands. The two acts sharing a stage every night is the closest thing to a homecoming the genre will get in 2027.

The announcement landed November 17, 2026, a day before presales opened, and it explains why "Jason Isbell tour 2027" and "Drive-By Truckers tour 2027" both broke out on Google Trends the same morning. Isbell also has a separate one-off on the books before the joint run: April 24, 2027, at Firefly Distillery in North Charleston, South Carolina, with Nathaniel Rateliff. That show is not part of the Summer of Joy routing.

## Ticket and presale info

Presale sign-up opened with the announcement, with the artist presale running Wednesday, November 18, 2026, at 10 a.m. local time through Thursday, November 19, at 10 p.m. local time. General sale opens Friday, November 20, 2026, at 10 a.m. local time through jasonisbell.com and Ticketmaster. VIP packages are available for select dates. A dollar from every ticket sold supports Nuçi's Space.
