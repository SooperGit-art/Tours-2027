## The tour so far, for context

Westlife are marking 25 years since their debut, and the anniversary tour is the biggest global run of the group's career, with more than 90 shows already announced worldwide. The North American leg, announced November 16, 2026, is a milestone in itself: despite decades of hits — "Swear It Again", "Flying Without Wings", "You Raise Me Up", "World of Our Own" — the Irish group had never played Madison Square Garden, and the March 19 finale corrects that on St. Patrick's Day week, closing the leg at Allstate Arena near Chicago on March 17 before the Garden.

The routing covers both coasts and Canada: Inglewood and San Francisco to open, then Edmonton, Calgary and Vancouver, an East Coast swing through Uncasville and Boston, Orlando and Irving in the south, and Toronto and Rosemont before New York. Promoted by Live Nation, the run pairs Westlife with O-Town, the American pop group, as special guests at every stop — a fitting bill for a 25th anniversary celebration built on turn-of-the-millennium pop.

Beyond North America, the world tour keeps moving all year: Singapore's Indoor Stadium on January 18 and Bangkok's IMPACT Arena on January 25, European dates including Helsinki on June 14 and Gothenburg on June 16, and an Australia and New Zealand leg in July with shows in Perth, Adelaide, Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Auckland and Christchurch. More dates are promised, with the tour already described as the biggest of the group's career.

## Ticket and presale info

Presales begin Tuesday, November 17, 2026, for Citi cardmembers in the United States and American Express cardmembers in Canada, and general sale opens Friday, November 20, 2026, at 10am local time via westlife.com. The Madison Square Garden finale and the March 1 Inglewood opener are the likeliest to sell out first, so presale registration is worth doing before the general on-sale.
