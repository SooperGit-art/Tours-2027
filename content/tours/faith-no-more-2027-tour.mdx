## Quick answer

**Yes** — Faith No More are officially back on stage in 2027. Nine dates are announced so far: five stadium shows co-headlining with System of a Down across Brazil, Australia, and New Zealand in January and February, plus four European festival appearances in June. These are the band's first live performances since 2016. **October 2026 check:** no full standalone world tour or new album has been announced — the confirmed dates are the co-headline run and the festivals below.

## The comeback so far, for context

Faith No More have been one of rock's great on-again, off-again acts. The San Francisco band — Mike Patton, Bill Gould, Mike Bordin, Roddy Bottum, and Jon Hudson — split in 1998, reunited in 2009 for a global comeback tour, and released Sol Invictus in 2015, their first album in 18 years. They have played no shows at all since 2016: a planned 2020 return was first derailed by the pandemic, then canceled outright when Patton scrapped his 2021 appearances to address his mental health.

The 2027 return began with a single social media post: the band's eight-pointed star logo with the text "2027." Bill Gould confirmed it one day later on the Rock Talk podcast. "We're gonna do it. We're gonna play," he said, adding that the band's physical style of playing means they decided to go for it while they still can do it justice — "for a few more years." The announced 2027 schedule was first reported by JamBase in July 2026 and has since grown with the European festival announcements in late September.

This is one of the most anticipated rock comebacks of the year. Faith No More last played Australia in 2015 and the German Ring/Park festivals in 1995, so these dates are genuine rarities — and the band has shown no sign of announcing anything further yet.

## Ticket and presale info

Tickets for the Australia and New Zealand stadium shows went on public sale July 31, 2026, after an artist presale for mailing-list subscribers on July 28 and a PayPal First Access presale on July 29, with TEG Live handling ticketing. No official ticket links are published for the Rio de Janeiro date — watch the band's official channels. The four European dates are festival appearances, so entry is through each festival's own ticketing: Rock for People, Rock im Park, Rock am Ring, and Mystic Festival. Mystic Festival 2027 three-day passes were listed at PLN 879, with VIP passes at PLN 1,399.
