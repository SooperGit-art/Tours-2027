Chase Atlantic are an Australian trio from Cairns, Queensland — brothers Mitchel and Clinton Cave with Christian Anthony — formed in 2014 and now one of alternative R&B's most streamed acts. Their sound blends trap, synth-pop and pop rock, and they have released four albums, most recently LOST IN HEAVEN in 2024. They toured heavily behind that record, including South American dates, but the road has gone quiet since late 2025, which is part of why a search spike like this one catches fire: fans are waiting.

They are not the same act, and none of those dates belong to Chase Atlantic.
