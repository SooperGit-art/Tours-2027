## Quick answer

**Yes** — Nick Cave is touring North America in 2027. He announced the return of Conversations With Nick Cave: An Evening of Talk and Music on November 6, 2026: a 25-date run from April 9 in Vancouver to May 21 in New Orleans, with two-night stands in Seattle, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Toronto, Boston and Brooklyn. It is Cave alone with his piano, answering unvetted audience questions and playing songs from his catalog — not a Bad Seeds show.

**November 2026 check:** all 25 dates below are officially announced. Presale runs November 11–12, 2026, with general sale on November 13. No UK or European leg has been announced.

## The tour so far, for context

Cave first took the Conversations format on the road in 2019, and he has described those nights as unexpectedly healing — he travelled across the US, stepped onto stages in different cities, and fielded whatever the audience asked. Eight years on, the format returns with a bigger engine behind it: The Red Hand Files, the Q&A project Cave launched in 2018, now has over 250,000 subscribers and hundreds of questions submitted each week. Announcing the 2027 run, Cave wrote that it felt like the right time to return to America — "a different America from eight years ago" — with his piano, to play some songs and answer questions.

One warning for searchers: a ticket-broker site, nickcavetour.com, currently lists stale Nick Cave dates from the 2022 Carnage era (a March 2022 North American run with Warren Ellis). Those dates are years out of date and have nothing to do with the 2027 Conversations tour — use nickcave.com and nickcaveconversations.com for real information. Cave's most recent album work is Wild God, released with the Bad Seeds in 2024.

## Ticket and presale info

Register for presale access at nickcaveconversations.com. The artist presale runs Wednesday, November 11 at 10am local time through Thursday, November 12 at 10pm local time. General sale begins Friday, November 13, 2026 at 10am local time, except in Montreal, where it starts at 11am. Tickets are sold through Ticketmaster and venue box offices. There are no support acts on this tour.
