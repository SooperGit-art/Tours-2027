## The tour so far, for context

Ronan Keating first found fame as the lead singer of Boyzone, one of the defining boy bands of the 1990s, before building a long solo career with hits of his own. The tour name is a statement of intent rather than a farewell: Keating has framed it as a new chapter, bringing Boyzone favourites and solo hits together for audiences who have followed him for three decades. That context matters because the show is not built around a single new album — it is a career-celebration set.

The routing tells the same story. Australia and New Zealand open the tour in March, the UK summer dates follow in June, July, and August — many of them outdoor and forest venues with Blue on the bill — and a European arena leg runs through September. Boyzone themselves reunited for two landmark shows at London's Emirates Stadium in June 2026, their first performances together since 2019, which set the stage for Keating's solo anniversary run.
