## Why this tour is a big one for Simply Red

Mick Hucknall's band have been a summer staple in Europe for decades, and Summer Funk '27 is one of their biggest continental runs in years: 14 German open-air shows alone, festival stops from Tollwood in Munich to the Amphitheatre of Pompeii, and a UK August run closing with racecourse and seaside venues that suit their crowd. The tour leans on the classic songbook — Stars, Holding Back the Years, Fairground — that has kept them arena-fillers across five decades.

The run also shows how fragmented modern European touring has become: city squares, castle grounds, festival fields and racecourses, with most on-sales handled country by country rather than through one promoter.

## Ticket and presale info

Dates are on sale through Ticketmaster and national promoters (eventim in Germany, local partners in Italy and the UK). Prices vary by market and venue type — open-air German shows typically start lower than the arena nights in Amsterdam and Lisbon. Buy through official channels per country; avoid resale listings for the unconfirmed Carinthia and Rome dates.
