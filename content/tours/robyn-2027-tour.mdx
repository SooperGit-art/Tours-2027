## Why a 2027 tour makes sense

Robyn's comeback is one of the biggest pop stories of 2026. *Sexistential*, her ninth studio album and first since 2018's *Honey*, arrived on March 27, 2026 to critical acclaim, and the Sexistential Tour that followed — announced February 6, 2026 — was her first full arena tour, running 24 shows from Dublin's 3Arena on June 24 to Rod Laver Arena in Melbourne on November 24, with guests including Romy, Erika de Casier, Smerz and 808 State.

A festival-heavy 2027 is the natural next step: Coachella, Primavera and Open'er are exactly the stages where a returning Robyn reaches both her longtime fans and a new generation who met her through *Brat*-era collaborations and the *Sexistential* singles. Whether those three bookings grow into a headline run depends on what she announces next — the festival dates keep her on the road through early July, leaving the autumn open.
